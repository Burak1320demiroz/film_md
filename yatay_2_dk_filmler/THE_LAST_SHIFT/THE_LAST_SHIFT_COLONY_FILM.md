## 0.2 NOVUM — tek cümle, ilk sahnede kurulur

> **Bu vadi hasat ediliyor: zemin, kusursuz 60 × 60 metrelik parseller
> hâlinde, dört metre derinliğinde KALDIRILIYOR. Toprak, su ve üstündeki
> her şey gidiyor; geriye ana kayanın düz kesiği ve amber bir toz kalıyor.
> Sıra hiç bozulmuyor: her doksan saniyede bir parsel.**

Novum bir manzara değil, dünyanın **kuralı**dır ve saati kurar:

```
Kural gösterilir  → S0: uzak bir parselin kenarında amber toz kalkar;
                    toz oturduğunda o parsel dört metre alçalmıştır.
                    Kimse yok, makine yok, açıklama yok.
Kural sayılır     → S3: yerleşim ile hasat sınırı arasında ALTI parsel
                    var. Altı parsel = dokuz dakika.
Kural adlandırılır→ S7: çevirmen kutusu dört gün önceki oturumu tekrar
                    oynatır. Kelime "savaş" değil: "hasat".
Kural bedel ister → S10: yerleşimin direği elle tutulan bir mandal
                    istiyor ve mandal ÇIPLAK TEMAS istiyor. Kırk saniye.
                    Kırk saniye, bir parselden daha uzun değil — ama
                    mandalı tutan kişi son parselin üstündedir.
```

**Bilim çapası:** Hasat bir felaket değil, bir İŞLEMDİR. Kesikler
düzgün, sıra düzenli, hız sabit. Filmin dehşeti buradan gelir: kimse
saldırmıyor, bir yer **işleniyor**. Kaynak filmin cümlesi birebir
geçerli: *"bu bütün bir hayvan DEĞİL"* — biz de bütünü hiç göstermiyoruz.

## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — VADİ: TESSERA HAVZASI

```
CORE CONCEPT: A broad shallow valley on a habitable world at 0.9 g
(8.8 m/s²) under a breathable but spore-loaded atmosphere at 0.8
Earth pressure. The sky is a flat, featureless grey-green overcast —
the sun is never visible and nothing casts a hard shadow. The valley
floor is dry grey-green ground: packed silt, low dead scrub stubble,
no standing water, no living plant, no animal.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE — THE VALLEY IS BEING HARVESTED: the ground
itself is being removed in perfect 60-by-60-meter plots, each cut
exactly 4 meters deep, leaving flat bedrock and a razor-clean
vertical face. The plots are taken one at a time in strict order,
one every 90 seconds. When a plot goes, amber dust rises no higher
than 20 meters and settles within 6 seconds; between plots nothing
moves at all. The edge never crumbles, never erodes, never flows —
it steps, plot by plot, like a field being mown.

THE CAUSE IS NEVER SHOWN: there is no machine, no vehicle, no
excavation site, no opening in the sky, no explosion. What does the
work is seen exactly once in this film and only as a single limb
segment at 300 meters through dust. No body, no head, no eyes.

THE COLONY: a small settlement of 9 low prefabricated buildings on
the intact side of the harvest line, the tallest 7 meters. It is
completely undamaged and completely unlit — no window light, no door
light, no mast light, no street light. Its doors are heavy sliding
plates and every one of them is SEALED FROM THE OUTSIDE: manual bars
dropped into their brackets, in neat sequence, by hand. Nothing here
burned, nothing was forced, nothing was looted.

THE BASIN: a 40-meter open water basin beside the settlement, bone
dry, with a fine white salt line 3 meters up its inner wall marking
where the water stood. The intake pipe at its floor is CUT — a
clean, flat, machine-quality cut, not a break.

THE DORMITORY: two rows of twelve bunks, every bunk made, blankets
squared. Under each bunk stands one pair of heavy work boots. Sixty
one pairs in total across the room. There are no people, no bodies,
no bones and no remains anywhere in this film.

THE MARKS: on the outer wall of the tallest building, four parallel
gouges, each 3 centimeters deep and 80 centimeters long, torn
through the metal. Beside them, from the INSIDE, a scatter of bullet
holes punched outward. Two kinds of violence, one wall, both old.

THE MAST: a 14-meter open lattice mast at the settlement edge with a

THE TRANSLATOR: a 24-centimeter matte grey box with a single small

still runs on its last cell and replays, in flat broken machine
speech, the session it recorded four days ago.

SOUND: still and dead. Footsteps are dull. There is no wind, no
bird, no insect, no machine hum. The only event sound is a plot
being taken: one very low, very long descending pull — not an
impact — followed by four seconds of grain hiss, then nothing.
```
