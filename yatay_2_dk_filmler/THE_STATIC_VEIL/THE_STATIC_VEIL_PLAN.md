## 📖 BIBLE'LAR

### 👤 Character Bible

White heavy pressurized extravehicular activity spacesuit, rigid composite chest plate with dark grey tactical harness straps, dark grey flexible carbon fiber accordion joint seals at shoulders elbows and wrists. Gold-tinted reflective spherical visor helmet completely concealing the human face under high specular gold mirror reflection. Dark grey chest control pack with a small rectangular external lamp battery clipped to the right shoulder harness. Wrist-mounted mechanical pressure gauge on left cuff. The suit back is a flat unbroken composite panel carrying no back-mounted pack or rear unit. Dust accumulation visible as fine grey powder coating on suit surfaces increasing scene by scene. The golden reflective visor progressively turns matte grey with abrasive dust coating from S1 onward.

### 🌍 World Bible

Interior shadow zone of a wide shallow impact crater on an airless rocky body. Crater floor is compacted grey regolith with scattered angular basalt fragments. A metal ground guide rail runs along the crater slope leading to a cylindrical optical beacon housing mounted on a low steel pylon. The crater rim casts a razor-sharp shadow line across the slope separating absolute black shadow from harsh unfiltered sunlight. In the shadow zone near the light boundary fine regolith dust particles levitate as thin luminous threads rising vertically one to two meters above the surface with no wind and no atmosphere. Deep black sky with no atmospheric glow. Distant sun is a single intense point source above the crater rim producing specular highlights on every metallic surface.

### 🎥 Camera Bible

Horizontal 16:9 widescreen format. Spherical lenses only — no anamorphic horizontal flare (§3.1 vakum kuralı: atmosferik saçılma yok). Three focal lengths: 24mm wide establishing shots, 50mm medium character shots, 85mm close-up detail work. Steady lockoff tripod for all shots — no handheld, no tracking, no pan. High contrast exposure: shadow areas near black, lit surfaces harsh specular. Focus rack permitted only within a single depth plane.

### 💡 Lighting Bible

Single harsh point-source sun above crater rim. No atmospheric scattering, no ambient fill light, no bounce light in shadow (§3.1 vakum). Shadow edges are razor-sharp geometric lines with zero feathering. In shadow zone: objects are lit ONLY by reflected light from nearby sunlit surfaces — faint grey indirect illumination. Astronaut gold visor catches specular sun reflection when facing crater rim. Kıvılcım (S7 and S9): brief intense white-blue electrical discharge, duration under 0.3 seconds, no sustained glow or fire. Beacon light (S11): warm amber directional beam cutting through crater darkness.

### ⚙️ Physics Bible

Strict airless vacuum environment on rocky body surface. Gravity approximately 0.16g (lunar class) — astronaut steps are longer and slower with slight upward float at apex. No atmosphere: no wind, no airborne sound propagation, no sky glow, no atmospheric haze, no dust settling (toz yükselen partiküllerdir — yere İNMEZ, §3.1). Electrostatic dust levitation: UV photoelectric charging creates potential difference at shadow-light boundary; fine regolith grains (10-100 micron) rise vertically 1-2 meters and adhere electrostatically to any surface they contact. Abrasive regolith: angular microshards with no weathering scratch polycarbonate visor on contact or wiping. Electrical discharge in vacuum: brief luminous arc, no sustained flame, no smoke — ionized particles disperse instantly.
