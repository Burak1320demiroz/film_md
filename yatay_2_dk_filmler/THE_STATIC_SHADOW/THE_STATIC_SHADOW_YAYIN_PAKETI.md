### DESCRIPTION

```
A 500-meter carbon-ceramic orbital sunshield locks its angular gyro, drifting at ~0.008 rad/s. As the shadow slips off the 80-meter cryogenic space observatory behind it, intense stellar radiation threatens permanent sensor destruction.

With manipulator arms qualified below the extreme sunward radiative flux, the service pod pilot takes a final, irreversible decision: driving the pod's titanium nose directly into the shield's sacrificial titanium-tantalum capture rail. Through extreme friction, galling, and vacuum cold-adhesion, the pod fuses to the shield edge—transforming into a 15-meter moment arm.

By expending all remaining delta-v through attitude thrusters, the pilot cancels the shield's angular momentum, locking the shadow back over the telescope array at 0.000 error.
```
