### 🎥 Camera Bible

Camera never rolls; deck stays down and ceiling stays up throughout all scenes. Micro-gravity is conveyed exclusively through human tether physics, boot locking/releasing, and creature surface-gripping mechanics. Lens focal length stays between 28mm (wide spatial coverage) and 65mm (close equipment latches and face reactions). Shot cuts match action across 17 total cuts.

### 💡 Lighting Bible

Neutral white task illumination from ceiling fixtures keying human faces through clear visors. Deep red emergency warning lights provide background danger cues without washing human faces into monochromatic red. Overhead white lighting separates dark creature carapaces from background dark surfaces.

### ⚙️ Physics Bible

- Micro-gravity environment: Bodies drift along vector after push off; motion changes only via boot anchor, handrail grip, or tether force.
- Magnetic boots anchor exclusively to dark ferromagnetic deck strips.
- Creatures grip surfaces with four two-clawed limbs; zero free-floating mid-air course corrections without surface contact.
- Tether snag: Noah's line loops around the forked lower arm of the service bracket, wedging in its narrow neck under tension.
- Door seal: Shield and combined shoulder force compress creature claw against frame, prompting release before mechanical door closure.

### 🚫 Negative Bible — Bu Filme Özel Yasaklar (Master Plan §12)

1. Show only the named human and creature identities specified for this shot. Do not add background crew, duplicate faces or extra creatures. The complete film contains five humans and ten creatures; do not force the full cast into every frame.
2. Each creature has exactly four anatomical limbs and two short hooked claws at each limb tip. Natural occlusion is allowed. No additional limbs, tails, wings, human faces or glowing eyes.
3. Any visible human wears the locked costume and clear closed pressure visor. Preserve the assigned helmet geometry, markings, equipment, laterality and face identity. Clear visors must reveal human faces (D79 vizör şeffaflığı kilidi).
4. Equipment, clothing and damage stay consistent with the shot's continuity state. Only the explicitly described damage or deliberate transfer may change them. No spontaneous shield, weapon, tether, holster or tool duplication. The back of the suit carries a slim life-support panel integrated along the back collar to hip, carrying no extra backpacks or unmentioned tanks (D79 sırt ünitesi kilidi).
5. Microgravity motion follows visible pushes, contacts, restraints and vehicle acceleration. No Earth-gravity falling, unsupported direction changes or unexplained instant stops.
6. Use motivated practical lighting and readable silhouettes. No excessive bloom, unmotivated glow, continuous strobe or opaque visor reflections hiding the intended facial performance.
7. Keep human hands, gloves, claws, cables, armor and surrounding surfaces visibly separate. No merging, phasing, elastic anatomy or a cable passing through a solid body.
8. No readable text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks inside the generated scene. Only the approved geometric costume markings are allowed. Titles and optional subtitles are added separately in editing.
