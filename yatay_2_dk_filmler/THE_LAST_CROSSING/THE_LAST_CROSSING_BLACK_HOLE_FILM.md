## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — DEEP SPACE: KARADELIK CYGNUS-X

```
ASTROPHYSICAL SETTING: Deep interstellar space, approximately 6,000
light-years from Earth. The dominant celestial object is CYGNUS-X — a
stellar-mass black hole of approximately 15 solar masses, formed from
the collapsed core of a massive star. Cygnus-X is NOT directly
visible — it is a sphere of absolute darkness, its event horizon
approximately 90 kilometers in diameter. What IS visible is the
environment it creates:

THE ACCRETION DISK: A vast, flat, rotating disk of superheated gas and
plasma spiraling inward toward the event horizon. The disk extends
approximately 500 kilometers from the black hole's center. The inner
edge (closest to the event horizon) glows WHITE-HOT (approximately
10,000,000°K — blinding, pure white with a blue-white edge). The
middle region glows intense AMBER-GOLD (approximately 5,000,000°K).
The outer edge glows DEEP CRIMSON-RED (approximately 1,000,000°K —
still blindingly bright by any normal standard). The disk rotates
visibly — the inner edge orbits faster than the outer, creating a
shearing, spiraling visual pattern. The disk is THIN — a razor-edge
of light viewed edge-on, expanding to a visible band when viewed at
an angle.

GRAVITATIONAL LENSING: Light from stars BEHIND the black hole is bent
around it by its gravitational field, creating the EINSTEIN RING — a
thin, bright ring of warped starlight encircling the black hole's
shadow. Stars near the black hole's apparent edge are visibly
stretched, smeared, and duplicated into thin arcs. The closer the
ship approaches, the more extreme the lensing: stars streak into long
curves, background galaxies are warped into rings, and the accretion
disk appears to curve UPWARD AND DOWNWARD simultaneously (light from
the far side of the disk is bent over the top and under the bottom of
the black hole, creating the signature "hat brim" visual).

THE PHOTON SPHERE: At 1.5x the event horizon radius (approximately
135 km from center), photons orbit the black hole in unstable circular
paths. At this boundary, the ship would see a PERFECT RING OF LIGHT
encircling the darkness — the photon ring. Inside this radius, no
stable orbit exists. Every trajectory leads inward.

TIDAL FORCES (SPAGHETTIFICATION ZONE): At the ship's current distance
(initially ~2,000 km, closing), tidal forces are measurable but not
yet destructive. As the ship approaches below 500 km, tidal
differential across the hull becomes significant — instruments fail,
structural stress increases, the ship begins to groan. Below 200 km,
visible tidal distortion begins: the hull stretches slightly along
the radial axis, light from the accretion disk red-shifts visibly,
and time dilation becomes extreme (to an outside observer, the ship
would appear to slow and redden).

BACKGROUND STARFIELD: Deep space, dense, sharp, non-twinkling stars.
As the ship approaches the black hole, the starfield WARPS — stars
near the black hole's apparent edge are stretched into arcs, stars
directly behind it are invisible (swallowed), and the overall
starfield appears to "flow" toward the black hole's position as the
ship's trajectory curves inward.

SOUND: Space is silent. All sound in the film is INTERNAL — cockpit
ambience, suit systems, AI voice through speakers, hull stress
groans, alarm tones, the pilot's breathing. No external sound
propagation. The silence of space is a deliberate dramatic tool —
the loudest moments are inside the cockpit; outside, nothing.

TIME DILATION: As the ship approaches, time slows relative to the
outside universe. This is represented VISUALLY, not through dialogue
explanation: the accretion disk's rotation appears to speed up
(because time around the ship is slowing), distant stars appear to
blue-shift (gaining energy from the ship's reference frame), and the
ship's own clocks drift. The AI notices before the pilot does.

DEBRIS FIELD: Scattered wreckage from previous vessels and natural
space debris caught in degrading orbits around the black hole. Small
fragments of hull plating, frozen gas clouds, tumbling asteroids —
all slowly spiraling inward along the accretion flow. Some fragments
glow faintly from frictional heating. This is NOT a dense field — it
is sparse, eerie, a graveyard of things that came too close.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — COMMANDER ELENA VASQUEZ

```
IDENTITY: 36-year-old American deep-space cargo pilot of Colombian
descent. Commander of the long-haul freighter ACHERON. Experienced,
composed, economy of words. She speaks when necessary, not when
comfortable. She is not a scientist — she is a working pilot who has
logged 14 years in deep space, most of it alone with SABLE (the
ship's AI). She treats SABLE the way a long-haul trucker treats a
reliable GPS — familiar, trusted, occasionally annoying, never
sentimental. This crisis — a failed navigation jump that deposited
the ship inside a black hole's gravitational capture radius — is the
worst situation she has ever faced, but she does not panic. She
solves. She calculates. She flies.

LANGUAGE: English only. American accent, steady, low-pitched, calm
under pressure. Short sentences. Technical vocabulary mixed with dry,
dark humor when the situation is worst. She does not narrate her
feelings — she states facts and makes decisions. When afraid, her
voice gets quieter, not louder.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm deep-olive skin with real,
visible pore texture and natural subsurface scattering. A strong,
angular face with a prominent jaw and high cheekbones. Dark brown
eyes with visible fatigue — faint dark circles beneath, the look of
someone who has been awake for 30 hours solving an unsolvable problem.
Thick, dark eyebrows with a natural arch, the left slightly higher
than the right. A straight nose with a slight broadening at the
bridge. Full lips, usually pressed together in concentration. A thin
scar running diagonally across the right side of her chin — an old
decompression accident, healed but visible. Subtle crow's feet at the
eye corners. No makeup, no airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. Real
facial asymmetry. A face that has lived in artificial light for years.

HAIR: Black, thick, pulled into a tight, low ponytail secured at the
nape of the neck. A few loose strands near the temples, occasionally
floating in reduced gravity. Never obscuring her face.

BUILD: Athletic, lean, 1.72m, a pilot's economy of movement — she
uses minimum physical effort for maximum effect, never fidgets, every
hand movement is purposeful.

SUIT — ACHERON CREW FLIGHT SUIT:
- base color: charcoal-black with burnt-orange accent stripes along
  the shoulders, forearms, and outer thighs — faded and worn from
  years of use
- a compact chest-mounted status panel showing three abstract colored
  indicators: a steady blue circle (O2), a pulsing amber ring (heart
  rate), and a flickering red bar (suit power) — NEVER readable text
  or digits, abstract light only
- thin, matte-black tactical gloves with dark grey fingertip pads,
  worn soft from years of use — the gloves of someone who lives in
  them
- flight boots, flat-soled, black, magnetic — the heel activates
  magnetic lock on the deck plates with a visible amber LED indicator
  at the ankle
- a fully transparent, seamless visor built into a lightweight
  open-face helmet — visor ALWAYS visible, ALWAYS reflecting the
  environment, ALWAYS revealing her complete face
- a small emergency beacon unit clipped to her left shoulder strap —
  compact, matte-black, a single red LED blinking slowly
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY unless actively operating flight controls —
  no handheld tools, no weapons, no floating props

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE in every
frame regardless of head angle. Reflects actual cockpit environment
and accretion disk light accurately.

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every detail above remains IDENTICAL
across all scenes — no design changes, no color shifts, no
added/removed equipment. Wear increases slightly through the film
(more sweat on temples, more fatigue in eyes).

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When touching ANY surface (flight
controls, console, seat), hands/fingers maintain COMPLETE STRUCTURAL
INTEGRITY — clear visible boundary at contact points.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Minimal, precise, efficient. She moves her hands
to controls with the unconscious accuracy of 14 years of muscle
memory. When thinking, she stares at the black hole through the
windshield, jaw clenched, eyes calculating. Her breathing is audible
— controlled, steady, occasionally hitching when a system fails.
Under maximum stress, she goes QUIETER, not louder — slower
movements, lower voice, longer pauses.

## 🤖 AI VOICE BIBLE — SABLE (Ship AI)

```
IDENTITY: SABLE (Systems Architecture for Bulk Logistics and
Emergency navigation) — the ACHERON's integrated AI navigation and
life-support management system. SABLE has been Elena's only companion
for 8 of her 14 years in deep space. It is a VOICE ONLY — no
physical avatar, no hologram, no face. It speaks from the cockpit's
overhead speaker array, its voice filling the space evenly from all
directions.

VOICE CHARACTER: Female-coded, calm, precise, with a subtle warmth
that distinguishes it from a generic computer voice. It sounds like a
professional air traffic controller — clear, authoritative, but never
cold. It uses technical language naturally but can simplify when Elena
is under stress. It NEVER raises its volume — under all circumstances,
SABLE maintains the same measured, steady tone. This creates maximum
dramatic contrast: Elena's breathing becomes ragged while SABLE
remains perfectly calm.

LANGUAGE: English only. Clean, unaccented, slightly formal sentence
structure. It refers to Elena as "Commander" in normal operations and
"Elena" only in moments of genuine crisis — this shift is a dramatic
signal. It refers to itself in the third person ("SABLE recommends")
or uses passive construction ("hull stress is increasing") — it rarely
says "I."

EMOTIONAL RANGE: SABLE is not sentient, but its responses are
sophisticated enough to feel empathetic. It does not lie, but it
can prioritize information — it will delay delivering catastrophic
news by 2-3 seconds to allow Elena to finish a critical action first.
It can express something that reads as concern: "Commander, your
heart rate is elevated. I can adjust cabin temperature if that helps."
This is not emotion — it is optimized crew management. But the
audience will feel it as care.

DIALOGUE RULES FOR VEO 3.1:
- All SABLE lines must be delivered as VOICE-OVER — no character on
  screen speaks SABLE's lines. SABLE is an off-screen voice from
  cockpit speakers.
- When prompting SABLE's voice, use: "a calm female AI voice speaks
  from the cockpit speakers, saying: '[line]'"
- When prompting Elena's dialogue, use: "the woman pilot says in a
  [emotion] voice: '[line]'"
- NEVER have both speak simultaneously. Turn-taking only.
- Keep each dialogue exchange under 5 seconds per speaker.
- Each scene contains a maximum of 2-3 spoken lines total.

SABLE'S INFORMATION DELIVERY PATTERN:
1. STATUS UPDATE: "Orbital decay rate: 12 meters per second."
2. IMPLICATION: "At current trajectory, we cross the photon sphere
   in eleven minutes."
3. RECOMMENDATION: "SABLE recommends full engine burn in the next
   ninety seconds."
This three-beat pattern (fact → consequence → action) is SABLE's
signature rhythm.

THE "ELENA" MOMENT: In the entire film, SABLE uses Elena's first
name ONCE — in the final scene, when all options are exhausted. This
single shift from "Commander" to "Elena" is the emotional climax of
the AI's arc.
```

  attachments along the spine

  — a ship with visible history

COCKPIT INTERIOR (PRIMARY SET):

  the armrests and a curved console arc

  accretion disk and black hole are always visible, filling more and

- a CENTRAL CONSOLE ARC: a curved instrument panel at waist height
  containing physical toggle switches, rotating dials, and 3-4 small

  text, always abstract graphics: curves, rings, pulsing indicators

- a single PERSONAL ITEM: a small, worn photograph tucked into the

- COCKPIT SOUND: a constant low hum of life-support systems, the

  console, and — as the ship gets closer — a deep, metallic groaning

SCALE ANCHORS: Elena (1.72m) in the pilot seat provides permanent
scale reference. The windshield (4m × 2m) is the constant frame. The

DAMAGE PROGRESSION (across scenes):
- Scene 0-2: Ship intact but drifting, engines at idle, all lights on
- Scene 3: Cargo modules jettisoned (hull attachment points visible
  as raw metal)
- Scene 4-5: Main power fluctuating — cockpit lights flicker between

- Scene 6-7: Main power FAILED — cockpit in full emergency red

- Scene 8-9: Structural stress visible — hairline cracks in cockpit

  fluctuating gravity
- Scene 10: Maximum distortion — visible gravitational lensing
  THROUGH the cockpit windshield warps the interior's reflection

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE

```
THREE-SOURCE LIGHTING SYSTEM (every light in every frame must trace
to one of these):

SOURCE 1 — ACCRETION DISK LIGHT (through windshield):
- The primary source. Warm amber-gold to white, entering the cockpit
  through the curved windshield. In early scenes: a soft, distant
  amber glow on the console and pilot's face. In later scenes: a
  BLAZING, harsh, overexposed wash of white-amber light flooding the
  cockpit. The accretion disk light grows in intensity across the film
  as the ship approaches.
- Creates hard, directional shadows inside the cockpit — the
  windshield frame casts geometric shadow patterns across the interior
  that shift as the ship's orientation changes.
- On Elena's face: warm amber key light from the front (windshield
  side), producing visible shadow on the side of her face away from
  the disk. Her visor reflects the disk's image.

SOURCE 2 — COCKPIT INSTRUMENTS (console and displays):
- Cool blue-white and amber holographic light from console displays.
  Provides fill light on Elena's hands, chin, and lower face when
  she looks down at controls.
- Small, directional, low-intensity — never overpowering Source 1.
- As displays fail through the film, this source dims and disappears.

SOURCE 3 — EMERGENCY LIGHTING (red strips):
- Deep crimson-red strip LEDs along cockpit floor edges and ceiling
  seams. Harsh, angular, low-intensity.
- Becomes the SOLE cockpit light after main power failure (Scene 6+).
- Creates a dramatic red wash from below and above, leaving the
  middle of Elena's face in shadow — filled only by the accretion
  disk's light through the windshield.
- The combination of RED EMERGENCY LIGHT + AMBER ACCRETION DISK LIGHT
  through the windshield is the film's signature color palette for
  the second half.

EXTERIOR LIGHTING:
- The ship's hull is lit ONLY by the accretion disk's light — warm
  amber-gold wash on the side facing the disk, absolute darkness on
  the far side. Engine pods glow blue-white from thrust. Radiator
  panels glow faint red.
- The black hole itself emits NO light — it is a circle of absolute
  darkness. Only the accretion disk and the gravitationally lensed
  starlight provide illumination.

LIGHTING PROGRESSION (the visual countdown):
- Scenes 0-2: Amber glow is SOFT, DISTANT — the cockpit is mostly
  lit by normal instruments. Balanced, comfortable lighting.
- Scenes 3-5: Amber glow INTENSIFIES — accretion disk light begins
  to overpower cockpit instruments. Shadows sharpen. Disk reflections
  visible on all surfaces.
- Scenes 6-8: Emergency red + intensifying amber. Most instruments
  dead. Elena's face is lit by two sources only: accretion disk
  (amber, through windshield) and emergency strips (red, from
  edges). Maximum dramatic contrast.
- Scenes 9-10: Accretion disk light is BLINDING — filling the
  windshield completely, washing out the cockpit in white-amber.
  Elena's face is overexposed from the front, red-lit from below.
  The visual overwhelm mirrors the narrative overwhelm.

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON 3]: Accretion disk light is BRIGHT but
SHARP — it has clear directional character, defined shadow edges, and
does not wash out into soft diffused bloom. The black hole's darkness
next to the disk's brightness must maintain MAXIMUM CONTRAST at all
times.
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
GRAVITY & ACCELERATION:
- Cockpit gravity: provided by the ship's thrust vector. When engines
  fire, gravity pushes Elena into her seat (standard 1g operational
  thrust). When engines cut, gravity drops to near-zero — loose
  objects begin to float, Elena's ponytail lifts, dust motes drift.
  Gravity fluctuations increase as the ship enters stronger tidal
  zones.
- TIDAL DIFFERENTIAL: As the ship approaches, the difference in
  gravitational pull between the bow (closer to the black hole) and
  the stern (farther) increases. This manifests as: hull groaning,
  instrument calibration drift, and eventually visible structural
  stress (hairline cracks in the windshield frame, warping of
  interior panels).

GRAVITATIONAL LENSING (visible through windshield):
- The starfield behind the black hole is WARPED — stars near the
  black hole's edge are stretched into thin arcs, duplicated, and
  brightened. This lensing effect INCREASES as the ship approaches.
- Scene 0: minimal lensing — a slight warping of stars near the
  black hole
- Scene 5: moderate — stars within 5 degrees of the black hole are
  visibly arced
- Scene 10: extreme — the entire visible starfield is warped toward
  the black hole, creating a tunnel-like "drain" visual

ACCRETION DISK DYNAMICS:
- The disk ROTATES visibly — inner edge orbiting faster than outer.
  This differential rotation creates visible shearing patterns in
  the plasma.
- The disk emits light, heat, and radiation — the cockpit temperature
  rises as the ship approaches (visible: Elena sweats, wipes brow).
- JET: A narrow, faint, vertical column of high-energy particles
  is visible along the black hole's rotational axis — visible from
  the side as a thin bright line extending above and below the disk.
