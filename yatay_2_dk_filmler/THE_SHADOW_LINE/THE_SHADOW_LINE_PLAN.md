Giant photovoltaic arrays and heat radiator panels on deep black space background without stars due to intense light glare. Sharp 45-degree diagonal shadow line separating blinding orange solar plasma radiation zone from pitch-black shadow zone. High-contrast metallic hull plating with conduits, pipes, and a mechanical cooling valve assembly with a brass toggle latch safety catch.

Horizontal 16:9 widescreen format, high contrast cinematic lighting, sharp focus on mechanical details, dynamic handheld motion for action, steady lock for close-up decisions. Anamorphic lens characteristics with subtle horizontal blue/gold flare streaks under direct solar plasma illumination.
