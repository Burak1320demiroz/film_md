## 📖 BIBLE'LAR

### 👤 Character Bible
- **Astronot A (Kaptan Vance):** Male astronaut, wearing a sleek white futuristic pressure suit with dark blue chest stripes and a yellow digital telemetry gauge mounted on his left wrist. The back of the suit is one continuous smooth white panel from collar to hip, carrying no backpack and no rear life-support container. Golden reflective helmet visor concealed face.
- **Astronot B (Uzman Ray):** Male astronaut, wearing a sleek white futuristic pressure suit with bright orange chest stripes. The back of the suit is one continuous smooth white panel from collar to hip, carrying no backpack and no rear life-support container. Golden reflective helmet visor.
- **Varlık (The Mimic Fauna):** Alien quadruped fauna of TRAPPIST-1e. Faceless, smooth head without eyes or mouth, translucent lavender skin. Four flexible dorsal tendrils extend from its back, emitting bioluminescent cyan and golden light waves. Zero human features.

### 🌍 World Bible
- **TRAPPIST-1e Planet Surface:** Violet crystalline regolith ground dotted with bioluminescent cyan moss patches. Atmospheric twilight under a deep purple sky. Distant craggy alien mountains on the horizon.

### 🎥 Camera Bible
- **Style:** 35mm Anamorphic, 50mm Prime, and 85mm Macro lenses, subtle handheld motion, cinematic 16:9 widescreen ratio.

### 💡 Lighting Bible
- **Key Light:** Soft violet ambient dusk light. reads as a clean dark silhouette against the floor.
- **Practical Light:** Cyan and golden bioluminescence emanating from the entity's dorsal tendrils and the telemetry scanner beam.

### ⚙️ Physics Bible
- **Low Gravity (0.7g):** Slow, deliberate steps without kicking up heavy dust. Smooth, fluid movement of alien tendrils.

### 🚫 Negative Bible — `KILAVUZ_REHBERI` §5 Negatif Çekirdek
`Single unbroken frame, 0% bloom, no glow filter, no soft focus. No readable text, letters, digits, numbers or symbols on any display, surface, patch or sign. No backpack, no rear life-support pack, suit back is a smooth single panel from collar to waist.`
