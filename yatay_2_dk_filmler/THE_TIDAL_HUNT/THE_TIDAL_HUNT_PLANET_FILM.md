## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: FLUCTA

```
TIDAL PHYSICS: An ocean world locked into an unusually close, massive-moon orbit —
the same gravitational principle behind Earth's tides (and the even more extreme
tidal locking seen in systems like Pluto-Charon), taken to a planetary extreme.
Instead of a modest tidal range, Flucta's single oversized moon pulls the entire
ocean back on a 6-hour cycle so complete that whole continental shelves — up to 10
kilometers of seabed — are exposed and resubmerged twice within a single day.
Nothing here is magic — it is real tidal-lock physics taken to its logical, awesome
extreme.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: The 6-hour cycle is fixed, mechanical, and merciless. The sea
retreats at a measurable, visible rate for roughly the first hour, exposes the full
seabed for the middle hours, then returns as a single continuous wave front in the
final hour — not a gradual refill, but a genuine, physically real wave, driven by
the sheer volume of returning water funneled by the seabed's shape.

THE SIX HOURS (fixed structure, every cycle):
- HOUR 0-1 — RETREAT BEGINS: the ocean surface drops visibly, first rocks and reef
  crowns break the surface.
- HOUR 1-2 — EMERGING FOREST: the submerged forest breaks the surface, wet,
  glistening, still dripping and steaming faintly in the sun.
- HOUR 2-3 — DESERT PLAIN: the sea has fully retreated; the ancient seabed lies bare
  and dry, cracked and pale.
- HOUR 3-4 — HUNTING HOUR: land predators emerge from shelter to hunt the exposed
  seabed's food before the water returns.
- HOUR 4-5 — RETURN ALARM: the horizon begins to darken and rise; every creature on
  the exposed seabed begins moving toward high ground.
- HOUR 5-6 — THE GREAT WAVE: the ocean returns as a single wave front, swallowing
  the seabed whole.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: near-Earth gravity (0.97g), a humid, salt-heavy atmosphere.
The oversized moon (VARIS) is visible in the sky throughout the cycle, unusually
large and close, its gravitational pull the visible cause of the entire event.

SOUND: Retreat begins as a low, continuous roar of draining water. The Emerging
Forest drips and creaks. The Desert Plain falls nearly silent but for wind over
cracked ground. Hunting Hour carries distant animal calls and running footfalls.
The Return Alarm brings the shrill mass scream of the Alarm Birds. The Great Wave is
a deep, rolling roar, growing to a wall of white noise. After it passes: total,
sudden silence.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. DALGA KIYI

```
IDENTITY: 33-year-old Turkish marine biologist, the first researcher to attempt a
full solo crossing of Flucta's exposed seabed within a single 6-hour tidal cycle.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Sun-weathered olive skin with real, visible pore
texture, deep laugh lines at the eyes, a scatter of salt-and-sun freckles across the
nose and cheeks, no airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. A strong, symmetric-leaning
but not perfect jaw, a faint pale scar along the left jawline from an old diving
accident. Dark eyes, sharp and quick, with a habitual glance toward the horizon.
Slightly chapped lips, windburned. A real, weathered, capable human face.

HAIR: Dark brown, cropped short and tight to the scalp, entirely practical, no loose
strands to catch water or wind.

BUILD: Athletic, broad-shouldered, 1.75m, built equally for swimming and running —
a powerful, economical mover.

SUIT: Fitted, dark ocean-blue waterproof survey suit with coral-orange accent
piping at the seams, heavily mission-worn:
- a low-profile chest-mounted rebreather unit, integrated into the suit, never a
  separate handheld device
- webbed inserts between fingers and toes on her diving gloves and boots, folding
  flat for running, unfolding for swimming — real amphibious adaptation, not a
  costume detail
- salt residue and wet sheen visible across the suit depending on the hour, drying
  to a pale crust by the Desert Plain hours
- a fully transparent, seamless dive mask/visor always revealing her complete face,
  beaded with water in the early hours, dry and dusty by the Hunting Hour
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY OF HANDHELD TOOLS — no scanners, no spears. All readings,
  including a constant countdown to the cycle's final hour, come from a single
  scratched, well-used wrist display on her left forearm.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: A controlled sprinter's economy — she runs low and fast across
uneven wet or cracked ground, glances at her wrist display and the horizon in the
same practiced motion, and has a habit of shielding her eyes with one hand when
scanning open distance under direct sun.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — AMPHIBIOUS SURVEY VESSEL _GELGİT_

```
HULL LENGTH: 140 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: An amphibious survey vessel capable of holding position on a
permanently dry rock spire safely above the tidal cycle's reach, used only to drop
Dalga at the start of her crossing and to retrieve her after the Great Wave has
passed — it never operates on the exposed seabed itself during the active cycle,
by design, since nothing built for landing could outrun the returning wave.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a broad, low central hull, 70 meters, matte deep-blue and white, with a single row
  of reinforced viewports, each approximately 1 meter in diameter, running its
  length as a scale ruler
- four wide, splayed stabilizer legs, 20 meters each, fitted with broad footpads for
  stable anchoring on uneven wet rock
- two hover-capable engine pods, each 8 meters in diameter, mounted at the stern
- one forward cockpit window, 2.4 meters high by 4 meters wide, positioned at the
  hull's bow

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 1m viewports as a
scale ruler along the hull, the 2.4m cockpit window with Dalga visible inside at
correct proportion, Dalga's 1.75m figure beside a stabilizer leg, or the hull
silhouetted against the rock spire or the returning Great Wave for direct scale
contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective, correct
engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must never appear
miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 140m hull
in view AND (b) a legible human face. Across the film, ship-scale shots and
character-scale shots are always kept as separate, dedicated scenes.

GIANT-CREATURE SCALE RULE: The same qualifier applies to the Desert Hunter (12m),
the Underwater Trees (30m), the Giant Water Serpent (80m), and the Great Wave (50m)
— every prompt featuring one states its exact scale and includes a human or ship
reference where physically possible.
```

no shadow, no motion.

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 0.97g fixed throughout.
- The ocean retreats at a visible, measurable rate across Hour 0-2, exposing up to
  10 kilometers of seabed by Hour 2.
- The Great Wave (Hour 5-6) is a single, genuine 50-meter wave front, driven by real
  fluid volume and seabed funneling — not a gradual tide refilling, and not an
  unmotivated CGI wall.
- Underwater Trees are a real photosynthetic-predatory hybrid organism: in water
  they stand as rooted, branching 30-meter "trees"; on air exposure they contract
  and writhe like a scaled-up sea anemone or tube worm retracting from touch.
- Tide Crabs (5m) are armored for both terrestrial sprinting and aquatic life.
- The Desert Hunter (12m) hunts only within its Hour 3-4 window.
- The Giant Water Serpent (80m) travels only within the returning wave itself.
- Human-to-environment scale: Dalga 1.75m; ship 140m; Tide Crabs 5m; Desert
  Hunter 12m; Underwater Trees 30m; Migrant Herd 2m; Great Wave 50m; Giant
  Water Serpent 80m; Alarm Birds 1m.

COLLISION INTEGRITY [LESSON 17 — CRITICAL]: ALL objects obey RIGID BODY COLLISION.
Nothing phases through, clips through, or merges with any other solid object. Ship
landing gear rests ON rock, not through it. Creature limbs contact surfaces, not
penetrate. Character limbs interact WITH creature bodies, never through them.
Tentacles/branches collide, never pass through. Every solid has an impenetrable
boundary.

CREATURE LOCOMOTION [LESSON 18]: Every creature moves with ARTICULATED LIMB MOTION:
- Walking/running: each leg lifts, plants, pushes with visible weight transfer
- Flying (Alarm Birds): wings flap with joint articulation, maintain shape
- Swimming (Serpent): body undulates with consistent rhythm
Creatures NEVER slide, glide, or drift across surfaces without limb movement.

WEIGHT & CONTACT [LESSON 4]: Visible weight on every surface. No floating, no
sliding. Rigid skeletal proportions. No jello, no rubber-banding.

DYNAMIC WATER [LESSON 14]: Ocean shows continuous micro-motion. Great Wave shows
spray at crest, foam, internal turbulence. NEVER frozen, static, or gel-like.

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: Thruster exhaust = volumetric with real
turbulence. Dust kicked by creatures/running = volumetric. Never frozen or flat.

CONTACT INTEGRITY [LESSON 11]: Hands/fingers NEVER merge with surfaces.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: Terrain remains STATIC. No melting, warping.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts, no transitions, no dissolves, no fades, no jump cuts, no montage, no
editing, no multiple camera angles, no scene changes, no camera teleportation.
No shaky camera, no video game chase-cam. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look,
no Unreal Engine aesthetic, no video game aesthetic. No airbrushed or plastic skin.
No unnatural perfect facial symmetry. No oversaturated colors. No bloom, no glow
filter, no dreamy haze. No disaster-movie imagery. No cartoonish creature design.
No unmotivated instant wave. No frozen/static water — ocean and wave must show
continuous motion. No object phasing — nothing passes through any solid object.
No creature sliding — all creatures use articulated limb motion. No hand-surface
merging. No terrain melting or morphing. No frozen particles or static exhaust.
No rubber-banding or jello effect. No face morphing behind visor. No floating
heads. No unmotivated slow-motion. No warped or mismatched reflections. No
handheld tools in her hands. No text overlay, no AI-generated text on displays,
no title card, no watermarks.

VISOR PERSISTENCE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE.
FACE LOCK [LESSON 16]: Face maintains IDENTICAL bone structure throughout.
SUIT CONTINUITY [LESSON 8]: Equipment matches CHARACTER BIBLE EXACTLY.
NO AI TEXT [LESSON 13]: Wrist display = abstract patterns only. NEVER text.
REFLECTION ACCURACY [LESSON 19]: All reflections match source object accurately.
```

# THE TIDAL HUNT

### "Deniz her 6 saatte çekiliyor. Ve çekildiğinde, kıtalar çıkıyor — üzerinde canlılarla birlikte."

Tek karakter (Dr. Dalga Kıyı), sıfır kesme, sürekli akış — devasa bir ayla kilitlenmiş
bir okyanus gezegeninde, 6 saatlik gel-git döngüsüne karşı bir yarış.

Bu belge tam **AI Production Bible** formatında, **13 sahne** olacak şekilde
yazılmıştır (istek üzerine standart yapıya dönülüp Sahne 10 eklendi: 0-10 ana anlatı
