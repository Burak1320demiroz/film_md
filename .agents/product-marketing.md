## What we make

- AI-generated cinematic **sci-fi short films** — space genre ONLY
  (tür kararı 26.08.2026; zombi/savaş kapalı).
- Formats: vertical 9:16 shorts; horizontal 16:9 in 2-4 / 3-5 / 7-9 /
  10-20 minute bands. Every film is written in this repo as a
  scene-by-scene prompt package (PLAN → BIBLE → PROMPT → QA_SPEC).
- Produced with AI video models (Veo pipeline; Google Flow for music).
