>

> Ton baştan sona: arkeolojik hayranlık, melankoli, huşu. Korku filmi değil, bir keşif şiiri.

> **GEZGİN — DR. MERVE AKAY**

> öncü üyesi (tek başına ön-keşif görevi).

> yapıların eski taş ve bronz rengiyle uyumlu, kamufle olacak kadar doğal.

## 🎬 SAHNE 0 — HOOK (YÜRÜYÜŞ ADIMI)
**"Merve yerde küçük bir çatlak görüyor, eğiliyor — çatlak aslında dev bir yüzün göz kırışıklığı."**
*(Bu sahne videonun en başına, izleyiciyi ilk 3 saniyede durduracak şekilde monte edilir.)*

### 📸 GÖRSEL PROMPT
```
Cinematic sci-fi still image — Scene 0 (cold open hook) of 12. Solo female xeno-archaeologist
alien-megastructure planet discovery film. DESIGNED FOR MAXIMUM VIRAL SCALE SHOCK.
Time: Dawn. Location: Ground level on a mossy, seemingly ordinary terrain — before the scale
is understood.

SUBJECT: EXTREME CLOSE-UP, ground level, looking along the surface of what appears to be a long,
deep crack in mossy stone. DR. MERVE AKAY crouches at its edge, reaching one gloved hand into
the crack, exploring it with her fingers — it is wide enough for her arm to enter to the elbow.
A small reference object: her full helmet sits beside her on the "ground."

THE SCALE REVEAL: The camera angle is carefully chosen so that, in the far background, the full
context becomes unmistakably clear — this "crack in the stone" is the OUTER CORNER OF AN EYELID
of a colossal buried face, the eye itself the size of a lake, the entire "terrain" Merve stands
on is the forehead of an entity so vast its full extent disappears into the horizon in both
directions.

COMPOSITION: Merve and the "crack" dominate the foreground in sharp detail — ordinary, almost
domestic scale. The horrifying/awe-inspiring background context visible over her shoulder:
the vast curve of a buried brow ridge stretching to the horizon, a second eyelid-crack visible
far in the distance, the scale comprehensible only now, all at once.

LIGHTING: Soft gold dawn light raking low across the "terrain" surface, picking out the texture
of what is now clearly carved stone eyelid detail at impossible scale — each carved line
several meters deep.

STYLE: Photorealistic cinematic, 35mm, f/2.8, ISO 300. Sharp foreground on Merve's hand in
the crack, background just sharp enough to make the scale reveal unmistakable. Engineered for
the exact moment of comprehension.

Output: 16:9, 4K, no text, no watermark.
```

### 🎥 VIDEO PROMPT
```
Widescreen 16:9. Single continuous shot, 0 cuts, 7 seconds. (Designed as the cold-open hook —
plays before any title card or context.)
Starts on the exact Scene 0 image. Merve is crouched, arm in the "crack," studying it closely.
At 0s: she traces the crack's edge with her fingers, fully absorbed — treating it as an ordinary
geological feature, no alarm.
At 2s: she pulls her arm out and stands, taking one step back to look further down its length —
as she rises, the camera RISES WITH HER, slowly revealing more of the background context.
At 4s: the camera continues rising slightly above her eyeline — the full impossible background
context snaps into total clarity: the "terrain" she stands on is a face, its scale now
undeniable, the horizon itself is its jaw, the second eyelid-crack in the distance the size
of a canyon.
At 5s: Merve goes completely still, arm at her side, the comprehension hitting all at once.
By 7s: the camera holds on her frozen silhouette against the now-understood landscape — one
human figure on the forehead of something beyond scale. Hard cut to Scene 1. No cuts within the shot.
```

Genre: Cinematic ambient orchestral-electronic hybrid, archaeological-wonder score.
