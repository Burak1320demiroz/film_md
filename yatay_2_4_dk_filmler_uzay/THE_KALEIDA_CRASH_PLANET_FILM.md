## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: KALEİDA

```
PLANETARY PHYSICS: A terrestrial world with a thick, humid, breathable-
pressure-equivalent atmosphere and a surface almost entirely covered by
a single continuous forest of naturally formed PIEZOELECTRIC CRYSTAL
GROWTHS — not biological trees but mineral formations that grow, branch,
and reach heights of 20-80 meters through a unique, slow geological
accretion process, their crystalline lattice structure dense enough to
absorb and re-emit kinetic energy as visible light.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE — KINETIC ABSORPTION: When any significant kinetic
force strikes the crystal structures — a footstep, a falling object, an
impact, a shockwave — the energy is not transferred mechanically (no
shattering, no cracking, no fragmentation) but is instead converted to
a brief, sharp VISIBLE LIGHT PULSE emitted by the crystal at the point
of impact, its color and intensity scaling directly with the force
applied. A footstep produces a soft, brief neon-violet glow in the
crystal beneath the boot; a crash-landing produces a massive, blinding
violet-white flash visible for kilometers. This is real piezoelectric
physics (the same principle as quartz lighters and ultrasound
transducers) taken to planetary extreme.

THE CRYSTAL FOREST: Dense, towering formations of translucent-to-
opaque mineral crystal, ranging from 20m saplings to 80m ancient spires,
their surfaces covered in natural prismatic facets that continuously
refract ambient light into soft, slowly shifting rainbow micro-patterns
on everything nearby. Each crystal trunk branches upward like a
petrified tree, ending in flat, broad crystalline "canopy plates" that
interlock overhead to form a continuous prismatic ceiling, filtering
all ground-level light through their facets.

GROUND-LEVEL LIGHT: Beneath the crystal canopy, ground level exists in
a permanent state of soft, diffused, rainbow-filtered light — shifting
prismatic patterns playing across every surface as the planet's star
moves. The ground itself is a bed of fine, pale crystalline sand and
compacted crystal fragments, piezoelectrically active: every footstep,
every dropped object, every vibration produces a brief neon-violet glow
at the point of contact, leaving a fading luminous footprint trail that
persists for approximately 15 seconds before dimming.

THE MERCURY RIVER: A wide, slow-flowing river of hyper-dense, metallic-
silver liquid — not true mercury but a naturally occurring metallic
colloidal suspension, perfectly reflective, dense enough to support
floating crystal debris on its surface. It flows through a deep crystal
canyon, its mirror-surface reflecting the prismatic canopy above in
perfect, undistorted detail.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: 1.05g, thick, humid, breathable-pressure
equivalent — warm and dense, with visible atmospheric haze reducing
long-distance visibility to approximately 2km. No wind at ground level
beneath the crystal canopy; above the canopy, strong upper-atmosphere
thermal currents.

SKY (above canopy): A warm, hazy gold-amber sky with a single large,
warm yellow-white star. Kaleida also orbits a distant binary companion
— a cooler, smaller, deep orange-red star visible at certain hours,
producing a brief DUAL SUNSET when both stars set in sequence.

SOUND: The crystal forest is acoustically rich — every impact, every
step, every vibration produces a clear, bell-like resonance that
propagates through the connected crystal lattice, creating a continuous,
layered harmonic hum that shifts in pitch and density depending on the
size and proximity of the crystals. The crashed mothership's wreckage
produces a constant, low, metallic groan audible from kilometers away.

CRASH DEBRIS FIELD: The mothership's atmospheric breakup scattered a
trail of burning debris across a 12km stretch of crystal forest,
leaving a visible, blackened scar of fused and heat-stressed crystal
formations, flickering emergency lights from embedded wreckage
fragments, and a persistent column of thick, dark smoke rising from the
main impact crater where the hull came to rest.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. AYLA ERDEM

```
IDENTITY: 29-year-old Turkish astrophysicist and emergency-systems
engineer aboard the deep-space survey vessel Kaşif. When the ship's
reactor containment failed during atmospheric survey, she was the ONLY
crew member to reach an escape pod before the hull breached. She is
now alone on an uncharted planet with a damaged suit, a functioning
visor, and a single objective: reach the mothership's wreckage and
activate its emergency rescue beacon before her suit's life support
runs out.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm olive-tan skin with real,
visible pore texture, currently showing visible stress — faint
capillary flush across cheekbones, a thin fresh cut across the left
eyebrow sealed with the suit's auto-medical strip (a small, visible
silver adhesive patch), a smudge of dark soot on her right cheek from
the escape. No airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. A sharp, angular
face with high cheekbones and a strong jaw. Thick, dark eyebrows, the
left one interrupted by the fresh cut. Intense, dark brown eyes with
visible adrenaline-widened pupils, a look of focused determination
rather than panic. A straight nose, a small mole beneath the left
nostril. Lips pressed firmly together — a habit when concentrating
under pressure.

HAIR: Black, thick, pulled into a tight high bun, partially loosened
by the escape — several strands have pulled free and hang across her
forehead and temples, catching prismatic light.

BUILD: Athletic, lean, 1.68m, a trained emergency-response physicality
— she moves fast when needed, climbs confidently, and makes snap
decisions with her body before her mind fully processes.

SUIT — DAMAGED KAŞIF CREW EMERGENCY SUIT:
- base color: deep charcoal-black with amber-orange emergency accent
  panels along the forearms, shins, and spine (designed to be visible
  in low-light rescue operations)
- DAMAGE (consistent across ALL scenes from Scene 2 onward): a 15cm
  tear in the outer shell across the right shoulder, exposing the pale
  inner thermal layer beneath; the left knee panel cracked and showing
  a hairline fracture; fine crystal dust ground into every seam and
  joint from the crash
- a fully transparent, seamless visor ALWAYS revealing her complete
  face — the visor has a fresh, visible diagonal scratch across the
  lower-left quadrant (from the crash impact) that catches light in
  every scene, a persistent visual reminder of the crash
- a single wrist display on her left forearm showing TWO abstract
  readouts only: a pulsing amber ring (suit integrity — wide = stable,
  tight = critical) and a slow red pulse (life support remaining — the
  pulse rate increases as reserves drop, NEVER showing numbers or text)
- HANDS: reinforced, scuffed black climbing gloves with amber-orange
  grip pads on the palms and fingertips
- BOOTS: thick-soled, broad-tread emergency boots, the treads visibly
  clogged with crystal dust from Scene 2 onward
- a short, retractable multi-tool tether built into the right forearm
  gauntlet — her only "tool," used for climbing anchor points and lever
  actions, never held separately in her hand

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every damage detail, every smudge,
every crystal-dust accumulation listed above remains IDENTICAL and
PERSISTENT across all scenes — suit damage only ACCUMULATES, never
repairs itself, and no new damage appears that wasn't caused by a
visible on-screen event.

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE in every frame
regardless of head angle. The diagonal scratch is visible in every shot.

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When touching ANY surface (crystal,
wreckage, river bank, climbing surface), her hands/fingers maintain
COMPLETE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY — clear visible boundary at all contact
points, never merging or melting into what she touches.

FACE LOCK [LESSON 16]: Face maintains IDENTICAL bone structure, eye
spacing, nose shape, mole position, cut location throughout.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Fast, decisive, efficient — the economy of someone
trained for emergencies. She runs with a low center of gravity. She
climbs with confident, planned hand placements. She pauses only to
assess, never to contemplate. When she is still, she is listening or
planning her next move. The only moment of true stillness is the final
scene, when the beacon is lit and her mission is complete.
```

## 🦎 CREATURE BIBLE — PRİZMA HAYALET (THE REFRACTOR)

```
SPECIES: A solitary, large (approximately 3 meters tall at the
shoulder, 5 meters nose to tail) quadrupedal predator-analog native to
Kaleida's crystal forest, called a Refractor because its hide is
composed of the same piezoelectric crystal material as the forest
itself — making it nearly invisible when stationary, its body refracting
and bending light around itself like a living lens, visible only as a
faint prismatic distortion in the air.

APPEARANCE (when visible): A lean, powerful, feline-analog body plan —
four articulated legs, each ending in broad, flat, crystalline paw-pads
that leave no luminous footprints (unlike the human — the Refractor's
native crystal biology does not trigger the piezoelectric response). A
long, tapering head with two large, forward-facing eyes that glow a
soft, steady amber — the ONLY part of the Refractor consistently visible
even when the rest of its body is refracting. A ridge of tall,
translucent crystal spines runs from the back of its skull to the base
of its tail.

BEHAVIOR: Curious, not hostile. It approaches Dr. Erdem because her
suit's amber-orange emergency panels and the energy signature of her
damaged electronics are unlike anything in its environment. It circles
her slowly, its amber eyes tracking her movement, its crystal-hide
shifting between near-invisible refraction and brief, partial visibility
as it moves through patches of direct vs. diffused light. It NEVER
attacks, NEVER threatens — it observes, approaches, and eventually moves
on. The encounter is awe, not horror.

FIXED ANATOMY [LESSON 2]: Rigid, locked body plan — four legs, one
head, one tail, one dorsal spine-ridge. No shape-shifting, no morphing,
no anatomy drift between frames. Background rendering uses shallow DOF.

CREATURE LOCOMOTION [LESSON 18]: Each of the four legs lifts, swings
forward, and plants with visible, articulated joint motion and real
weight transfer — NEVER sliding, gliding, or floating across surfaces.
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 1.05g, standard-feeling, consistent throughout.
- KINETIC ABSORPTION RULE: Crystal structures absorb kinetic energy and
  re-emit it as visible light — proportional to force. A footstep =
  soft neon-violet glow lasting ~15 seconds. A thrown rock = brighter
  flash, ~3 seconds. The escape pod crash = massive violet-white flash
  lasting ~1 second. The mothership's shockwave = a propagating wave of
  intense violet light sweeping through the forest at visible speed.
  CRITICALLY: this means crystals DO NOT SHATTER on normal impact —
  they absorb, pulse, and remain intact. The only exception: the
  mothership's crash trench, where sustained, extreme, multi-second
  contact at terminal velocity exceeded the absorption threshold,
  fusing and deforming (not shattering) the crystal in the trench.
- LUMINOUS FOOTPRINT RULE: Every boot-step on the crystal sand produces
  a clearly visible neon-violet glow at the point of contact, persisting
  for approximately 15 seconds before fading. Dr. Erdem's trail is
  therefore continuously visible as a fading line of glowing footprints
  behind her in every walking/running scene.
- THE MERCURY RIVER: Hyper-dense, metallic-silver liquid — supports
  floating crystal debris, reflects its surroundings in perfect mirror
  detail, flows with continuous visible current. It is NOT water — it is
  denser, slower, and perfectly reflective. [DYNAMIC WATER adaptation:
  the river must show continuous, slow, heavy flow with visible current
  and realistic wake around any object breaking its surface.]
- COLLISION INTEGRITY [LESSON 17]: ALL objects obey rigid-body collision.
  The pod does not phase through the ground. Dr. Erdem does not phase
  through crystal surfaces. Wreckage rests ON surfaces.
- VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: Smoke from the wreck, crystal dust
  from footsteps, debris from the crash — all volumetric, physically
  accurate, dispersing under 1.05g, never frozen or static.
- Human-to-environment scale: Dr. Erdem 1.68m; escape pod 3m long;
  crystal forest 20-80m; the Refractor 3m at shoulder; mothership wreck
  sections 130-200m; engine nacelles 12m; beacon tower 30m; crash
  trench 2km.

GRAVITY VECTOR [LESSON 15]: Hair, loose suit elements, dust, smoke,
and debris all respond to consistent 1.05g downward gravity at all
times.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: Crystal formations remain STATIC and
rigid unless explicitly struck by a kinetic event. The forest does not
breathe, warp, or morph.
```
