## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: VERDANA

```
SCALE PHYSICS: A single, continuous, planet-spanning forest world with no oceans and
no bare ground beyond the forest floor itself. This is real botanical and ecological
stratification — the same layered logic of an Earth rainforest's emergent layer,
canopy, understory, and floor — taken to a kilometer-scale extreme. Slightly reduced
gravity (0.85g) and an unusually dense, oxygen-rich lower atmosphere allow structural
biology (wood fiber, leaf rib cartilage, fungal chitin) to support organisms and
plant structures far beyond Earth-normal scale, in the same way lower gravity and
denser atmosphere are hypothesized to have supported Earth's own Carboniferous
gigantism. Nothing here is magic — it is real structural biology taken to its
logical, awesome extreme.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: Each of Verdana's seven vertical layers has its own distinct
light quality, atmosphere, sound, and resident life, changing measurably every few
hundred meters of descent. The single governing tree species is uniform across the
whole world — one planet, one forest, one continuous organism-scale — but its
character transforms completely with altitude.

THE SEVEN LAYERS (top to bottom):

1. TAÇ KATMANI / THE CROWN (2,000-3,000m): Above the cloud line, gold-green sunlight
   direct and unfiltered across an unbroken sea of giant leaves, each leaf up to 10
   meters across, rigid-ribbed like a scaled-up lily pad, flexing but load-bearing
   underfoot. Home to the SUN SAILORS — translucent gliding creatures with a 20-meter
   membrane wingspan, riding thermals on the cloud line.

2. RÜZGAR KORİDORU / THE WIND CORRIDOR (1,500m): A wide-open band between major
   branches, strong sustained cross-winds funneled by the trunk's geometry, bright
   white-gold light with intermittent blue sky visible through gaps. Home to the
   BRANCH RUNNERS — fast, four-armed primate-analogs that leap branch to branch on
   momentum alone.

3. SİS KUŞAĞI / THE MIST BELT (1,000m): A thick, permanent band of white fog trapped
   against the trunk, diffusing all light into a soft, shadowless white-grey glow.
   Dotted with giant bracket-fungus platforms up to 12 meters across, naturally
   spring-elastic. Home to the MIST JELLYFISH — 5-meter translucent, gas-bladder
   drifting creatures that feed on airborne moisture.

4. YOSUN KATEDRALİ / THE MOSS CATHEDRAL (500m): Vast curtains of hanging moss, tens
   of meters long, filtering the fading light into a warm green-gold cathedral glow.
   Home to the MOSS GIANTS — slow, gentle, 10-meter herbivores that move through the
   curtains like drifting shadows.

5. KARANLIK KATMAN / THE DARK LAYER (200m): Almost no ambient light reaches here;
   every visible glow is biological, produced by real bioluminescent chemistry
   (luciferin-luciferase reaction, as in Earth fireflies and foxfire fungi) scaled up
   across bark, fungus, and animal life alike. Home to the LIGHT HUNTERS — 3-meter
   anglerfish-analog predators that hunt using their own bioluminescent lure, curious
   rather than aggressive, but unsettling at close range.

6. KÖK LABİRENTİ / THE ROOT LABYRINTH (50m): A maze of root buttresses tens of
   meters thick, riddled with underground waterways and clear, mineral-lit crystal
   pools. Home to the CRYSTAL CRABS — 1-meter transparent-shelled scavengers living
   in the shallow pools.

7. ZEMİN / THE GROUND (0m): No external light source reaches this level at all — and
   yet the ground itself glows, a warm amber-white bioluminescent pattern spread
   across its entire visible surface. This is the film's final reveal: the "ground"
   is the dorsal surface of the GROUND WORM, a single, colossal, slow-moving
   subterranean creature over 100 meters long, whose bioluminescent back has been
   mistaken for terrain by every visitor before her.

SOUND: Crown — steady high wind and distant thermal roar. Wind Corridor — a
continuous, powerful cross-wind howl. Mist Belt — near-total muffled silence, faint
dripping. Moss Cathedral — soft, resonant hush, occasional deep low groan from Moss
Giants. Dark Layer — near-total silence, broken only by faint clicks and the soft
hiss of bioluminescent chemistry. Root Labyrinth — constant running water. Ground —
absolute silence but for a slow, deep, almost sub-audible breathing rhythm.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. YAPRAK ORMAN

```
IDENTITY: 28-year-old Turkish xenobotanist and vertical-ecology specialist. First
human to descend Verdana's full seven-layer canopy in a single continuous expedition.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm tan skin with real, visible pore texture,
windburned cheeks and a scatter of sun-freckles across the nose, no airbrushing, no
plastic smoothness. A narrow, expressive face with a slightly upturned nose and a
small, faded scar through the right eyebrow from an old climbing fall. Hazel-green
eyes, wide-set, with visible catchlight and a habitual squint when judging distance
or grip. Natural asymmetric smile, a small chip in the left canine. Loose strands of
hair escaping her braid, damp with mist or sweat depending on the layer — a real,
weathered, capable human face.

HAIR: Dark auburn, braided tightly down the back, streaked with a few sun-lightened
strands at the crown.

BUILD: Lean, compact, 1.68m, dense climber's musculature visible through the suit,
low center of gravity, quick and precise in every movement.

SUIT: Ultra-lightweight dark emerald-green climbing suit with bioluminescent
yellow-green accent piping along the seams (functional, dims and brightens with her
own bio-monitor, never a costume glow), heavily mission-worn:
- a compact harness at hip and chest with an integrated, spring-loaded rope reel —
  the rope deploys and retracts automatically; it is never coiled or carried loose
  by hand except during active rappelling, where it runs through her gloved palm as
  a direct extension of the climb itself — the one deliberate exception to the
  empty-hands rule, consistent with how climbing sequences work elsewhere in this
  series
- a thin, foldable wing-membrane integrated into the suit's underarms and sides,
  deployable only for controlled gliding between layers, never a separate garment
- reinforced, high-grip climbing gloves and boot soles, scuffed and pale with dust,
  moss stain, and mineral residue depending on the layer
- a fully transparent, seamless visor always revealing her complete face, fogging
  faintly in the Mist Belt, beading with moisture in the Root Labyrinth
- HANDS EMPTY OF SEPARATE HANDHELD TOOLS — no scanners, no wands. All readings come
  from a single scratched, well-used wrist display on her left forearm. Her hands are
  used only for direct climbing, gripping, and the rope passing through her palms
  during descent.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Constant, fluid motion — she tests every leaf, branch, or
platform with a quick foot-tap before committing weight, transitions between
climbing, sliding, and jumping with barely a pause, and has a habit of glancing down
past her own feet before any drop, gauging distance with a quick, practiced squint.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — AERIAL SUPPORT VESSEL _SARMAŞIK_

```
HULL LENGTH: 55 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A compact, highly maneuverable aerial forest-operations vessel, built
to weave between giant branches and anchor directly onto bark or wood surfaces —
far smaller than typical exploration vessels, by design, so it can operate inside
the canopy itself rather than only above or below it.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a central hull pod, 30 meters long, housing the cockpit and a small field lab,
  with a single row of reinforced viewports, each approximately 0.8 meters in
  diameter, running its length as a scale ruler
- four articulated grapple-tendrils, each extending up to 25 meters, mounted at the
  hull's corners, used to anchor the vessel directly onto branches or trunk bark
- two quiet-hover repulsor pods, each 5 meters in diameter, mounted beneath the
  hull for vertical lift and controlled descent between layers
- one forward cockpit window, 1.8 meters high by 3 meters wide, positioned at the
  hull's bow

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 0.8m viewports as a
scale ruler along the hull, the 1.8m cockpit window with Yaprak visible inside at
correct proportion, a 25m grapple-tendril anchored against a branch of known giant
scale, or the hull silhouetted against a single giant leaf or moss curtain for
direct scale contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective, correct
engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must never appear
miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 55m hull
in view AND (b) a legible human face. Across the film, ship-scale shots and
character-scale shots are always kept as separate, dedicated scenes.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / VERTICAL-SCALE shots → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / EMOTIONAL / CHARACTER / ACTION shots → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (leaf texture, bioluminescent detail, water/crystal detail) →
  100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in, pull-back, tilt-up,
tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow lateral track, and slow vertical descent
tracking (reserved for rappelling, gliding, and falling sequences — Scenes 0, 2.5,
5, 8).

FRAMING RULE: no contradictory framing in one prompt (never "show the entire vertical
forest" and "push toward her face" together — see Shot-Split Rule).

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: every video prompt is exactly 10 seconds, single continuous shot,
no cuts.

CONTINUITY LOCK [LESSON LEARNED — CRITICAL]: This is a SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE.
The camera must not teleport, jump-cut, reset position, change angle suddenly, or
transition to a different setup. Every frame must flow directly and continuously
from the previous frame. The camera moves ONLY as specified in the MOTION description
— no additional movements, no resets, no edits. Each scene is generated as a
SEPARATE 10-second video — scenes must NEVER be combined into a single longer video.
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE (per layer — named explicitly, never just "cinematic lighting")

```
CROWN (2-3km): direct pale gold sunlight key light, soft white cloud-bounce fill,
deep green shadow beneath leaf ribs.

WIND CORRIDOR (1.5km): bright white-gold key light, intermittent cool blue sky-gap
fill, fast-moving cloud-shadow bands.

MIST BELT (1km): diffused, shadowless soft white-grey ambient glow, no directional
key, faint cool blue undertone within the fog itself.

MOSS CATHEDRAL (500m): warm filtered green-gold key light through hanging moss,
deep amber shadow within the curtains, soft golden dust-mote haze.

DARK LAYER (200m): near-total darkness as base state; cool blue-green
bioluminescent glow as the only light source, sourced explicitly from bark,
fungus, and the Light Hunters' lures — never an ambient glow.

ROOT LABYRINTH (50m): cold blue-white light reflected and refracted through the
crystal pools and running water, deep root-shadow black beyond the water's reach.

GROUND (0m): total absence of external light; warm amber-white bioluminescent glow
sourced entirely from the ground's own surface, the only light in the frame,
casting soft upward shadow onto Yaprak's face and suit.

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON LEARNED]: ALL bioluminescent light sources (Dark Layer,
Moss Cathedral glow, Ground Worm surface) must emit SHARP, DIRECTIONAL light with
clear falloff — NOT a soft, diffused, dreamy glow. Maintain HIGH CONTRAST between
lit and unlit areas. The light must create defined shadows, not wash out the entire
frame. Think practical film set lighting with actual physical light sources, NOT a
post-production bloom filter. Skin under bioluminescent light must retain visible
pore texture, natural subsurface scattering, and realistic shadow — never
airbrushed, never plastic, never smooth under colored light.
```

```
Every shot includes at least one of the following, drawn from this fixed catalog:

  weight
- a glance down past her own feet before any drop or jump, a practiced squint
  gauging distance
- rope sliding through her gloved palm during active rappelling, a controlled,

- a brief, controlled exhale before a jump, glide, or drop
- fine dust, spore, or moisture catching light around her as she moves through it
- a startled, held-breath stillness at the proximity of an unfamiliar creature
- a gloved hand brushing a surface and reacting to what it does (moss flaring,
  ground shifting)
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 0.85g fixed throughout.
- Giant leaves (Crown) flex measurably underfoot but remain load-bearing, per real
  rigid-rib leaf structure scaled up — never simply "solid ground."
- Bracket-fungus platforms (Mist Belt) are genuinely spring-elastic, absorbing and
  returning momentum with each landing, per real fungal chitin elasticity.
- Bioluminescence (Dark Layer, Moss Cathedral, Ground) is always sourced from a
  specific visible organism, bark pattern, or creature — never an unexplained
  ambient glow.
- Water in the Root Labyrinth behaves as ordinary Earth-normal fluid — no exotic
  viscosity, a deliberate contrast to the rest of the world's biological strangeness.
- Human-to-environment scale is always internally consistent: Yaprak is 1.68m; the
  ship is 55m; giant leaves are up to 10m across; Sun Sailors have a 20m wingspan;
  Mist Jellyfish are 5m; Moss Giants are 10m; Light Hunters are 3m; Crystal Crabs
  are 1m; the Ground Worm is 100m+.

WEIGHT & CONTACT RULE [LESSON LEARNED]: The character must show VISIBLE WEIGHT on
every surface contact. Footfalls must cause measurable surface reaction (leaf dip,
dust kick, water splash, platform compression). The character must never float,
hover, or glide across a surface — every step must read as a real human foot
pressing against a real physical surface with real mass.

BODY INTEGRITY [LESSON LEARNED]: The character's limbs, torso, and head must
maintain rigid skeletal proportions throughout all motion. No rubber-banding, no
jello effect, no elastic stretching of limbs during fast movements. Arms and legs
bend only at real joint positions (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle).

WATER INTERACTION RULE [LESSON LEARNED]: Water must interact physically and
correctly with the character's suit and body. Splashes must spray outward from
contact points, droplets must bead and run down surfaces, foam must form and
dissipate naturally. Water must never look painted on, static, or frozen.

CREATURE ANATOMY LOCK [LESSON LEARNED]: Every creature in every shot must maintain
FIXED, RIGID anatomy throughout the entire 10-second clip. Limb count, body shape,
head shape, and overall silhouette must remain identical from frame 1 to frame 300.
No morphing, no shape-shifting, no anatomical drift. Background creatures rendered
with SHALLOW DEPTH OF FIELD to prevent AI morphing artifacts.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY LOCK [LESSON LEARNED]: All background environmental elements
(trees, roots, rocks, mushrooms, moss, water surfaces) must remain COMPLETELY
STATIC and unchanging unless explicitly described as moving. No background breathing,
no edge warping, no texture swimming, no slow morphing of stationary objects.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts, no transitions, no dissolves, no fades, no jump cuts, no montage, no
editing, no multiple camera angles, no scene changes, no camera teleportation.
No shaky camera, no video game chase-cam. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look,
no Unreal Engine aesthetic, no video game aesthetic. No airbrushed or plastic skin,
no smooth skin under colored light. No unnatural perfect facial symmetry. No
oversaturated colors. No bloom, no glow filter, no soft focus glow, no dreamy haze,
no lens flare on bioluminescent sources. No cartoonish creature design — all fauna
must read as biologically plausible and photoreal, not stylized. No creature
morphing — locked anatomy throughout. No unexplained ambient glow (all
bioluminescence traced to a visible source). No background element morphing, no
edge warping, no texture swimming on stationary objects. No handheld tools in her
hands beyond the rope during active rappelling. No rubber-banding or jello effect
on limbs. No painted-on or static water splashes. No text overlay, no title card,
no credits, no subtitles, no watermarks, no logos, no on-screen text of any kind.

VISOR PERSISTENCE [LESSON LEARNED]: The transparent visor/helmet glass must remain
ALWAYS VISIBLE in every frame, regardless of head angle or lighting. It must NEVER
disappear, become invisible, or merge with the face.

SUIT CONTINUITY [LESSON LEARNED]: The character's suit, equipment, and all
accessories must match the CHARACTER BIBLE description EXACTLY in every scene. No
design changes, no color shifts, no added or removed equipment between scenes.
```

# THE CANOPY WORLD

### "Her katman farklı bir dünya. Aşağı indikçe kurallar değişiyor."

Tek karakter (Dr. Yaprak Orman), sıfır kesme, sürekli akış — 2-3km yüksekliğindeki
ağaçlarla kaplı bir gezegende, taçtan zemine yedi katmanlı dikey bir keşif ve iniş.

Bu belge tam **AI Production Bible** formatında, **12 sahne** olacak şekilde
yazılmıştır — her sahne tek bir 📸 image prompt ve tam **10 saniyelik** tek kesintisiz
🎥 image-to-video prompt içerir. Aksiyon omurgası kullanıcı konseptindeki dört ana
sahneye birebir sadık kalınarak kuruldu: **Sahne 2** (taç katmanında yaprak koşusu),
**Sahne 4** (sis kuşağında mantar platformu atlayışı), **Sahne 6** (karanlık katmanda
ışık avcılarından kaçış), **Sahne 8** (kök labirentinde nehir sürüklenmesi). İki viral
ara sahne (**2.5:** kanopiden atlayış, **5.5:** yosun katedralinde dokunuşla yayılan
ışık dalgası) eklendi. Gemi (Sarmaşık) bilinçli olarak küçük ve çevik tasarlandı —
dev dallar arasında manevra yapabilmesi için — ve film boyunca sadece iniş (Sahne 1)
ve final kurtarma (Sahne 9) sahnelerinde görünür.
