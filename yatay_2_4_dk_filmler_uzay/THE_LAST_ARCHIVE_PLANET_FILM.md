## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: BİBLİOS-VII

```
PLANETARY PURPOSE: An entire rocky planet, deliberately and completely re-engineered
by a now-vanished civilization over an unknown span of millennia into a single,
planet-wide physical data archive. Every visible rock formation — cliffs, canyon
walls, plains, mountain faces — is not natural geology but deliberately inscribed
storage medium: microscopically precise script etched at macroscopic scale directly
into the crust itself, layer upon layer, representing the accumulated knowledge,
history, art, and memory of an entire species across its entire physical existence.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: This is a library, not a ruin. Nothing here is broken, decayed,
or hostile. The inscriptions are still perfectly legible after eons because they were
carved at a molecular-hardened level specifically designed to survive geological time.
The tone throughout is reverent and solemn — a reading room the size of a world —
never eerie, never threatening.

THE SCRIPT: A dense, geometric, hierarchical script system, visible at multiple
scales simultaneously: from orbit, vast inscribed regions read as enormous abstract
patterns; walking distance reveals those patterns are made of smaller repeating
glyph-clusters; close inspection reveals each glyph itself contains even finer
sub-script — a genuinely fractal, self-similar writing system, implying unimaginable
information density. The script glows very faintly from within the rock under direct
starlight, a natural photoluminescent mineral property engineered into the stone
itself, making dense text-regions appear to hold a soft, warm inner light.

SURFACE STRUCTURE:
- vast plains of flat, inscribed bedrock, script running in uninterrupted bands to
  the horizon
- deep canyons whose sheer walls are entirely covered in inscription from base to
  rim, functioning as the archive's largest "pages"
- a colossal central structure — the Index Spire — a single, needle-thin natural-
  looking rock spire roughly 800 meters tall, itself entirely inscribed, slowly
  rotating on a near-frictionless internal mechanism (the archive's master catalog,
  built to physically rotate its face toward the ancient reading population as
  needed, now rotating for no one)
- shallow, perfectly still reflection pools where flat inscribed bedrock dips
  slightly, doubling the visible script in reflection
- no vegetation, no ruins of habitation — this was never a living-space, only ever
  a repository

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: Thin, breathable-pressure equivalent for suit purposes
(radiation and temperature protection required). Standard-feeling gravity,
approximately 0.95g. Minimal wind, allowing extremely long, clear sightlines across
inscribed plains.

SKY AS CHARACTER: A single, steady, warm golden-white sun, positioned to provide
consistent, even, raking light ideal for reading inscribed stone at any hour — as if
the orbit itself was engineered for legibility. At certain hours, a faint, high,
thin ring of ancient orbital data-satellites (long dead, drifting) is visible as a
pale line across the sky.

LIGHT BEHAVIOR: Harsh, directional golden-white sunlight rakes across inscribed
surfaces, throwing every carved glyph into sharp micro-relief — shadow and light
together are what make the script readable at all. The stone's faint internal
photoluminescence adds a second, softer glow independent of the sun, most visible in
shadowed canyon interiors.

SOUND: Thin atmosphere permits faint sound — a very subtle, resonant hum is audible
near the Index Spire, its rotation mechanism still faintly active after eons.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. NEHİR ULUÇ

```
IDENTITY: 35-year-old Turkish xenolinguist and comparative epigrapher. Foremost
living expert in dead-civilization script systems. First human to set foot on
Biblios-VII, invited by an automated distress-adjacent signal that turned out to be
not a distress call at all, but an open invitation to read.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm, deep-brown skin with realistic visible pore
texture and natural weathering, no airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. A soft,
rounded face with high, gentle cheekbones. Thick, expressive dark eyebrows, often
slightly furrowed in concentration. Warm, deep-set dark eyes with visible catchlight,
faint natural fine lines from decades of close reading work, a habit of narrowing
slightly when focused. A small, straight nose. Full lips, often slightly parted in
concentration, naturally chapped. A small, faded ink-like birthmark near her right
temple. Subtle natural facial asymmetry — a real, thoughtful, human face.

HAIR: Thick, dark curly hair, pulled back into a low, practical twist, a few
realistic loose curls escaping near her temples and forehead, tucked imperfectly
behind her ears where they meet the helmet seal.

BUILD: Average height, 1.63m, a slightly hunched, close-reading posture even while
standing — the physical habit of someone who spends most of her life bent over text.

SUIT: Deep warm-umber and cream field-linguistics survey suit, heavily mission-worn:
- fine mineral dust ground permanently into every seam
- a small hand-drawn glyph, copied from an earlier expedition, sketched in marker
  on the inside of her left cuff, visible through the fabric
- a soft, worn patch reading (in her own hand, faded) her expedition's informal
  nickname for the mission
- scuffed knee padding from years of kneeling before inscribed surfaces
- only faint, aged warm-gold accent lines (echoing the planet's photoluminescent
  script) rather than bright glowing lines
- a fully transparent, seamless helmet always revealing her complete face, with
  faint breath-condensation near the seal, and a small crack (sealed, harmless) in
  the lower-left corner of the visor from an old fall
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY — no handheld tools, styluses, or scanners. All readings,
  translations, and script analysis appear on a single deeply-worn wrist display on
  her left forearm, its screen displaying warm amber-gold translated glyphs as she
  reads.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: A reader's stillness — she moves slowly, often stopping
mid-stride entirely for many seconds at a time when something catches her attention,
completely absorbed. Frequently crouches or kneels to bring her face close to a
surface without ever touching the inscribed stone directly (protective instinct,
professional habit). Occasionally mouths words silently as she reads, visible
through the visor.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — RESEARCH VESSEL _KÜTÜPHANE_

```
HULL LENGTH: 340 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A linguistics and archival survey vessel — quieter, more contemplative
in design than a combat or mining ship, with an unusually large sensor array section
dedicated to long-range script-imaging rather than weapons or heavy cargo.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a broad, flattened forward sensor section, 120 meters, dominated by a large,
  gently curved imaging dish, 40 meters in diameter, angled forward
- a slender mid-section housing crew quarters and the translation archive lab, 140
  meters, with rows of small observation ports, each approximately 1.3 meters in
  diameter, running its length as a scale ruler
- a compact aft engine section, 80 meters, housing two engine clusters, each 8
  meters in diameter
- four slender landing struts, 15 meters tall, ending in wide, flat 3-meter pads
  designed to rest without damaging inscribed bedrock
- one forward observation window, 2 meters high by 4.5 meters wide, positioned
  beneath the sensor dish

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 15m struts against
a visible expanse of inscribed bedrock (whose glyph-bands provide a natural scale
texture), the 2m observation window with a human figure inside at correct
proportion, Nehir's 1.63m figure against the hull, or the ship silhouetted against
the 800-meter Index Spire in the background for direct scale contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective, correct
engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must never appear
miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 340m hull
in view AND (b) a legible human face. Always split into separate shots (A = wide
hull shot, B = medium window/character shot).
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / HORIZON-SCALE shots → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / EMOTIONAL / CHARACTER shots → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (glyph and script detail) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in, pull-back, tilt-up,
tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow crane-rise (reserved for Scene 0 hook).

FRAMING RULE: no contradictory framing in one prompt (never "show the entire ship"
and "push toward her face" together — see Shot-Split Rule).
```

---

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE

```
PRIMARY SOURCES (named explicitly, never just "cinematic lighting"):
- hard, even, golden-white sunlight key light, raking across inscribed surfaces to
  reveal micro-relief script
- soft, warm inner photoluminescent glow from the stone itself, most visible in
  shadow, independent of the sun
- warm amber wrist-display and suit-seam glow
- deep charcoal-black shadow within canyon interiors and deeply-carved glyph
  recesses
- soft silver-white reflection in the still data-pools, doubling the inscribed
  surface above

COLOR GRADE (LUT LANGUAGE): hard golden-white key light / warm inner stone-glow /
deep charcoal-black glyph-shadow / warm amber wrist-display glow / soft silver-white
pool reflection.
```

## 🏃 MOTION BIBLE (Micro-Behavior Catalog)

```
Every shot includes at least one of the following, drawn from this fixed catalog:
- crouching or kneeling to bring her face close to inscribed stone, never touching it
- silently mouthing words as she reads, visible through the visor
- a long, complete stillness mid-stride when something catches her attention
- fine dust settling straight down after a footstep
- fogging breath at the visor seal, clearing after a beat
- fingers hovering just above a glyph surface, tracing its shape in the air without
  contact
```

---

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Standard-feeling gravity (approx. 0.95g).
- Every inscribed surface shown must display genuinely fractal detail: visible from
  a distance as large abstract patterns, resolving at walking distance into
  repeating glyph-clusters, and at close range into fine sub-script within each
  glyph — this fractal consistency is a non-negotiable visual rule of the film.
- The stone's photoluminescent glow is always present but subtle, brightening
  slightly in shadow and never overpowering the golden-white sunlight in direct
  light.
- Human-to-environment scale is always internally consistent: Nehir is 1.63m; the
  ship is 340m; landing struts are 15m; the Index Spire is 800m tall.
```

---

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts. No shaky camera. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look. No airbrushed or
plastic skin. No perfect symmetry. No oversaturated colors. No random/meaningless
scratches standing in for script (all inscribed surfaces must read as a genuine,
consistent, fractal glyph-system). No horror-movie eeriness or hostile atmosphere —
tone is reverent, solemn, library-like, never threatening. No handheld tools in her
hands. No text (English/Latin) overlay in the image itself. No watermark.
```

## SAHNE 0 — HOOK (DETAYLI, SPESİFİK VERSİYON): "BİZİ HATIRLA"

_"Kaşif kayalara baktığında desenlerin rastgele olmadığını anlıyor. Hepsi bir dil. Ve başladığı cümle: 'Bizi hatırla.'"_

### 📸 IMAGE PROMPT

```
SCENE:
Dr. Nehir Uluç crouches before a single, modest, waist-high inscribed boulder —
seemingly one small, unremarkable carved rock among many scattered across a flat
plain — running her eyes across its surface with growing, specific recognition.

ENVIRONMENT:
[WORLD BIBLE] A flat plain scattered with dozens of similar-looking inscribed
boulders, each covered in dense, geometric glyph-clusters, faint warm inner glow
visible in their shadowed undersides. Hard golden-white sunlight rakes low across
the plain, throwing every glyph into sharp relief.

SUBJECT:
[CHARACTER BIBLE] Nehir crouches directly before one specific boulder, her head
tilting slowly, eyes moving left to right across its surface exactly as if reading
a physical page. Her lips move silently. Her wrist display begins to populate, glyph
by glyph, with a soft amber translation glow — the first few resolved symbols visibly
forming recognizable shapes: not abstract marks, but clearly a structured sentence,
its first two words already legible on her display: "REMEMBER US."

CAMERA:
32mm Cooke anamorphic, f/5.6, ISO 400, camera positioned approximately 3 meters away
at her eye level, framing her face, the boulder, and her wrist display together in
one tight, intimate composition — deliberately NOT yet revealing the wider plain.
Physically accurate perspective.

LIGHTING:
[LIGHTING BIBLE] Hard golden-white sunlight raking directly across the boulder's
inscribed face, throwing individual glyphs into crisp, readable micro-relief, warm
amber glow from her wrist display lighting the underside of her jaw.

PHYSICS:
The boulder's glyph pattern is visibly fractal even at this close range — small
sub-glyphs nested within each larger symbol, exactly consistent with the World
Bible's rule.

NEGATIVE:
[NEGATIVE BIBLE — full list applies; specifically no random meaningless scratches,
this must read as genuine language]
```

### 🎥 VIDEO PROMPT

```
MOTION (9 seconds, 32mm Cooke anamorphic, slow crane-rise beginning at 3 meters):
0s — Nehir's eyes move slowly across the boulder's surface, lips moving silently,
her wrist display resolving the first two words: "REMEMBER US" — glowing softly in
amber.
2s — She goes suddenly, completely still, recognition washing over her expression —
this is not random erosion, this is language, and it is addressed to whoever finds it.
4s — The camera begins a slow, continuous crane-rise and pull-back, widening the
frame beyond the single boulder for the first time.
6s — As the frame widens, dozens more boulders come into view, each covered in the
same dense script — and beyond them, the true scale becomes apparent: the entire
visible plain to the horizon is inscribed bedrock, one continuous, unbroken text
stretching in every direction, of which her single boulder was only the very first
sentence.
8s — Nehir slowly rises to her feet, turning her head along the horizon, overwhelmed.
9s — She stands motionless, small against the vast inscribed world. Hard cut to
Scene 1A.
[PHYSICS + NEGATIVE bibles apply throughout]
```
