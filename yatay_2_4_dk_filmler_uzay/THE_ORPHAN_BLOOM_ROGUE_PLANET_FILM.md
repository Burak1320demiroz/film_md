## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: NOCTIS-7 (Rogue Planet)

```
PLANETARY CLASSIFICATION: Rogue planet — a terrestrial world ejected
from its parent star system approximately 2 billion years ago, now
drifting through interstellar space with no host star. Surface
temperature: -220°C at the surface, but the planet retains a molten
core and active geothermal system that sustains subsurface liquid water
and a thriving chemosynthetic biosphere.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE — TOTAL DARKNESS: Noctis-7 has NO external light
source. There is no sun, no nearby star, no reflected light from a
moon. The surface exists in PERMANENT, ABSOLUTE DARKNESS — the only
natural illumination is: (1) amber-orange geothermal light leaking
upward through fractures in the crust, and (2) blue-green
bioluminescent organisms that thrive around geothermal vents and in
subsurface cave systems. ALL artificial light comes from the explorer's
suit systems (headlamp, chest LEDs, wrist display). Every photon in
every frame must have a TRACEABLE, MOTIVATED SOURCE.

THE SURFACE: Black basalt plains — smooth, cold, ancient, featureless
except for a network of geothermal fractures that split the surface
like the cracked glaze on dark pottery. These fractures range from
hairline cracks (barely visible) to 5-meter-wide chasms that descend
hundreds of meters into the warm subsurface. The fractures glow from
within: amber-orange geothermal light pulses upward from deep below,
creating a network of warm light veins across the otherwise black
terrain. Fine black mineral dust covers all surfaces.

THE FRACTURE SYSTEM: Deep cracks in the planetary crust, widening as
they descend, their walls transitioning from cold black basalt at the
surface to warm, amber-veined igneous rock at depth. Below 20 meters,
the walls are progressively colonized by bioluminescent organisms —
thin films, then thick mats, then dense, three-dimensional growth —
the deeper you go, the more life there is.

THE CATHEDRAL CHAMBER: At approximately 80 meters below the surface,
certain fractures open into massive geode-like caverns — natural
chambers up to 200 meters across, formed by ancient gas pockets in
the cooling crust. Every surface of these chambers is covered in
dense, living bioluminescent growth: cool blue-green dominant, with
patches of pale violet and warm amber. Natural mineral stalactite
formations are draped in bioluminescent organisms that pulse in
slow, synchronized waves. The overall effect: a living cathedral
of biological light in total geological darkness.

THE UNDERGROUND SEA: Beneath the fracture system, a vast subterranean
ocean of geothermally heated water (approximately 18°C, comfortable)
fills the deepest accessible caverns. The water is clear, dark, and
populated by dense clouds of bioluminescent plankton that produce a
shimmering, shifting field of blue-green points of light across the
water surface — creating the illusion of a starfield reflected on
the sea. The plankton respond to vibration: a footstep on the shore
causes a ripple of brightening that propagates outward in concentric
rings.

THE SKY: Because Noctis-7 has no star, no moon, and only a trace
remnant atmosphere (too thin to breathe, but enough to carry sound
faintly), the sky from the surface is the CLEAREST VIEW OF THE
GALAXY possible from any solid ground. The Milky Way stretches across
the entire visible sky in extraordinary detail — individual star
clusters, dust lanes, nebulae — unobstructed by any atmospheric
scattering, light pollution, or stellar glare. The galaxy is visible
but does NOT illuminate the surface — it is too distant and diffuse
to cast usable light. The surface remains dark.

ATMOSPHERE: Trace — approximately 0.02 atm. Not breathable. Thin
enough to be effectively vacuum for light transmission purposes
(hence the crystal-clear galaxy view) but dense enough to carry
faint sound at close range (within 10 meters). The explorer's suit
provides full life support.

GRAVITY: 0.87g — slightly less than Earth. Noticeable in longer
strides, slightly higher jumps, and the gentle arc of kicked dust.
Not dramatic enough to feel "alien" in walking — more like a subtle
lightness.

SOUND: Near-silence on the surface. Faint vibrations carry through
the rock. Underground, the geothermal system produces a continuous,
very low-frequency rumble felt more than heard — a planetary heartbeat.
In the Cathedral Chamber, dripping mineral-rich water creates
intermittent, bell-like tones that echo.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. DEFNE KARA

```

chemosynthetic extremophile organisms — life forms that derive energy
from chemical reactions rather than sunlight. She has spent 12 years
searching for evidence of life on starless worlds. Noctis-7 is the
first rogue planet humanity has ever landed on, and Defne designed
the mission profile herself. She is not a survivor or an accidental
visitor — she is here BY CHOICE, and the discovery of bioluminescent
life in the fractures is the vindication of her life's work.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm olive skin with visible pore
texture, natural subsurface scattering under colored light. A broad,
open face with a high forehead and wide-set dark brown eyes that
reflect every light source with accurate, physics-correct
reflections. Strong, defined eyebrows — the left slightly higher
than the right, a natural asymmetry. A straight nose with a slight
bump on the bridge. Full lips, naturally slightly parted when
concentrating. A small circular birthmark on the right temple, at
the hairline. Faint smile lines at the corners of her eyes — she
is 34 and has laughed often. No airbrushing, no plastic smoothness,
no artificial symmetry.

HAIR: Dark brown, thick, worn in a single low braid that hangs
between her shoulder blades inside the suit. In zero-external-wind
conditions (which is always, on this airless surface), the braid
rests flat against her back. Loose wisps at the temples, visible
through the visor.

BUILD: Average, 1.65m, practical — not athletic, not fragile. A
scientist's body, not a soldier's. She moves with careful precision,
not athletic speed. Every gesture is measured and intentional — the

- base color: deep midnight-blue (nearly black in darkness, reads as
  dark blue when lit by headlamp or bioluminescence)

  fracture descent (Scenes 4-5)
- a small sample collection pouch on the right thigh — sealed,
  midnight-blue, visible in every full-body shot

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every detail listed above remains
IDENTICAL and PERSISTENT across all scenes. Dust accumulation only
INCREASES. The sample pouch begins empty (flat) and becomes visibly
fuller after Scene 6 (when she collects bioluminescent organisms).

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE in every
frame regardless of head angle. Face always readable.

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When touching ANY surface (rock,
cave wall, organisms, water), her hands/fingers maintain COMPLETE
STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY — clear visible boundary at all contact points,

FACE LOCK [LESSON 16]: Face maintains IDENTICAL bone structure, eye
spacing, nose shape, birthmark position, brow asymmetry throughout
ALL scenes.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Slow, deliberate, reverent. She moves like a
person in a museum — careful not to disturb, careful to observe
everything. When she discovers bioluminescent life, her face shows
quiet, profound wonder — not shock, not excitement, but the deep
satisfaction of someone whose hypothesis was correct. Her hand
movements are precise and gentle — she touches surfaces with
individual fingertips, never grabbing or pressing.

## 🦑 CREATURE BIBLE — THE DRIFT (Sürüklenme)

```
SPECIES: A large (2.5 meters long, approximately 0.8 meters tall at
the widest), slow-moving, eyeless cave-dwelling organism native to
Noctis-7's subsurface ecosystem. Called a Drift because it moves
across cave surfaces with an imperceptible, glacial slowness — more
like a flowing liquid than a walking animal.

APPEARANCE: A broad, flat, soft-bodied organism — imagine a giant
deep-sea flatworm crossed with a translucent salamander. Four wide,
flat limbs that spread against cave surfaces like suction pads,
propelling the body forward in a slow, rippling wave-motion.
Completely EYELESS — the head end is a smooth, rounded, featureless
dome with a ring of short, delicate sensory tendrils (each 10cm
long) that detect vibration, heat, and chemical gradients. The body
is TRANSLUCENT — its internal organs are visible through its skin
as dark, shadowy structures, and scattered throughout its body are
dozens of bioluminescent organs (photophores) that pulse in slow,
rhythmic waves from head to tail, each pulse taking approximately
4 seconds to travel the full body length. Color: the photophores
glow cool blue-green, identical to the cave wall organisms (they
are part of the same ecosystem). The body surface has a faint,
wet sheen — not slimy, but moist.

BEHAVIOR: Thermotropic — the Drift is attracted to heat sources.
It has never encountered a warm-blooded organism or an artificial
heat source before. When it detects Defne's body heat (radiated
through her suit), it slowly redirects its path toward her. Its
approach is not predatory — it is investigative. It moves toward
the warmth the way a plant grows toward light: automatically,
without intention or threat. It stops approximately 1 meter from
her and its photophores pulse slightly brighter and faster in
response to her proximity. It does NOT touch her. After
approximately 30 seconds of proximity, it slowly turns and resumes
its original path.

FIXED ANATOMY [LESSON 2]: Rigid, locked body plan — four limbs,
one featureless head dome, one tapered tail, dorsal photophore
line. No shape-shifting, no morphing, no anatomy drift. Background
rendering uses shallow DOF.

CREATURE LOCOMOTION [LESSON 18]: The Drift does NOT slide across
surfaces. Its four broad limbs execute a slow, visible, sequential
wave-motion: each limb lifts slightly, extends forward, and
re-adheres to the surface in sequence (1-2-3-4, front-left, front-
right, rear-left, rear-right), creating a smooth but clearly
ARTICULATED ripple of movement. The body undulates gently with each
cycle. It moves at approximately 0.1 meters per second — glacially
slow.
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE (motivated sources only)

```
THIS FILM'S LIGHTING PHILOSOPHY: Every photon has a source. There is
no ambient fill light, no "cinematic lighting from nowhere." The
darkness is REAL. Only three light families exist in this film:

LIGHT FAMILY 1 — ARTIFICIAL (Cool White, 5500K):
- Defne's headlamp: narrow 15-degree cone, cool white, sharp beam
  with visible falloff into darkness. This is the PRIMARY light source
  in most scenes.
- Defne's chest LEDs: soft, diffused, cool white fill — illuminates
  close objects she faces directly.
- Derinlik's landing lights: powerful, wide, cool white floods —
  visible in surface scenes as the bright circle around the ship.

LIGHT FAMILY 2 — GEOTHERMAL (Warm Amber-Orange, 2200K):
- Fracture glow: warm amber-orange light pulsing upward from deep
  within the planet's crust fractures. Directional (upward only).
  Creates dramatic under-lighting on faces and surfaces near fracture
  edges.
- Deep cave warmth: as the explorer descends, the ambient rock
  temperature increases, and deeper walls emit a faint, warm amber
  glow from mineral inclusions.

LIGHT FAMILY 3 — BIOLUMINESCENCE (Cool Blue-Green, varies):
- Cave wall organisms: cool blue-green (dominant), pale violet
  (patches), warm amber (rare). Sharp, directional, clear falloff.
  NOT soft bloom.
- Creature photophores: cool blue-green, pulsing rhythmically.
- Underground sea plankton: cool blue-green pinpoints, thousands of
  them, scattered across the water surface.

PHASE 1 — SPACE + SURFACE (Scenes 0-3):
Dominant light: ship's landing lights (cool white) + headlamp
(cool white). Secondary: geothermal fracture glow (amber-orange)
from ground cracks. The sky contains the Milky Way but it does NOT
illuminate the surface — it is visual only. Surface scenes are
dominated by the harsh contrast between the headlamp beam and total
surrounding darkness.

PHASE 2 — DESCENT + CAVES (Scenes 4-7):
Dominant light shifts from artificial (headlamp) to natural
(bioluminescence). The deeper Defne descends, the more bioluminescent
organisms appear, progressively replacing her headlamp as the primary
light source. By the Cathedral Chamber (Scene 6), bioluminescence is
dominant and the headlamp becomes secondary fill. Color temperature
transitions from cool white artificial to cool blue-green biological.

PHASE 3 — UNDERGROUND SEA + RETURN (Scenes 8-10):
The underground sea is lit almost entirely by bioluminescent plankton
— a shimmering field of blue-green light from below. Defne's headlamp
provides the only cool white accent. Upon surface return (Scene 10),
lighting reverts to headlamp + fracture glow, but the galaxy is now
the emotional light source (visible but not illuminating).

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON 3]: ALL bioluminescent sources are
SHARP, DIRECTIONAL, and HIGH-CONTRAST. They cast defined shadows
and have clear falloff boundaries. Never soft, never diffused, never
dreamy bloom. Skin retains visible pore texture under every light
condition. The darkness STAYS dark — bioluminescence does not wash
out the frame.

MOTIVATED LIGHTING RULE: Every light source in every frame must have
a VISIBLE, LOGICAL ORIGIN — the headlamp, the chest LEDs, a fracture
below, organisms on a wall, plankton in water. NO light appears from
nowhere. If a surface is lit, the source must be identifiable.

COLOR TEMPERATURE NARRATIVE: The film's emotional arc is told through
color temperature shift:
- Scenes 0-3: Cool white dominance (artificial, clinical, isolated)
- Scenes 4-6: Cool white + blue-green mixing (discovery, transition)
- Scenes 6-9: Blue-green dominance (natural, organic, alive)
- Scene 10: Cool white + amber below + galaxy above (synthesis, peace)
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 0.87g. Noticeable as slightly longer strides, a touch more
  float at the top of each step, dust that settles slightly slower
  than Earth-normal. Not dramatic — subtle.

- DARKNESS RULE: The surface of Noctis-7 is in PERMANENT TOTAL
  DARKNESS. There is no ambient light, no sky glow, no horizon glow.
  Beyond the reach of artificial lights and geothermal fractures,
  there is NOTHING — pure black. The headlamp beam has a visible,
  sharp boundary where illumination ends and darkness begins. Objects
  beyond this boundary are invisible.

- GEOTHERMAL FRACTURE PHYSICS: Fracture light is UPWARD-DIRECTIONAL
  ONLY — it comes from below, illuminating from underneath. It creates
  dramatic under-lighting on faces and objects near fracture edges.
  The light pulses slowly (approximately one cycle per 8 seconds) as
  geothermal convection currents shift deep below. Warm air (visible
  as faint heat shimmer) rises from wider fractures.

- BIOLUMINESCENCE PHYSICS: Organisms emit sharp, directional light
  from specialized photophore organs. The light has clear falloff —
  it illuminates surfaces within approximately 2 meters of the source
  organism and falls to darkness beyond. When touched, organisms
  pulse BRIGHTER at the contact point and the brightening propagates
  outward from the touch at approximately 0.5 meters per second —
  a visible wave of increased luminosity spreading across the wall.
  This is a biological defense/communication response.

- UNDERGROUND SEA PHYSICS: The water is warm (18°C), clear, dark,
  with no current (geothermally heated, enclosed). Plankton float
  at the surface and respond to vibration — a footstep on the rocky
  shore causes a CONCENTRIC RING of brightened plankton to propagate
  outward from the vibration point at approximately 1 meter per
  second. The water has normal Earth-like surface tension and behavior.
  [DYNAMIC WATER RULE: water shows continuous micro-motion, realistic
  surface tension, accurate wakes and ripples. NEVER frozen, static,
  or gel-like.]

- COLLISION INTEGRITY [LESSON 17]: ALL objects obey rigid-body
  collision. Boots press against rock. Hands touch walls with clear
  boundaries. Nothing phases through any solid.

- VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: Dust kicked up by footsteps
  disperses with 0.87g — slightly slower than Earth, with wider
  dispersal arcs. Heat shimmer from fractures is volumetric and
  physically accurate.

- Human-to-environment scale: Dr. Kara 1.65m; Derinlik lander 18m
  long; surface fractures 0.5-5m wide; Cathedral Chamber 200m across;
  The Drift 2.5m long; underground sea extends beyond visibility.

- GRAVITY VECTOR [LESSON 15]: Hair, loose suit elements, dust, and
  water all respond to consistent 0.87g downward gravity. The braid
  hangs correctly. Dust settles at the correct rate.

- ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: Rock formations, cave walls, and
  geological features remain STATIC. They do not breathe, warp, or
  morph. Only biological organisms and water show movement.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```

montage, no editing, no multiple camera angles, no scene changes, no
camera teleportation. No shaky camera, no handheld look, no video game

aesthetic, no video game aesthetic. No airbrushed or plastic skin. No
unnatural perfect facial symmetry. No oversaturated colors.

No bloom, no glow filter, no soft focus glow on ANY light source —
bioluminescence is SHARP and DIRECTIONAL with clear falloff, never
dreamy, never washing out the frame. The darkness must STAY dark —
bioluminescent sources do not fill the entire frame with ambient glow.

No ambient fill light from unknown sources — every photon has a
motivated origin (headlamp, chest LEDs, fracture, organisms, plankton,
landing lights). No "cinematic lighting from nowhere."

No text, digits, letters, or symbols on ANY display or surface —
abstract pulsing light readouts only. No creature shape-shifting or
morphing. No floating or sliding movement — all locomotion is
articulated. No hand-surface merging. No terrain warping. No frozen
particles. No static dust. No face morphing. No extra limbs. No
rubber-banding. No jello limbs. No disconnected joints.

No horror tone, no jump scares, no threatening atmosphere. This is
DISCOVERY and AWE, not survival or fear. The Drift is curious and
gentle, never predatory.
