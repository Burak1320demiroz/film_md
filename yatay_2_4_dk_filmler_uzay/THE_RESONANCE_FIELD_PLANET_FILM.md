## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: SFORZA

```
MINERAL PHYSICS: A near-airless, dry desert world whose entire surface regolith is
composed of a naturally hyper-piezoelectric silicate compound. This is real physics
taken to planetary extreme — the same principle behind quartz piezoelectricity and
Chladni-plate cymatics (sound organizing loose particles into geometric standing-wave
patterns), except here the mineral lattice doesn't just arrange loose sand into flat
patterns — it locks, fuses, and rises into genuine three-dimensional crystalline
structures within seconds of sustained acoustic or seismic vibration, then slowly
relaxes back into fine sand over several minutes once the vibration source stops.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: Structure size, complexity, and geometric symmetry scale directly
and physically with the amplitude, duration, and purity of the triggering sound —
a short handclap yields a small, simple hexagonal spike; a sustained, held tone yields
a tall, branching, fractal cathedral-like formation. Nothing here is magic — it is
applied cymatics and piezoelectricity taken to its logical, awesome extreme.

THE ANCIENT SPIRE FIELD: Scattered across Sforza's central plain stand colossal,
permanently fused crystal spires — some over 100 meters tall — remnants of ancient,
unexplained resonance events far larger than anything a human voice or engine could
produce. They never dissolved. Their origin is unknown and never explained in the
film; they exist purely as a scale-and-mystery backdrop.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: 0.92g, an extremely thin, nearly windless atmosphere — Sforza's
default acoustic state is near-total silence, which is precisely what makes any sound
here dramatically and physically significant.

SKY AS CHARACTER: A hazy violet-gold sky, tinted by a permanent high-altitude layer
of fine suspended crystal dust that refracts sunlight into soft prismatic bands. A
single small, fast-orbiting moon (PIANO) crosses the sky visibly within the span of
the film, pale grey-lavender, contributing a faint, constant background hum too low
for the human ear but felt as a subtle vibration underfoot.

SOUND: Default state is near-total silence — no wind, no ambient hum beyond the
faintest sub-audible moon-hum. Against this silence: the formation of crystal reads
as a rising, resonant chime-groan; dissolution reads as a soft, dry sand-whisper.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. ADA YILDIRIM

```
IDENTITY: 30-year-old Turkish acoustic geophysicist and resonance cartographer. First
human to deliberately trigger and map a controlled cymatic bloom on Sforza. Weeks into
a solo mapping expedition of the Ancient Spire Field.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm tan-olive skin with real, visible pore
texture, faint sun-freckling across the nose and cheekbones, no airbrushing, no
plastic smoothness. An angular, alert face with high cheekbones and a slightly
crooked nose (an old childhood break, barely visible). Dark brown eyes, wide and
attentive, with a habitual narrowing when listening intently. Naturally uneven
eyebrows, a thin white scar through the left brow. A small gap between her front
teeth, visible when she speaks. Subtle natural facial asymmetry — a real, alert,
capable human face, not a rendered one.

HAIR: Black, cropped short and practical, just past the ears, tucked behind a thin
suit-integrated ear-seal, a few loose strands catching the dust-light.

BUILD: Lean, wiry, 1.72m, a runner's build — long-limbed, economical, built for
sustained movement across open ground rather than raw strength.

SUIT: Matte charcoal-grey and pale bone-white acoustic-survey suit, heavily
mission-worn:
- a low, close-fitting suit-integrated throat-and-chest collar — her only tool,
  never a handheld device — capable of projecting a single sustained, controlled
  tone outward from a small forward-facing resonator plate at her collarbone
- fine, pale crystalline dust ground into every seam, cuff, and knee joint
- worn, matte-finish boot soles with a soft, wide, sound-dampened tread
- a short coil of unlit, aged pale violet accent piping along the suit's seams,
  never bright or glowing
- a fully transparent, seamless visor always revealing her complete face, a thin
  layer of fine dust often visible on the outer glass
- a small, hand-frayed strip of red fabric tied around her right wrist — a personal
  talisman, not equipment
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY OF HANDHELD TOOLS — no wands, scanners, or microphones. All
  readings come from a single scratched, well-used wrist display on her left
  forearm. Sound is projected only through the suit-integrated chest collar,
  activated by a small stud she touches at her sternum — never separately held.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: A field scientist's controlled stillness broken by sudden bursts
of motion — she moves in careful, deliberate steps across untested ground, pauses
often to listen with a slight head-tilt, and has a habit of touching two fingers to
her sternum stud before activating any sound, as if bracing herself.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — SURVEY VESSEL _YANKI_

```
HULL LENGTH: 210 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A dedicated acoustic-survey vessel built for silent operation —
dampened, low-vibration repulsor engines and a matte, sound-absorbing hull coating,
designed to land and operate on Sforza's surface without triggering unwanted
large-scale resonance blooms.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a long, low central hull, 95 meters, matte charcoal-grey, non-reflective, housing
  crew quarters and the acoustic-mapping lab, with a single row of small reinforced
  observation ports, each approximately 1.2 meters in diameter, running its length
  as a scale ruler
- two dampened repulsor/engine pods, each 7 meters in diameter, mounted low at the
  stern, designed to fire with minimal audible or seismic signature
- four wide, silent-tread landing struts, 14 meters long each, fitted with broad,
  sound-dampening padded feet to avoid triggering ground resonance on touchdown
- one forward observation window, 2 meters high by 3.6 meters wide, positioned at
  the hull's bow

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 1.2m observation
ports as a scale ruler along the hull, the 2m observation window with Ada visible
inside at correct proportion, Ada's 1.72m figure beside a landing strut, or the hull
silhouetted directly against one of the 100m+ ancient spires for direct scale
contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective, correct
engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must never appear
miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 210m hull
in view AND (b) a legible human face. Across the film, ship-scale shots and
character-scale shots are always kept as separate, dedicated scenes.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / SPIRE-FIELD-SCALE shots → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / EMOTIONAL / CHARACTER shots → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (crystal-growth texture, dust, fracture detail) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in, pull-back, tilt-up,
tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow lateral track (reserved for the running
sequence, Scenes 7-8).

FRAMING RULE: no contradictory framing in one prompt (never "show the entire ship"
and "push toward her face" together — see Shot-Split Rule).

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: every video prompt is exactly 10 seconds, single continuous shot,
no cuts.
```

## 🏃 MOTION BIBLE (Micro-Behavior Catalog)

```
Every shot includes at least one of the following, drawn from this fixed catalog:
- two fingers touching the sternum stud for a beat before activating a sound,
  bracing herself
- a slight head-tilt, listening posture, testing the silence before moving
- fine crystal dust settling and catching light around her boots with each step
- a cautious, delayed weight-shift when stepping onto ground she hasn't tested
- fine dust residue on her visor, wiped away with a slow glove pass
- a full-body flinch or held breath at a sudden distant boom
- a controlled, measured exhale before a demanding sprint
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 0.92g fixed throughout.
- Crystal formation is near-instantaneous (2-4 seconds) once a sound reaches
  sufficient amplitude/duration, and scales directly and physically with that
  sound's power and purity — bigger, sustained, purer tones produce taller, more
  complex, more symmetrical branching structures; short or weak sounds produce
  small, simple spikes.
- Formed crystal structures are genuinely solid and load-bearing the instant they
  finish forming — never translucent illusion, never CGI sparkle.
- Dissolution back to fine sand is gradual, taking several minutes, never instant.
- The Ancient Spire Field's colossal structures (60-120m) are permanently fused and
  never dissolve — their scale implies an original resonance event far beyond
  anything depicted in the film.
- Human-to-environment scale is always internally consistent: Ada is 1.72m; the
  ship is 210m; the ancient spires range 60-120m; freshly bloomed crystal during the
  story ranges from 2m (small, deliberate blooms) up to 40m (the climactic chain
  reaction).
- Default ambient state is near-total silence and stillness; nothing moves or
  glows without a specific triggering sound or seismic event.
```

---

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts. No shaky camera. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look. No airbrushed or
plastic skin. No unnatural perfect facial symmetry (crystal geometric symmetry is
physically intended and exempt from this rule). No oversaturated colors. No magic
sparkle, fairy-dust, or glitter FX on crystal growth — light must read as physically
refracted within the lattice, never as an external glow layer. No instant,
unmotivated crystal growth without a visible or implied triggering sound. No
handheld tools in her hands (the resonator is suit-integrated, never a separate
device). No text. No watermark.
```
