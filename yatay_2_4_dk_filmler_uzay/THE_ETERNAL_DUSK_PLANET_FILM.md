## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: MERIDIAN VE "THE DUSK BELT"

```
PLANETARY MECHANICS: Meridian is a tidally-locked terrestrial world orbiting a dim,
cool red-dwarf star at extremely close range — one face permanently turned toward
the star, one face permanently turned away. This is a real, well-understood
astrophysical configuration (the same locking mechanism as Earth's Moon), taken to
its logical planetary extreme rather than invented physics.

DAY FACE ("YANIK YÜZ"): A permanent, unbroken desert of fused silicate glass and
bare black rock, baked for uncounted eons under a fixed, unmoving red-dwarf sun
directly overhead. Surface temperatures make this hemisphere entirely uninhabitable.
Never seen at ground level in this film — referenced only as the source of the
belt's warm-side wind and light.

NIGHT FACE ("KARANLIK YÜZ"): A permanent, unbroken field of frozen methane and
water-ice glaciers, kilometers thick in places, under a starfield sky that never
changes. Surface temperatures make this hemisphere equally uninhabitable. Never
seen at ground level in this film — referenced only as the source of the belt's
cold-side wind and the visible glacier line at the belt's outer edge.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE — THE DUSK BELT: The only habitable region on Meridian is a
narrow, ring-shaped strip — approximately 140 kilometers wide — that circles the
entire planet along the exact day/night terminator line. Within this belt, and
ONLY within this belt, the star sits fixed at the horizon forever: neither rising
nor setting, neither morning nor evening, an eternal frozen instant of dusk. Every
location within the belt experiences the SAME light, the SAME temperature, and the
SAME sky, unchanging, for as long as the planet has existed.

THE FIXED SUN: A large, dim, deep red-orange disk, sitting motionless exactly on
the horizon line at all times, ringed by a permanent atmospheric refraction halo —
a soft double-ring of amber and rose light bent around the disk by the thick,
still, ever-present terminator-line atmosphere. It never moves within a single
shot, within a single scene, or across the entire film. This is a deliberate,
central visual rule of this film, the opposite of a shifting-light world — the
light here is fixed, permanent, uncanny in its total stillness.

THE SPLIT GROUND: Because the terminator line runs directly through the belt, any
single ridge, boulder, or tree can show ONE physical face lit warm and dry by the
fixed sun and the OPPOSITE face frosted, rimed, and dusted with fine ice crystal —
in the same shot, on the same object, permanently. This split-face condition
applies to every solid surface in the belt without exception.

VEGETATION — GLASS-LEAF FOREST: A forest of rigid, black silicate-glass trunked
trees, each broad leaf-frond fused flat and permanently canted toward the fixed
sun, functioning as a living solar collector. Sun-facing frond surfaces glow a
faint, warm amber from stored light; shaded undersides show a cool, frost-rimed
blue-white. No frond ever moves or reorients — they have had no reason to move in
millions of years.

WIND: A single, constant, unchanging planetary wind flows always in the same
direction across the belt — thermal convection driven eternally from the hot day
face toward the cold night face. It never gusts, never reverses, never pauses.

SOUND: Near-total stillness at ground level — no dawn chorus, no dusk chorus,
because there is no dawn and no dusk to sing for. The only sounds: the constant,
low, steady wind; the occasional deep groan of glacier ice at the belt's cold edge;
and, very rarely, a slow, massive, almost sub-audible breathing rhythm from
beneath the ground itself — unexplained until Scene 8.

FROM ORBIT: Seen from space, Meridian displays a stark, continuous divide — one
hemisphere a dull, scorched red-black, the other a pale, frozen blue-white — with
a single, thin, luminous ring of amber-gold light tracing the exact boundary
between them: the Dusk Belt, glowing faintly even from orbital distance, the only
living seam on an otherwise dead world.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. SEHER AYDIN

```
IDENTITY: 31-year-old Turkish planetary climatologist and tidal-lock specialist.
Came to Meridian to take the first direct ground-level readings of a true, fixed
terminator zone — a phenomenon long theorized but never confirmed until Meridian's
Dusk Belt was found intact and stable.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm olive skin with realistic visible pore
texture and natural subsurface scattering, deliberately lit asymmetrically by the
belt's permanent split light — one side of her face always warmer and more golden,
the other always cooler and bluer, regardless of head angle. No airbrushing, no
plastic smoothness. A long, angular face with a strong, straight nose and high
cheekbones. Dark, level eyebrows. Deep brown eyes with visible catchlight, faint
crow's-feet from years of squinting at instrument readouts in bright field
conditions. A thin, pale scar across the bridge of her nose from an old
equipment accident. Naturally chapped lips. A real, weathered, quietly intense
human face.

HAIR: Black, straight, cut short to the jaw, tucked entirely behind the ears and
into the helmet seal — practical, low-maintenance, chosen specifically for a suit
that must seal cleanly on both sides of her head.

BUILD: Average height, 1.70m, wiry and economical in movement, the deliberate,
measured gait of someone used to conserving energy in extreme environments.

SUIT — THE DUALITY SUIT (its defining, unchanging design, mirroring the world):
- LEFT HALF (permanently the sun-facing half in every shot): matte, dark
  charcoal-black insulated material, heat-reflective micro-ridging visible on
  close inspection, a small sun-angle sensor on the left shoulder
- RIGHT HALF (permanently the night-facing half in every shot): pale, reflective
  silver-white insulated material, fine frost-resistant coating visible on close
  inspection, a small cold-angle sensor on the right shoulder
- both halves meet in a visible, reinforced center seam running down the spine,
  chest, and the center of the helmet itself
- a single wrist display on her left forearm only, its screen showing two simple
  abstract readouts side by side — a warm amber bar and a cool blue bar, never
  rendered text or numbers
- HANDS: left glove matte black (for touching sun-warmed surfaces), right glove
  pale reflective white (for touching frost-rimed surfaces) — she consistently
  and visibly chooses the correct hand for the correct surface
- scuffed matte-black boot sole on the left foot, frost-pale boot sole on the
  right foot
- a fully transparent, seamless helmet, split down its own center by a single
  fine structural seam matching the suit's halves, always revealing her complete
  face, with visible warm-side condensation and cool-side faint frost crystal
  forming near the respective sides of the seal

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: A habitual pause with one foot planted on each side of any
visible ground-level split-line, weight balanced evenly, before continuing.
Switches gloved hands deliberately and visibly before touching any new surface,
based on which side of the terminator that surface sits on. Tilts her head only
slightly, never fully, when studying the fixed sun — a lifetime spent adjusting
to a horizon that never moves.
```

## 🦋 CREATURE BIBLE

```
SINIR KELEBEKLERİ / BORDER MOTHS: Wingspan approximately 40 centimeters, moving in
loose, drifting swarms back and forth across the terminator line and nowhere else
— thermal-gradient feeders, drawn to the exact boundary between hot and cold air.
Each wing is split down its own center line exactly like the World Bible's split-
ground rule: one wing-half matte, heat-dark, faintly warm; the other pale, frost-
white, faintly cold. FIXED ANATOMY: six thin, articulated legs, one pair of
antennae, wing shape and split pattern locked and identical from first frame to
last — no morphing, no shape drift, no anatomical change regardless of flight
angle.

ALACA YÜRÜYÜCÜLER / TWILIGHT STRIDERS: Large, slow, six-legged grazing animals,
approximately 4 meters at the shoulder, moving with a deliberate, weight-bearing
gait — each of the six legs lifts, swings, and plants in a fixed, repeating
sequence, never sliding, never gliding. Their armored hide follows the same split
rule as the ground itself: sun-side plating a dark, sun-warmed mineral black;
night-side plating a pale, frost-crusted white, the two halves meeting in a
visible ridge line down the spine. They graze exclusively on glass-leaf fronds,
their heads turning between light and shade with slow, deliberate motion. Fixed,
rigid anatomy — six legs, one long low head, one dorsal ridge line — never
varying shot to shot.

THE MERIDIAN TITAN (final reveal, Scenes 8-8.5 only): A colossal, ancient, dormant
creature, its exposed dorsal ridge over 300 meters long, indistinguishable from a
natural rock-and-ice ridgeline until Scene 8. Like every other surface on
Meridian, its body follows the split rule absolutely: the sun-facing flank is
dark, sun-baked mineral hide; the night-facing flank is pale, glacier-crusted
hide; the two halves meet along its dorsal spine — the very ridge Dr. Aydın has
been walking on since Scene 3. It has stood motionless, straddling the
terminator, for a span of time longer than any recorded history on this world,
its impossibly slow breathing the sub-audible rhythm noted in the World Bible's
SOUND section since Scene 0. FIXED ANATOMY RULE: even mid-movement in Scene 8.5,
its silhouette, plating pattern, and proportions must remain internally
consistent and rigid — this is slow, monumental, geological-scale motion, never
soft-body deformation or morphing.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — SURVEY VESSEL _TERAZİ_ (THE SCALE)

```
HULL LENGTH: 210 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a
visible reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A lightweight atmospheric-entry and long-duration field survey
vessel, named for its dual-sided sensor array — one bank of sensors permanently
oriented toward the day face, one toward the night face, constantly comparing
both hemispheres' readings, the ship's own instrumentation mirroring the world's
central duality.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a slender forward crew section, 70 meters, with a low, wide observation window
- a broad mid-section, 100 meters, carrying two external sensor pods, 6 meters
  each — one matte-dark (day-facing sensor bank), one pale-reflective (night-
  facing sensor bank) — plus rows of small observation ports, each approximately
  1 meter in diameter, running its length as a scale ruler
- a compact aft engine section, 40 meters, housing two engine clusters, each 5
  meters in diameter
- four slender landing legs, 12 meters tall, ending in wide, flat 2.5-meter pads
  designed to rest evenly across a split ground surface without tilting
- one forward observation window, 1.8 meters high by 3.6 meters wide

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 12m landing legs
against the belt's split ground line, the 1.8m window with Dr. Aydın's figure
inside at correct proportion, her 1.70m figure against the hull, or the ship's
210m length directly compared against the visible Meridian Titan ridgeline in
Scenes 8 through 10.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective,
correct engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must
never appear miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 210m
hull in view AND (b) a legible human face. Always split into separate shots (A =
wide hull shot, B = medium window/character shot).
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / ORBITAL-PLANET shots → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / EMOTIONAL / CHARACTER shots → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (frost crystal, glass-leaf, moth-wing detail) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance explicitly, in meters — or,
for Scene 0's orbital-to-surface shot, in kilometers transitioning to meters.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in, pull-back, THE
DESCENT (this film's signature move — a continuous, unbroken push from orbital
distance down through atmosphere to ground level, reserved for Scene 0's hook and
echoed in reverse for Scene 10B's closing ascent), slow orbit, fixed lockoff,
lateral track.

FRAMING RULE: no contradictory framing in one prompt (never "show the full
planet from orbit" and "show her facial expression" together — see Shot-Split
Rule).
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE

```
PRIMARY SOURCES (named explicitly, never just "cinematic lighting"):
- the fixed, dim red-orange disk of Meridian's star, sitting motionless exactly
  on the horizon in every ground-level shot, ringed by a soft double-halo of
  amber and rose refracted light
- warm, flat, unchanging amber-red key light on every sun-facing surface
- cool, flat, unchanging blue-violet fill light on every night-facing surface,
  supplemented by dense, unmoving starlight and a faint, constant aurora glow
- warm amber suit-glow (left/day half) and cool blue-white suit-glow (right/
  night half) from Dr. Aydın's Duality Suit

COLOR GRADE (LUT LANGUAGE): flat warm amber-red day-side key / flat cool blue-
violet night-side fill / soft rose-amber horizon halo / warm amber suit glow
(left) / cool blue-white suit glow (right) / dense fixed starfield.

FIXED LIGHT RULE (central, non-negotiable directive of this film): light
direction, light color, and light intensity must remain COMPLETELY CONSTANT
across every single frame of every shot and across the entire film. No sunrise,
no sunset, no shifting shafts, no time-lapse, no gradual warming or cooling of
the palette. The eerie, total stillness of the light is the film's core visual
identity — the sun never moves, so the light never changes, full stop.
```

## 🏃 MOTION BIBLE (Micro-Behavior Catalog)

```
Every shot includes at least one of the following, drawn from this fixed catalog:
- a deliberate pause with one foot planted on each side of a visible split-line
- switching gloved hands (left matte-black, right reflective white) before
  touching a new surface
- a slight, incomplete head-tilt when studying the fixed, unmoving sun
- fine ice-crystal dust drifting off a frost-rimed surface in the constant wind
- fingers hovering near — not touching — an unfamiliar surface until the correct
  glove is chosen
- a brief stillness, listening, when the sub-audible breathing rhythm is felt
  rather than heard
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Standard gravity (1.0g) on Meridian's surface.
- The fixed sun's position and the split-light rule apply absolutely and without
  exception in every ground-level shot — see LIGHTING BIBLE's Fixed Light Rule.
- The constant, single-direction thermal wind (day-face toward night-face) blows
  continuously through every shot; no gusts, no reversals, no pauses.
- Any glacier or ice-cliff motion (Scene 4.5) is real, physically accurate
  fracturing and calving — cracking, shearing, and falling ice with correct mass
  and momentum, never a soft, gel-like collapse.
- The Meridian Titan's motion (Scene 8.5) is geological in scale and speed —
  slow, massive, and continuous, never sudden or jerky, consistent with a
  300-meter creature that has not moved in an unrecorded span of time.
- Human-to-environment scale is always internally consistent: Dr. Aydın is
  1.70m; the ship is 210m; the Titan's exposed ridge is over 300m, explicitly
  stated per scene.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts. No shaky camera. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look. No airbrushed
or plastic skin. No perfect symmetry. No oversaturated colors. No shifting or
drifting light (the fixed sun and split-light rule must never waver — see
LIGHTING BIBLE). No horror-movie framing or hostile tone — Meridian is vast,
still, and awe-inspiring, never menacing, even in Scene 8.5. No handheld tools in
her hands. No readable text, letters, or numbers on the wrist display or any
in-world surface. No text overlay, no title card, no watermark.

Per the LESSONS_LEARNED archive, every shot in this production additionally
enforces: CONTINUITY LOCK (single unbroken take, no jump-cuts), CREATURE ANATOMY
LOCK (fixed rigid anatomy, no morphing, background creatures in shallow DOF),
CREATURE LOCOMOTION (articulated limb motion, never sliding), FACE LOCK
(identical bone structure and features throughout), CONTACT INTEGRITY (no hand-
surface merging, head always connected to neck/torso), WEIGHT & CONTACT (visible
weight on every surface, no floating, no jello limbs), GRAVITY VECTOR (correct
body lean on any inclined or vertical surface), COLLISION INTEGRITY (no phasing
or clipping between any solid objects or creatures), REFLECTION ACCURACY (any
reflection in glass-leaf fronds, ice, or the helmet visor matches its source
exactly), VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES (ice dust, frost fall, and glacier debris are
volumetric with real dispersion, never flat or frozen), MOTION SPEED (real-time
natural human movement unless slow-motion is explicitly specified), NO AI TEXT
(displays show only abstract amber/blue bars, never characters), and SUIT
CONTINUITY (the Duality Suit's exact split-color design repeats identically in
every single scene).
```

Reference feel: The suspended stillness of Max Richter's "Sleep" crossed with

sequences — a score for a world that never changes, disturbed exactly twice.

### "Güneş hiç batmıyor, hiç doğmuyor — sadece orada duruyor."

Tek karakter (Dr. Seher Aydın), sıfır kesme, sürekli akış — güneşin sonsuza dek
ufukta sabit durduğu, gece ile gündüzün asla değişmediği bir gezegenin dar
alacakaranlık kuşağında geçen bir keşif. Filmin imza hareketi — **THE DESCENT**
(uzaydan yüzeye kesintisiz iniş) — Sahne 0'da ana hook olarak, tam olarak
istenen "gezegeni önce dışarıdan görme" açılışıyla kullanılmış, Sahne 10B'de

kenarındaki buzul çöküşü, aynı karede donuk gündüz sessizliğiyle tezat
oluşturarak) ve **8.5** (ana viral aksiyon: 300 metrelik Meridian Titanı'nın
