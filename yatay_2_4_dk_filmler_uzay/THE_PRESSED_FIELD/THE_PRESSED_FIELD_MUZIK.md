## 🎼 MÜZİK PROMPTU — tek global tema

```
TOOL: Google Flow / Lyria
GENRE: minimal ambient score for a science-fiction short — no melody-led
       cinematic swell, no percussion kit, no synth arpeggios
TEMPO: 48 BPM, strictly constant, no accelerando anywhere in the piece
LENGTH: 100 seconds

INSTRUMENTATION — katmanlar giriş sırasıyla:
  1. 0:00  a dry low bowed double bass drone in D, almost no vibrato,
           doubled by a quiet companion tone two octaves higher so the
           weight survives on a phone speaker
  2. 0:10  a three-note rising figure on a muted upright piano, played
           once every two bars, never completing its phrase
  3. 0:20  a deep sub layer at the fundamental with its octave companion,
           entering exactly on a bar line, felt rather than heard
  4. 0:50  a second slow pulse on a struck metal plate, one hit per bar,
           dry, no reverb tail

STRUCTURE:
  0:00-0:20  drone alone, then the piano figure enters
  0:20-0:50  sub layer under it; density rises, tempo does not
  0:50-1:08  metal pulse joins; the piano figure breaks off unfinished
  1:08-1:20  ⛔ SILENCE — the score stops two seconds before the scene
             boundary and does not play through the peak
  1:20-1:40  one single piano note returns, then the drone; the final
             chord is unresolved and is cut, not faded

MOOD: indifferent, patient, geological — the music belongs to the plain,
      not to the person. It never comments on her, never warns her and
      never resolves.

NEGATIVE: no drums, no percussion kit, no arpeggiated synths, no choir,
          no orchestral swell, no rising strings, no tempo change, no
          fade out, no melody that resolves, no ambient wind, no risers,
          no impact hits, no braams.
```

  S8  adım + toz + iki saplama vuruşu + uzun çalışma nefesi
