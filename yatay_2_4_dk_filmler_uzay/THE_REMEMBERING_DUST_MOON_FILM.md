## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — İSİMSİZ UYDU / "THE LEDGER PLAIN"

```
CLASSIFICATION: A small airless moon (diameter ~1,600 km) orbiting an
INDIGO ICE GIANT in an outer system. The moon carries only a survey
designation — never named. It has been visited exactly once before, 41
years ago, by a two-person survey team whose final report was never
filed. The current mission is a routine re-survey: one person, one
lander, three days.

MIMARİ KUTUP: NOSTROMO değil, boş kulvar. Bu filmde insan yapımı
mimari neredeyse yok — tek yapı 9 metrelik iniş aracı. Filmin "mimarisi"
ZEMİNİN KENDİSİDİR: ova bir arşivdir, bir bina değil.

THE CENTRAL RULE — THE PLAIN REMEMBERS [NOVUM]:
The landing basin — a 12 km wide flat expanse called THE LEDGER PLAIN
in the mission file — is covered by 30-40 cm of ultrafine pale grey
ferroelectric dust resting on dark anorthosite bedrock. Any pressure
applied to the surface (a boot, a knee, a landing pad, a fallen tool)
polarizes the grain lattice and locks a fixed charge template into the
bedrock beneath it. The template is permanent and passive.
Every 19 hours the moon crosses the ice giant's MAGNETOTAIL. For roughly
40 minutes, the plain's surface charges strongly enough that the top
2-3 cm of dust lifts free of the ground on electrostatic repulsion and
hangs as a thin, flat suspended sheet. As the passage ends, the sheet
settles — and it does not settle evenly. It settles along ONE stored
charge template, reproducing the impression that made it: grain by
grain, in the exact order the original pressure was applied.
THE PLAIN RELEASES ITS OLDEST UNPLAYED RECORD FIRST — one record per
passage — and a record, once released, is discharged forever. The plain
is a queue. It pays its debts in order, and it never repeats.

THE ERASURE RULE [S10'DA GÖRÜLÜR]: The replayed record exists only in
the suspended top 2-3 cm. When the passage ends, the sheet settles
EVENLY across the whole basin and every replayed mark fills level and
disappears — the record is played once and then gone. Fresh pressure
marks are different: her boots press 7 cm into the layer BENEATH the
suspended one, so her own prints SURVIVE the settling, shallower but
still clearly readable. This asymmetry is the ending of the film: the
past is erased, her handprint stays in the queue.

WHY SHE CANNOT SIMPLY WAIT FOR MORE: the next passage is 19 hours away
and the queue is decades long. She is not being denied an answer by
bad luck — she is standing in front of an archive that opens one page
per 19 hours and started 41 years before her.

WHAT THIS MEANS ON SCREEN: A line of bootprints presses itself into the
dust, one after another, at walking speed, with no one walking. The
prints belong to a chevron-tread boot design retired 30 years ago. They
are 41 years old and they are arriving now.

SURFACE: Flat pale grey dust plain, broken by scattered angular boulders
0.4 m to 3 m across and ringed at 6 km by a 90 m basin rim. The bedrock
shows through as darker anorthosite patches where the dust is thin. No
craters inside the basin floor itself — the dust has filled them all.

ATMOSPHERE: None. Below 10^-11 atm — absolute vacuum for all purposes.
No sound propagates outside the suit. No wind. No weather. No haze.
The sky is BLACK between the ice giant and the stars. Stars are sharp
non-twinkling points.

GRAVITY: 0.11g. Footsteps float slightly at the top of each stride.
Displaced dust follows flat ballistic parabolas and LANDS — it does not
hang, does not billow, does not drift sideways. There is no air to
suspend it. The ONLY suspended dust in this film is the electrostatic
sheet during a magnetotail passage, and that suspension is charge-held,
vertical, and physically motivated.

THE ICE GIANT IN THE SKY: Fills approximately 30 degrees of sky —
deep indigo-blue, almost featureless, with two faint darker latitude
bands and a THIN DARK RING SYSTEM seen nearly edge-on as a single razor-
sharp dark line crossing its disc and extending past both limbs. It sits
high and to the RIGHT in the sky from the landing site, and it never
moves during the film. Its reflected light is the film's second light
source — cold indigo, weak, directional.

THE STAR: A small white-yellow star sits 9 degrees above the western
horizon — a hard, bright point, never a disc. It rakes the plain at a
shallow angle, so every boulder, every ridge, every 4 mm print rim
throws a shadow 8-10 times its own height. Shadows are ABSOLUTE BLACK.

TEMPERATURE: -180°C. Not visually represented except as fine frost
crystals in the shadowed lee of boulders.

SOUND: TOTAL VACUUM outside the suit. She hears only what conducts
through her own body and suit: her breathing, the suit fan, boot impact
transmitted through the sole, and the delayed relay voice in her helmet
speakers. The plain writing itself is COMPLETELY SILENT — this silence
is the film's most important sound design decision.

SOSYOLOJİ — TEK NESNE KURALI: Filmin tek sosyolojik nesnesi, S4'te
bulunan çapa çubuğunun başına ELLE DÜĞÜMLENMİŞ solmuş pas-kırmızı bez
şerittir. Kurum işi değil, kişisel bir işaret. 41 yıl önce biri orada
durup elleriyle bir düğüm attı. Tek nesne, sıfır replik, tam sosyoloji.
```

```
MOVEMENT SIGNATURE — "THE DAMPED STEP": each stride lifts slowly, floats

When something matters, she stops moving entirely, and only her chest
```

## 📡 RELAY VOICE BIBLE — GECİKMELİ YER KONTROL

```
IDENTITY: Not an AI. A human dispatcher, 6 hours 20 minutes away at
light speed, on a ONE-WAY relay. Every line that arrives was recorded
6h20 ago, in response to images she sent 6h20 before that. Nothing the
voice says can ever be an answer to what she is currently doing.

THIS IS THE FILM'S ANTAGONIST [UZAY_SENARYO_KILAVUZU §10, KANAL 3]:
Not a villain, not a machine, not the environment. A DISTANCE. The
relay is competent, careful and completely useless, and it is the only
other human presence in the film.

VOICE CHARACTER: Male-coded, calm, measured, warm. Slight transmission
compression — a thin band-limited quality, as if the top and bottom of
the voice have been shaved off. No urgency; the voice does not know
anything is happening. It sounds like someone reading a considered note
in a quiet room, hours ago, somewhere safe.

LANGUAGE: English only. Complete, polite sentences — the exact opposite
of a crisis AI's clipped delivery. The politeness is what makes it
unbearable.

DELIVERY RULES FOR VEO 3.1:
- The relay is ALWAYS OFF-SCREEN — helmet speaker audio only. There is
  never a second person, never an avatar, never a hologram, never a
  mouth in frame other than hers.
- Prompt syntax: "a calm male voice comes through her helmet speakers,
  band-limited by transmission compression, saying: '[line]'"
- Never simultaneous with her voice. Turn-taking only.
- Each spoken line under 5 seconds. Maximum 2 lines per scene.
- The spoken line is HEARD ONLY, never shown as text [DERS 40].

TOPLAM DİYALOG — FİLMDE SADECE 3 SAHNE KONUŞUR:
S5  RELAY: "Surveyor — we've reviewed your first images. Whatever is
           writing in that dust, do not walk beside it."

    HER:   "Acknowledged."   (…ve yerinden kımıldamaz)
S12 HER:   "Take your time."
```

## 🚀 LANDER BIBLE — TEK KİŞİLİK YÜZEY ARACI (prose adı: CANTOR)

```
Small, boring, functional. This is not a hero ship — it is a vehicle
parked at the edge of the frame, and it is the only man-made object in
the film besides the anchor spike.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, IDENTICAL in every scene):
- HEIGHT: 9.0 m. WIDTH ACROSS LEGS: 6.0 m. Bone-white upper hull with
  a graphite-grey lower equipment skirt — the same color language as
  her suit.
- FOUR splayed landing legs, each with a 1.2 m round flat pad resting
  ON the dust surface with visible compression under each pad.
- ONE oval hatch, 2.0 m tall, on the near face, standing open inward.
- ONE ladder from the hatch sill to the ground: 7 rungs, fixed at a
  70-degree angle. Never a staircase, never a ramp.
- ONE amber beacon (2700K) on the upper hull, flashing on a 4-second
  cycle. Never any other light on the lander.
- NO wings, no engine glow after landing, no antenna dish, no markings,
  no text, no insignia anywhere on the hull.

POSITION LOCK: The lander stands at the eastern edge of the plain,
60 meters from her work area, and it NEVER MOVES for the entire film.
In every wide shot it sits in the same place in the world; only the
camera's relationship to it changes.

VEHICLE SCALE LOCK [DERS 32]: 9.0 m tall against a 1.68 m person =
1:5.4 ratio. Hatch 2.0 m, ladder 7 rungs at 70 degrees, pads 1.2 m.
These numbers are copied identically into every prompt where the lander
is visible. No proportion drift, no ladder angle change, no hull
scaling between scenes.

THE PADS: Each landing pad has pressed a 1.2 m circular depression into
the dust. These four circles are visible in every wide shot and are
themselves a stored record — the plain will replay them one day, long
after she is gone. This is never explained on screen.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot type, never mixed:
- WIDE / PLAIN / SCALE / LANDER shots       → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- CHARACTER / MEDIUM / ACTION shots         → 75mm
- DUST DETAIL / PRINT FORMING / HAND shots  → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot, never combined):
- fixed lockoff — the film's DEFAULT. Ten of fifteen scenes are locked.
- slow push-in — only S2, S7
- slow pull-back — only S3, S13
- slow lateral track — only S5 (walking beside the track)
- slow crane-rise — only S10 (the plain erasing itself)
- slow tilt-up — only S11 (Overview)

THIS FILM'S SIGNATURE DECISION: THE LOCKED CAMERA. This is a film about
a surface that moves while everything else stays still. If the camera
moves, the surface stops being the subject. When in doubt: LOCK IT OFF.

THE REPEATED FRAME [YAPI = TEMA]: S2 and S12 are the SAME SHOT —
100mm macro, camera locked at 0.8 meters, same 25-degree high angle,
same composition: her right glove entering frame from the right, the
dust filling the lower two-thirds.
In S2 the hand hovers 8 cm above the dust and withdraws; one chevron
print sits at frame left.
In S12 the palm presses fully into the dust and stays; the chevron print
at frame left is GONE — erased in S10 — and the dust there is smooth.
Those two changes are the ONLY differences. Lens, distance, angle,
height, light and composition stay identical. This must be EXACT.

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: 10 seconds per scene. Hook (S0) = 8 seconds.

CONTINUITY LOCK [DERS 1 — CRITICAL]: SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE per
scene. No teleport, no jump-cut, no reset, no angle change, no
transitions. Each scene = SEPARATE video generation.

INTERIOR/EXTERIOR SPLIT: NONE. Every one of the 15 scenes is exterior,
on the surface, in vacuum. There is no cockpit scene, no interior
scene, and no scene in which she is inside anything. This is deliberate:
the film never cuts away from the ground.
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE

```
LIGHTING PHILOSOPHY: Two natural sources, both permanently visible in
the sky, neither of them moving for the entire film. The light never
changes because nothing here changes except the ground. The only
artificial light is her helmet lamp, and it appears in exactly three
scenes.

LIGHT FAMILY 1 — THE LOW STAR (KEY):
- A small white-yellow star (5800K), 9 degrees above the western
  horizon, hard and point-like — never a disc, never a flare.
- Rakes the plain at a shallow angle from FRAME LEFT.
- Every object throws a shadow 8-10x its own height, with a RAZOR-SHARP
  edge and an ABSOLUTELY BLACK interior.
- Critically: this grazing angle makes a 4 mm print rim read like a
  mountain range. The entire novum is only visible BECAUSE of this
  light. Never change the key angle.

LIGHT FAMILY 2 — PLANETSHINE (SECOND SOURCE, NOT FILL):
- The indigo ice giant, high and to the RIGHT, reflects a weak, cold,
  deeply blue directional light onto surfaces that face it.
- This is a SECOND DIRECTIONAL SOURCE with a visible origin in frame,
  not ambient fill. Surfaces facing away from BOTH the star and the ice
  giant stay PURE BLACK — no exceptions, no lift, no ambient wash.
- On her bone-white suit this reads as a cold indigo edge along the
  right shoulder, helmet and thigh in every exterior shot.

FILL LIGHT: NONE. Vacuum rule [KILAVUZ_REHBERI §3.1]. Shadows are
absolute black with a hard edge. Ders 41 (yumuşak ışık meşrudur) BU
FİLMDE GEÇERSİZDİR — burada atmosfer yok.

LIGHT FAMILY 3 — HELMET LAMP (used in S2, S6.5, S12 only):
- A single narrow cool-white beam (5500K) from the helmet's left
  temple, projecting a sharp-edged elliptical pool about 60 cm across
  onto the dust for close inspection work. Hard cone edge, clear
  falloff, no halo, no glow spill.

LIGHT FAMILY 4 — LANDER BEACON (S1, S13 only):
- Amber 2700K, 4-second cycle, on the lander's upper hull. Visible as a
  rhythmic pulse on the bone-white hull plating only. It does not light
  the ground and it does not light her.

DURING A MAGNETOTAIL PASSAGE (S3, S6.5, S10): the suspended 2-3 cm dust
```
