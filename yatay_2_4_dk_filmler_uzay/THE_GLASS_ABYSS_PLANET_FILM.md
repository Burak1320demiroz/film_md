## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: VITREA

```
MINERAL PHYSICS: A rocky, tectonically dormant planet whose entire
upper crust — approximately 10 kilometers thick — is composed of an
unusually pure, naturally formed transparent silicate crystal lattice.
This is real mineralogy taken to planetary extreme — the same optical
principle behind Earth's own Iceland spar (optical calcite), clear
quartz, and Mexico's Cave of Crystals selenite, except here a unique
geological epoch — billions of years of extremely slow, impurity-free
crystallization under low-iron, low-seismic conditions — produced a
continuous transparent shell spanning the entire planet.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: The ground is SOLID, LOAD-BEARING, and
STRUCTURALLY SOUND — it simply happens to be perfectly transparent.
Walking on it feels exactly like walking on rock. But LOOKING DOWN
through it reveals the entire visible geological column beneath:
colored mineral strata in horizontal bands, underground rivers,
bioluminescent cave networks, and — at the deepest visible limit — the
faint warm glow of the planet's molten mantle, 10 kilometers below.
The film's entire visual thesis rests on the contradiction between
tactile solidity underfoot and infinite visible depth below.

THE STRATA (visible layers, top to bottom):
- 0-50m CRYSTAL SHELL: perfectly clear, featureless, load-bearing.
  Footsteps leave no mark. Sound travels sharply through it, producing
  a faint, bell-like ring underfoot.
- 50-200m AMBER BAND: the first visible mineral inclusion layer — a
  warm, translucent amber-gold band of iron-oxide trace minerals,
  visible as a soft golden glow 50 meters below the surface.
- 200-800m BLUE VEIN ZONE: a network of naturally flowing underground
  rivers, their blue-mineral-tinted water clearly visible as bright
  blue channels coursing through the transparent rock, occasionally
  hosting visible, bioluminescent aquatic life — the DEPTH SWIMMERS
  (1-3m translucent eel-analog organisms with soft cyan glow).
- 800-3000m FOSSIL LATTICE: dense, ancient, fossilized crystalline
  organisms — the remains of a billion-year-old crystal-based ecosystem
  — visible as intricate, fractal-branching dark silhouettes suspended
  at multiple depths, like pressed flowers in glass.
- 3000-8000m PRISMATIC DEEP: increasingly dense crystal with natural
  internal fracture planes that split any light entering from above into
  slow-moving prismatic rainbow bands — a permanent, deep-layer aurora
  visible through the floor during sunset hours.
- 8000-10000m MANTLE GLOW: the deepest visible layer, where the
  crystal transitions to opaque rock and finally to the planet's molten
  mantle, visible only as a faint, warm, deep-orange underglow — the
  planet's heartbeat, 10 kilometers straight down through clear rock.

SURFACE: Flat, featureless crystal plains stretching in every direction,
broken only by occasional FAULT SCARPS — natural vertical fracture
walls where tectonic shifts long ago cracked the crystal shell, exposing
its layered cross-section edge-on like a geological core sample the
size of a cliff face. These scarps are the only locations where the
crystal's depth-layers can be touched directly rather than only seen
through.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: 0.92g, thin, dry, crystal-clear atmosphere with
minimal dust — the air itself is unusually transparent, maximizing
the optical clarity of the ground beneath. A single warm golden sun
provides consistent, low-angle light ideal for prismatic refraction
through the crystal floor.

SOUND: Near-total surface silence — no vegetation, no loose rock, no
soil to absorb or scatter sound. Footsteps on the crystal produce a
clear, resonant, bell-like ring that propagates visibly (as faint
ripples in the prismatic refraction pattern below) for hundreds of
meters. The underground rivers are faintly audible as a low, continuous
murmur transmitted through the crystal itself, heard more through the
feet than the ears.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. ZEHRA DERİN

```
IDENTITY: 31-year-old Turkish crystallographer and subsurface geologist.
First human to walk the transparent crust of Vitrea. Three days into a
solo geological survey expedition, still visibly affected by the
constant vertigo of seeing 10 kilometers of depth beneath every step.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm medium-olive skin with real,
visible pore texture, a faint scatter of sun-freckles across the bridge
of the nose and upper cheeks, no airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. An
open, expressive face with high cheekbones and a strong, slightly
rounded jaw. Thick, dark eyebrows with a natural arch. Large, dark brown
eyes with visible catchlight, a habitual widened look — not fear but
constant, involuntary alertness to depth. Slightly parted lips, a
nervous habit of pressing them together before each step. A thin, pale
scar across the left temple from an old climbing fall. Subtle natural
facial asymmetry — a real, alert, capable human face.

HAIR: Dark brown-black, pulled back into a tight, low braid, a few
loose strands escaping near her temples, occasionally catching the
prismatic light from below.

BUILD: Compact, steady, 1.66m, a mountaineer's low center of gravity
and deliberate foot placement — every step is conscious, controlled,
and visibly chosen.

SUIT: Matte warm-grey and amber-gold subsurface survey suit, heavily
mission-worn:
- reinforced, thick-soled boots with broad, flat treads designed to
  distribute weight evenly across the crystal surface, the soles
  slightly scuffed from three days of walking
- a low-profile ground-penetrating sonar unit integrated into her left
  forearm gauntlet — NEVER a handheld device — producing a soft amber
  pulse visible through the gauntlet's outer panel when active
- amber-gold accent piping along the suit's seams that shifts brightness
  subtly depending on the depth of the crystal beneath her (brighter
  over thin zones, dimmer over thick — a passive geological depth
  indicator woven into the suit's design)
- a fully transparent, seamless visor ALWAYS revealing her complete face,
  faintly catching prismatic reflections from the ground below
- a single scratched wrist display on her right forearm showing ONLY a
  slow pulsing depth indicator — a simple amber ring that widens over
  deep zones and tightens over thin zones — NEVER readable digits or
  text
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY — no handheld tools, scanners, or weapons
- a thin, matte-grey safety tether coiled at her hip, deployed only
  near fault scarps

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE in every frame
regardless of head angle. Never disappears or merges with the face.
Reflections must match actual environment lighting, including prismatic
ground-glow and the warm amber of her depth indicator.

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every detail above remains IDENTICAL
across all scenes — no design changes, no color shifts, no added/removed
equipment between shots.

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When touching ANY surface (crystal
ground, fault scarp wall, tether line), her hands/fingers must maintain
COMPLETE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY — clear visible boundary at all contact
points, never merging or melting into what she touches. Head remains
physically connected to neck/torso at all times.

GRAVITY VECTOR RULE [LESSON 15]: Hair, loose suit elements, and any
particulate on her suit hang and move with consistent 0.92g downward
gravity at all times. No floating, no inconsistent vector.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Slow, deliberate, mountaineer's footfalls — each
step placed with visible care and a brief pause before committing full
weight. A habit of looking down through the ground before stepping, then
quickly looking up to the horizon to reset her equilibrium. Occasionally
crouches to press one palm flat against the crystal, feeling rather than
seeing the ground's solidity. A controlled exhale before crossing any
zone where the depth indicator brightens.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — SURVEY VESSEL _PRIZMA_

```
HULL LENGTH: 90 meters. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A lightweight geological survey vessel with unusually
wide, flat landing pads designed to distribute its weight across the
maximum possible surface area of the crystal crust — the same
engineering principle as snowshoes on thin ice. It lands, surveys, and
departs without ever stressing the transparent ground beneath it.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a sleek, low central hull, 50 meters long, matte warm-grey with amber
  accent bands, a single row of 1m observation ports along its length as
  a scale ruler
- four wide, flat landing pad struts, each 12 meters long, ending in
  broad 4-meter-diameter circular pads designed to rest flat on the
  crystal surface — visible through the transparent ground from any
  angle below
- two compact survey-drone bays, sealed, 3m diameter, at the stern
- one forward observation window, 2m high by 3.5m wide, positioned at
  the hull's bow with a downward-angled lower pane providing a direct
  view into the crystal beneath the ship

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 1m
observation ports as a ruler along the hull, the 2m cockpit window with
Zehra visible inside at correct proportion, Zehra's 1.66m figure beside
a landing pad strut, or the hull visible THROUGH the transparent ground
from below for scale contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate
perspective, correct engineering proportions, realistic scale
relationships — the ship must never appear miniature, toy-like, or like
a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: Never show the full 90m hull AND a legible human face
in the same frame.

TRANSPARENCY RULE: In any shot where the ship rests on the crystal
surface, its landing pads and the underside of its hull MUST be faintly
visible THROUGH the transparent ground from any viewing angle that
includes both the surface and the depths below — the ship is literally
visible from underneath, seen through the floor.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / DEPTH-SCALE / FAULT SCARP → 32mm Cooke
  anamorphic
- MEDIUM / CHARACTER / ACTION / VERTIGO → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (crystal surface texture, prismatic refraction detail,
  underground river close-up through crystal) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in,
pull-back, tilt-up, tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow
downward tilt (reserved for vertigo-inducing depth-reveal shots).

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: every video prompt is exactly 10 seconds, single
continuous shot.

CONTINUITY LOCK [LESSON 1 — CRITICAL]: SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE. No
teleport, no jump-cut, no reset, no angle change, no transitions. Every
frame flows directly from the previous. Each scene = SEPARATE 10-second
video. NEVER combined.

MOTION SPEED RULE [LESSON 12]: All character and object movement occurs
at physically consistent speed — real-time human speed, mountaineer's
deliberate pace. No unmotivated slow-motion.

VERTIGO SHOT RULE (UNIQUE TO THIS FILM): In any shot where the camera
tilts downward to reveal depth through the crystal floor, the tilt must
be SLOW, STEADY, and CONTINUOUS — never a snap or a jump. The depth
must accumulate gradually, layer by layer, for maximum vertigo impact.
```

```
from internal crystal fracture planes refracting the surface light —
visible as a permanent, deep-layer color field shifting subtly with

crystal layers are lit edge-on by the surface sun — each stratum
catching light at a different angle, producing a visible,
high-contrast geological color spectrum in cross-section.

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON 3]: All bioluminescent and prismatic
light sources emit SHARP, DIRECTIONAL light with clear falloff — never
a soft, diffused, dreamy glow. High contrast between lit and unlit
layers at all times. Skin retains visible pore texture and realistic
shadow under every light condition.
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 0.92g fixed throughout.
- The crystal crust is SOLID, LOAD-BEARING, and STRUCTURALLY SOUND —
  it simply happens to be optically transparent. It does not flex,
  crack, or yield under human weight or the ship's distributed landing
  pads. The only exception: THIN ZONES near fault scarps, where the
  crystal is naturally thinner (2-5 meters instead of 10km) and her
  depth indicator brightens as a warning.
- Footsteps on the crystal produce a clear, resonant, bell-like ring
  that propagates as faint visible ripples in the prismatic refraction
  pattern below, dissipating within seconds.
- Underground rivers are GENUINE FLOWING WATER — continuously moving
  blue-mineral-tinted channels visible through the crystal, with real
  current, real turbulence, and occasionally visible bioluminescent
  aquatic life.
- Human-to-environment scale: Zehra 1.66m; ship Prizma 90m; landing
  pads 4m diameter; Depth Swimmers 1-3m; Fossil Lattice organisms
  (ancient, fossilized) 5-50m span; visible depth to mantle ~10km.

GRAVITY VECTOR RULE [LESSON 15]: All loose or hanging objects obey
consistent 0.92g downward gravity at all times. Hair, loose fabric, and
particulate hang downward. No inconsistent vector.

COLLISION INTEGRITY [LESSON 17]: ALL objects, the character, and the
crystal surface obey RIGID BODY COLLISION. The character never passes
through the crystal floor. Feet rest ON the surface, never sinking into
or merging with it. The ship's landing pads rest ON the crystal, never
phasing through.

CONTACT INTEGRITY [LESSON 11]: Hands/fingers NEVER merge with the
crystal surface or any other object.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: All crystal geometry remains STATIC
and rigid. The underground strata, rivers, and fossil formations are
fixed in position — only the water in the rivers flows, and only the
Depth Swimmers move. Nothing else below changes position or shape.

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: Any dust kicked by footsteps or
ship landing must behave as volumetric, physically accurate particles —
dispersing, settling under 0.92g, never freezing or static.

TRANSPARENCY PHYSICS (UNIQUE TO THIS FILM): The crystal's transparency
is optically accurate — objects deeper below appear slightly smaller
(correct perspective diminution), slightly bluer (atmospheric
absorption analog within the crystal), and slightly less sharp (natural
light scatter over distance). Objects at 50m depth are perfectly clear;
at 3000m they show visible haze; at 10000m the mantle glow is diffuse
and soft. This depth-haze gradient must be CONSISTENT across all shots.
```
