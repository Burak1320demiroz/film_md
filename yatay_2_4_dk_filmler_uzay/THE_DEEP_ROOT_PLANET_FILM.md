## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: RADIX

```
CORE CONCEPT: A barren, gray, seemingly lifeless rocky world whose entire
bioenergetic system exists UNDERGROUND. The surface is cracked basalt desert
under a pale, overcast sky — featureless and dead. But beneath the crust, a
vast network of bioluminescent energy roots spreads across the entire planet
like a living nervous system. These roots are not plant matter — they are
crystallized bioelectric conduits, mineralized organic channels that pulse
with cyan-white energy, branching fractally from hair-thin filaments to
trunk-thick main arteries up to 3 meters in diameter.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: The contrast between the dead gray surface and the
living cyan underground is the film's entire visual thesis. Every frame must
reinforce this duality. The roots are NOT magic — they are a real,
mineralized bioelectric network, analogous to Earth's mycelium networks or
neural pathways, scaled to planetary infrastructure.

SURFACE: Flat, cracked, pale gray basalt desert. No vegetation, no water,
no visible life. Occasional shallow canyons (50-100m deep) cut by ancient
water erosion, now bone-dry. Thin atmosphere, overcast white-gray sky, weak
diffused sunlight. Wind-carved rock formations. Silence.

UNDERGROUND (accessed via canyon fissures and cave systems):
- ENTRY LAYER (0-20m): Dark basalt cave walls, first faint cyan traces
  appearing as thin hairline cracks in the rock, pulsing faintly.
- ROOT NETWORK (20-100m): The main root system — branching cyan-white
  crystallized conduits from 1cm to 3m diameter, embedded in and growing
  through the dark rock, pulsing with slow, rhythmic bioelectric energy.
  The pulse is synchronized across the entire visible network — a single
  planetary heartbeat, roughly 8 seconds per cycle.
- CORE CHAMBER (100m+): A vast underground cathedral where thousands of
  roots converge into a single massive nexus node — a 30-meter sphere of
  crystallized energy, the planet's central bioelectric hub. The chamber
  is naturally carved by the roots' growth, walls entirely covered in
  branching cyan patterns.

SOUND: Surface — wind over barren rock, near-silence. Entry Layer — dripping
mineral water, faint electrical hum. Root Network — a deep, rhythmic pulse
like a slow heartbeat, growing louder with proximity to larger roots. Core
Chamber — a sustained, resonant harmonic chord from all roots vibrating
together.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. TOPRAK DERAN

```
IDENTITY: 31-year-old Turkish geobiologist specializing in subsurface energy
systems. First human to descend into Radix's root network.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Olive-brown skin with real, visible pore
texture, a thin pale scar across the bridge of the nose from a caving
accident, no airbrushing, no plastic smoothness. Angular cheekbones, deep-set
dark brown eyes with visible catchlight, heavy brows, a slightly crooked
nose. Thin lips, usually pressed together in concentration. A real,
weathered, focused human face.

HAIR: Black, buzzcut short, practical.

BUILD: Wiry, 1.72m, narrow shoulders, long arms — a natural caver's build.

SUIT: Matte charcoal-gray geological survey suit with cyan-blue accent
piping along the seams (functional bio-monitor indicators), heavily worn:
- integrated chest-mounted headlamp (single bright white beam, always visible
  and directional — the PRIMARY artificial light source underground)
- reinforced knee and elbow pads, scuffed and dust-covered
- a fully transparent, seamless visor ALWAYS revealing her complete face,
  showing condensation underground, dust on the surface
- a single scratched wrist display on her left forearm for readings
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY — no handheld tools, scanners, or weapons

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: The visor must remain ALWAYS VISIBLE in
every frame regardless of head angle. It must NEVER disappear or merge with
the face. Consistent reflections matching actual environment lighting.

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every detail above must remain IDENTICAL
across all scenes — no design changes, no color shifts, no added/removed
equipment between shots.

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When touching ANY surface (roots, rock,
energy nexus), her hands/fingers must maintain COMPLETE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY.
Fingers must NEVER merge with, melt into, or be absorbed by the surface.
Clear visible boundary at all contact points. Head must remain physically
connected to neck/torso at all times.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Cautious, methodical — she tests every foothold before
committing, moves through tight spaces with practiced economy, and has a
habit of holding her breath and tilting her head when listening to the roots'
pulse.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — GEOLOGICAL SURVEY VESSEL _KAZICI_

```
HULL LENGTH: 90 meters. Always anchored to this figure.

DESIGN LOGIC: A heavy, angular, ground-anchored survey vessel designed for
extended surface operations on barren, rocky worlds. Sits directly on the
surface on wide landing struts — never hovers during ground operations.

STRUCTURE:
- central hull, 50m long, angular matte-gray with orange accent panels
- four wide stabilizer struts, 12m each, with broad footpads
- two rear engine pods, each 6m diameter, mounted at the stern
- one forward cockpit window, 2m high by 3.5m wide
- a row of 0.9m viewports along the hull as scale rulers

SCALE ANCHORS: At least one of — 0.9m viewports visible, 2m cockpit window
with Toprak at correct proportion, or hull against canyon wall for scale.

MANDATORY: "physically accurate perspective, correct engineering proportions,
realistic scale relationships — not miniature, not toy-like."

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLE RULE [LESSON 10]: All dust, exhaust, and thruster
effects must be VOLUMETRIC with real dispersion, turbulence, and gravity.
Particles must NEVER freeze, drag with camera, or appear as flat 2D planes.
Exhaust must show visible turbulence and heat distortion.

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: Never show full 90m hull AND legible human face in the
same frame.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / UNDERGROUND SCALE → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / CHARACTER / ACTION → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (root texture, crystal detail, energy pulse) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY: push-in, pull-back, tilt-up, tilt-down, slow orbit,
fixed lockoff, slow lateral track.

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: 10 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts.

CONTINUITY LOCK [LESSON 1 — CRITICAL]: SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE. Camera
must not teleport, jump-cut, reset position, or change angle suddenly. Every
frame flows directly from the previous. Camera moves ONLY as specified in
MOTION. Each scene generated as SEPARATE 10-second video — NEVER combined.

MOTION SPEED RULE [LESSON 12]: All movement at REAL-TIME natural human speed
unless slow-motion explicitly specified. No unmotivated slow-motion drift.
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE

```
SURFACE: Flat, diffused white-gray overcast light, no directional key, pale
bounce light from cracked basalt. No warmth, no color — deliberately dull.

ENTRY LAYER (0-20m): Headlamp as primary key light (sharp white directional
beam). Faint cyan hairline glow in rock cracks as secondary fill. Deep
black shadow beyond the headlamp's reach.

ROOT NETWORK (20-100m): Headlamp key light supplemented by cyan-white
bioluminescent glow from the roots themselves — pulsing in 8-second cycles.
The roots cast sharp cyan shadows on surrounding rock. Deep black shadow
between pulses.

CORE CHAMBER (100m+): The 30m energy nexus as the dominant light source —
intense, steady cyan-white radiance. Headlamp becomes negligible. Sharp
cyan shadows from root structures on chamber walls.

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON 3]: ALL bioluminescent/energy sources must emit
SHARP, DIRECTIONAL light with clear falloff — NOT soft diffused dreamy glow.
Maintain HIGH CONTRAST between lit and unlit areas. The roots create DEFINED
shadows, not frame-washing bloom. Skin under cyan light retains visible pore
texture, realistic shadow. Visor reflections must MATCH actual environment
lighting angle and color.
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 1.02g (slightly heavier than Earth).
- Root energy pulse: synchronized 8-second cycle across all visible roots.
- Root material: crystallized mineral-organic composite, rigid and brittle
  at small scales, flexible at large scales — like coral or minerite.
- Root glow: real bioelectric discharge, analogous to electric eel biology
  at network scale. Light is emitted from within the crystalline structure.
- Human-to-environment scale: Toprak 1.72m; ship 90m; root diameters 1cm
  to 3m; core nexus sphere 30m; canyon depth 50-100m.

WEIGHT & CONTACT [LESSON 4]: Visible weight on every surface contact.
Footfalls kick dust, compress gravel, leave prints. No floating, no
hovering. Rigid skeletal proportions — no jello, no rubber-banding.

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: All dust/exhaust = volumetric, real
dispersion, turbulence, gravity. Never frozen, flat, or static.

CREATURE ANATOMY LOCK [LESSON 2]: N/A for this film (no creatures), but if
any organism appears in the root network, locked anatomy throughout.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: All background elements (rock walls, root
structures, canyon topology) remain COMPLETELY STATIC unless explicitly
described as moving. No breathing, warping, morphing, or topology changes.
Root branching patterns must remain FIXED throughout each shot.
```
