A hanging lamp is the only instrument on this ship. It is not pointing down.

She owns the ship and she flies it by hand. The route runs past three masses,
and at every one of them there is a wide line and a tight line. Nothing else
happens for two minutes: she drives, and the room answers.

THE SCIENCE
• Under thrust, "down" is opposite the total acceleration — so a steering
  burn tilts the apparent vertical by arctan(lateral ÷ axial). A 0.62 g turn
  against a 0.25 g cruise puts the floor 68 degrees out and makes everyone
  aboard weigh 2.67 times what they did a minute earlier.
• A plumb line is an accelerometer. It is how the first turn-and-bank
  indicators worked, and it is why a drink tilts in a cornering car.
• A black hole with nothing falling into it emits nothing. You see it by
  what it bends: a black disc inside a thin ring of lensed starlight, with
  the star field behind it stretched into arcs.
• Outside a hull in vacuum there is no sound at all. Everything you hear in
  this film is conducted through the ship's own structure.
