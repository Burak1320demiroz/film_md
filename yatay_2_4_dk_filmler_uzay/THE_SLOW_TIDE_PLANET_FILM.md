## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: LENTOS

```
PLANETARY MASS & OCEAN PHYSICS: A single-ocean world of unusually high planetary
mass — roughly 2.4x Earth's — with no continents, only a uniform, near-infinite
depth of water beneath a thin, breathable-pressure-equivalent atmosphere. The
combination of extreme deep-pressure and a unique dissolved mineral-salt content
gives Lentos's ocean genuinely non-Newtonian rheology at scale: under sudden or
small-scale stress (a footstep, a hand, a climbing grip) the water behaves with
startling rigidity, almost like dense, cool gel or aged pitch — climbable,
walkable, load-bearing for short durations. Under sustained, large-scale stress
(gravity, tidal forces, the accumulated weight of the ocean itself) it flows, but
at a pace almost too slow for human perception — true liquid motion measured in
centimeters per second even at the crest of the largest waves.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: This is real fluid dynamics extrapolated to extreme scale —
akin to Earth's own pitch-drop experiments, where solid-seeming tar flows
measurably over years. The film's entire visual thesis rests on this contradiction:
water that looks and behaves like a climbable, load-bearing cliff face at human
timescales while genuinely, physically flowing on planetary timescales. Nothing
here is magic or CGI-fantasy — it is applied physics taken to its logical, awesome
extreme.

THE WAVE: The specific wave the film follows has been rising for months, driven by
a distant, slow-orbit companion moon's tidal pull. It is currently 340 meters tall
at its face, moving at approximately 1.2 centimeters per second — imperceptible to
the naked eye moment-to-moment, but measurable and, over the course of the film's
single continuous day, visibly different in position between the opening and
closing shots. Its face is a near-vertical, textured grey-blue wall; its crest,
far above, has already begun a slow, permanent curl that will not complete its
break for several more weeks.

SURFACE TEXTURE: The wave's face is not smooth — frozen-looking ripples, standing
foam patterns, and individual airborne droplets caught mid-splash at its leading
edge are all visible, genuinely suspended in place by the fluid's extreme
viscosity, like a photograph made physical. Small marine organisms are visible
embedded and slowly drifting within the translucent upper meters of the wave's
body, caught in its slow-motion current.

OPEN OCEAN: Away from the wave, the ocean's vast, flat, ancient surface is nearly
mirror-still, its water so massive and slow-moving that surface disturbance
(footsteps, equipment, the ship's presence) dissipates almost immediately, unlike
Earth's water.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: Standard-feeling gravity, approximately 1.15g (owing to the
planet's higher mass), thin but breathable-pressure equivalent atmosphere. Suits
worn primarily for pressure/thermal regulation near the wave's cold interior.

SKY AS CHARACTER: A pale, cool blue-white sun, positioned low and constant, casting
long, even light across the flat ocean and up the wave's towering face. The distant
companion moon responsible for the tide is visible during portions of the day as a
pale grey-white disc, disproportionately large in the sky — a constant visual
reminder of the gravitational engine driving the slow tide.

SOUND: A low, deep, continuous groan-hum is present near the wave's base — not
crashing water, but the sound of immense, slow-moving mass under stress, felt as
much as heard.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. DENİZ KAYA

```
IDENTITY: 32-year-old Turkish rheologist and planetary oceanographer, specializing
in non-Newtonian fluid behavior at extreme scale. First human to physically climb
the face of a Lentos mega-wave. Weeks into a solo tidal-monitoring expedition.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Warm olive skin with realistic visible pore
texture and natural weathering from sun and cold-spray exposure, no airbrushing, no
plastic smoothness. A compact, athletic face with strong, defined cheekbones. Dark,
straight eyebrows, slightly furrowed in habitual concentration. Deep-set, calm dark
eyes with visible catchlight, faint natural laugh-lines, a steady, watchful gaze
suited to reading slow-moving water for hours at a time. A small, straight nose.
Full lips, windburned at the edges. A thin, faded rope-burn scar across her right
palm (visible when ungloved), and a small chip in her left front tooth, visible when
she smiles. Subtle natural facial asymmetry — a real, weathered, capable human face.

HAIR: Dark brown, tightly braided close to the scalp in a practical climbing braid,
a few realistic loose strands escaping near her temple where they meet the helmet
seal.

BUILD: Compact, powerfully built, 1.65m, a climber's dense muscularity visible even
under the suit, low center of gravity, deliberate and economical in every movement.

SUIT: Deep slate-teal and pale grey cold-water expedition suit, heavily mission-worn:
- reinforced climbing-grip gauntlets and boot soles (visible micro-tread pattern
  engineered for the wave's semi-rigid surface), scuffed from repeated use
- a coiled, load-rated tether spooled at her hip, connected to a small anchor-piton
  set — deployed from her suit, never separately handheld
- fine mineral-salt residue, pale white, crusted into every seam and joint from
  repeated contact with the wave's surface
- a small, hand-tied length of colored cord knotted around her left wrist — a
  personal talisman, not equipment
- only faint, aged pale cyan-white accent lines rather than bright glowing lines
- a fully transparent, seamless helmet always revealing her complete face, with
  faint breath-condensation near the seal and a light spray-mist residue often
  visible on the outer glass
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY OF HANDHELD TOOLS — no wands, scanners, or hammers. All
  readings come from a single heavily scratched, well-used wrist display on her
  left forearm. Her hands are instead used directly for climbing, gripping the
  wave's semi-rigid surface with her gauntlets — this is the one film in the series
  where her hands are her primary tool, by design.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: A climber's deliberate economy — she tests every grip before
committing weight, moves in slow, controlled sequences, and has a habit of pressing
her palm flat against a surface for a beat before trusting it. Calm under exertion,
breathing audible and visible as fog but never panicked.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — RESEARCH VESSEL _SÜKÛNET_

```
HULL LENGTH: 290 meters, stem to stern. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A hybrid atmospheric-hover and shallow-submersible research vessel,
designed to hold stationary position above open water using broad, flat
repulsor-stabilizer pontoons rather than traditional landing struts — built
specifically for Lentos's water-world conditions.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a broad, flattened central hull, 130 meters long, housing crew quarters and the
  rheology lab, with rows of small reinforced observation ports, each approximately
  1.4 meters in diameter, running its length as a scale ruler
- two long, low stabilizer pontoons extending fore and aft beneath the hull, each
  100 meters long, designed to rest flush against the ocean's semi-rigid surface
  without breaking it
- two main engine/repulsor clusters, each 9 meters in diameter, mounted at the
  stern
- four retractable mooring spikes, 12 meters long, capable of anchoring lightly
  into the wave's semi-rigid surface without damaging its structure
- one forward observation window, 2.2 meters high by 4 meters wide, positioned at
  the hull's bow

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 100m stabilizer
pontoons resting on the ocean's flat surface for scale contrast, the 2.2m
observation window with a human figure inside at correct proportion, Deniz's 1.65m
figure against the hull, or the ship silhouetted against the towering 340-meter
wave face in the background for direct scale contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate perspective, correct
engineering proportions, realistic scale relationships — the ship must never appear
miniature, toy-like, or like a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: A single frame never simultaneously demands (a) the full 290m
hull in view AND (b) a legible human face. Always split into separate shots
(A = wide hull shot, B = medium window/character shot).
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / WAVE-SCALE shots → 32mm Cooke anamorphic
- MEDIUM / EMOTIONAL / CHARACTER / CLIMBING shots → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (frozen droplet, surface texture detail) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in, pull-back, tilt-up,
tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow vertical crane (reserved for climbing
sequence, Scenes 6-8).

FRAMING RULE: no contradictory framing in one prompt (never "show the entire ship"
and "push toward her face" together — see Shot-Split Rule).

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: every video prompt is exactly 10 seconds, single continuous shot.

CONTINUITY LOCK [LESSON 1 — CRITICAL]: SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE. Camera must
not teleport, jump-cut, reset position, or change angle suddenly. Every frame flows
directly from the previous. Each scene generated as SEPARATE 10-second video —
NEVER combined into a single longer video.

MOTION SPEED [LESSON 12]: All movement at REAL-TIME natural human speed unless
slow-motion explicitly specified. No unmotivated slow-motion drift.
```

## 🏃 MOTION BIBLE (Micro-Behavior Catalog)

```
Every shot includes at least one of the following, drawn from this fixed catalog:

  it (testing the wave's semi-rigid grip)
- visible, audible-implied breathing, heavier and more labored during the climb

  pass
- a brief pause mid-climb, glancing down to gauge height, then back up
- fingers flexing inside her gauntlets before a new grip
- a slow, controlled exhale before a difficult reach or transition
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity: 1.15g fixed throughout.
- The wave's surface must always be depicted as genuinely load-bearing and
  climbable at human timescale (supporting boots, hand-grips, anchor-spikes) while
  its overall bulk motion remains nearly imperceptible moment-to-moment — visible
  only as a slow, cumulative positional change between the film's opening and
  closing shots.
- Frozen-looking ripples, standing foam, and suspended airborne droplets on the
  wave's leading edge are physically real, held in place by extreme viscosity —
  never CGI sparkle or magic light effects.
- The open, flat ocean away from the wave dissipates surface disturbance (footsteps,
  ship presence) almost immediately, distinct from the wave's own semi-rigid
  behavior.
- Human-to-environment scale is always internally consistent: Deniz is 1.65m; the
  ship is 290m; the wave face is 340m tall; stabilizer pontoons are 100m.

WEIGHT & CONTACT [LESSON 4]: Visible weight on every surface contact. Footfalls
create measurable depressions on the semi-rigid surface. No floating, no sliding,
no gliding walks. Rigid skeletal proportions — no jello, no rubber-banding.

GRAVITY VECTOR [LESSON 15]: On the vertical wave face, the character's body must
LEAN INTO the surface with visible climbing effort. Arms reach up, legs push, torso
angled. NEVER stand upright on a vertical surface. Hair and equipment hang DOWNWARD.

DYNAMIC WATER [LESSON 14]: The open ocean must show continuous subtle micro-motion
(rolling swells, shifting reflections). The wave face shows suspended-but-real
particles. NEVER frozen, static, or gel-like beyond the established viscosity rule.

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: Ship thruster exhaust must be VOLUMETRIC with
real turbulence, heat distortion, and directional flow. Never solid cones or flat
static shapes.

CONTACT INTEGRITY [LESSON 11]: Hands/fingers NEVER merge with wave surface. Clear
visible boundary at all contact points.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: Background ocean, wave topology, and sky remain
STATIC and consistent unless explicitly described as moving.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts, no transitions, no dissolves, no fades, no jump cuts, no montage, no
editing, no multiple camera angles, no scene changes, no camera teleportation.
No shaky camera, no video game chase-cam. Not miniature. Not toy-like. No CGI look,
no Unreal Engine aesthetic, no video game aesthetic. No airbrushed or plastic skin,
no smooth skin under colored light. No perfect symmetry. No oversaturated colors.
No bloom, no glow filter, no soft focus glow, no dreamy haze. No crashing/breaking
wave motion (the wave's bulk motion is nearly imperceptible — never depict fast,
Earth-normal wave dynamics). No frozen/static open ocean — subtle micro-motion
must always be present. No gel-like or muddy water appearance. No solid/static
thruster flames or exhaust cones — must be volumetric. No handheld tools in her
hands. No rubber-banding or jello effect on limbs or water. No hand-surface merging
— fingers NEVER melt into the wave surface. No floating heads, no disconnected
neck joints. No unmotivated slow-motion drift. No standing upright on vertical
surfaces — gravity vector must be correct. No face morphing behind visor. No text
overlay, no title card, no credits, no AI-generated text on displays, no
watermarks, no logos.

VISOR PERSISTENCE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE regardless of head angle.

FACE LOCK [LESSON 16]: Face maintains IDENTICAL bone structure throughout. No
morphing, no feature drift behind the visor glass.

SUIT CONTINUITY [LESSON 8]: All equipment matches CHARACTER BIBLE EXACTLY.

NO AI TEXT [LESSON 13]: Wrist display shows abstract patterns or blank glow only.
NEVER readable or garbled text, letters, numbers, or symbols.
```

## 🎶 MÜZİK PROMPTU — FİLMİN TEK GLOBAL TEMASI

```
Genre: Slow-tidal cinematic ambient, viscous drone, endurance-climb score.

Instrumentation: Deep, extremely sustained sub-bass drones (the ocean's immense,
patient mass), slow, heavy, low string glissandi that bend almost imperceptibly
across long durations (the wave's true bulk motion), sparse, resonant low bell
tones marking key discoveries, a labored, rhythmic breathing-adjacent percussive
texture (entering for the climb, Scenes 5-9), and a wide, cold string swell
(entering fully at the crest overhang, Scene 8.5).

Mood: Patient, monumental exertion — not urgency, but the specific tension of
working hard against something that isn't in a hurry at all. Awe at forces too
slow for human perception to fully register as dangerous, yet undeniably powerful.

Tempo: 40-50 BPM at rest, subtly increasing in perceived density (not tempo) during
the climb through denser layering rather than faster beats — the wave itself never
"speeds up."

Dynamic arc: Opens with a vast, glacial drone (Scene 0) — introduces the bent-stake
motif as a low, dissonant string stab (Scene 4.5) — the climb begins with labored
breathing-percussion layered over the sustained drone (Scenes 5-6) — grows denser
and higher in register as she ascends (Scene 7-8) — swells into the widest, coldest
string texture of the film beneath the crest overhang (Scene 8.5) — reaches full,
triumphant but restrained resolution at the summit (Scene 9) — resolves on descent
with the original glacial drone returning, subtly altered, acknowledging the wave
has moved on without needing her (Scene 10).

Reference feel: The patient, glacial string work of Jóhann Jóhannsson's "Arrival"
crossed with the physical, breathing-adjacent tension of a Ólafur Arnalds
performance piece — music that works hard without ever hurrying.

No lyrics. Sustained drone, glacial glissandi, and labored percussive texture carry
the emotion. One continuous evolving piece, roughly 2.5 minutes total, mapped to
the 15-shot arc above.
```

# THE SLOW TIDE

### "Saniyede bir santimetre ilerleyen bir dalga da bir dalgadır. Ve durdurulamaz."

Tek karakter (Dr. Deniz Kaya), sıfır kesme, sürekli akış — gezegen boyutunda, aşırı viskoz bir okyanusun 340 metrelik dalgasını tırmanma keşfi.

Bu belge tam **AI Production Bible** formatında yazılmıştır. Filmin fiziksel temeli gerçek bir olguya dayanır — Dünya'daki "zift damlama deneyi" (pitch-drop experiment) gibi, insan zaman ölçeğinde katı gibi davranan ama gezegen zaman ölçeğinde gerçekten akan bir sıvı. Viral merkezi aksiyon — **dalgayı tırmanmak** — Sahne 5-9 arasında ana omurga olarak işlenmiş, iki ek viral ara sahne (**4.5:** bükülen ölçüm direği, **8.5:** kıvrılan tepenin altında durmak) eklenmiştir. Gemi sahneleri (1, 10) ölçek tutarlılığı için A/B shot'larına bölünmüştür; karakterin elinde hiçbir zaman ayrı bir tutulan alet yoktur — tırmanış bu filmde bilinçli olarak _elleriyle_ yapılır, bu da onu serideki diğer filmlerden ayıran özel bir kural istisnasıdır.
