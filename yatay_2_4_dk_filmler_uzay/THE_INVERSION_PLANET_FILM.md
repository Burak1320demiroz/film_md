# 📖 BÖLÜM 1 — THE BIBLES

## 🌍 WORLD BIBLE — GEZEGEN: ASCENSA

```
CORE CONCEPT: Ascensa orbits inside the shredded ring of a long-dead companion
dwarf star — a band of ultra-dense degenerate-matter fragments circling close
overhead. Roughly every 9 hours, orbital resonance carries a dense cluster low
enough that its combined gravitational field briefly overwhelms and locally
INVERTS the planet's own surface gravity along a moving band, 4 kilometers
wide, that sweeps across the ground at a fixed, visible, measurable pace —
exactly like a weather front. Nothing here is magic — it is superposed
gravitational fields taken to a planetary extreme, the same principle behind
tidal forces, scaled up until gravity itself briefly reverses direction.

CRITICAL WORLD RULE: Gravity inversion is not an explosion and not chaos.
It is a slow, physically consistent DIRECTION CHANGE. Everything unanchored
drifts away from the ground at a steady, measurable rate — the same way a
dropped object falls at a steady rate, only upward. This slow, buoyant,
almost graceful quality — not a violent launch — is the film's entire visual
thesis.

THE INVERSION CYCLE (fixed structure, every 9-hour cycle):
- T-15:00 APPROACH: the horizon shimmers with a faint vertical heat-haze-like
  distortion — the Front, still distant, visibly advancing. Loose dust and
  pebbles begin to tremble and hop centimeters off the ground.
- T-3:00 WARNING: native Anchor Beetles dig into the soil across the plain;
  the shimmer wall is now close enough to distort the horizon behind it.
- T-0:00 CONTACT: the Front — a sharp, vertical, faintly violet-white
  shimmer wall roughly 600m tall — sweeps across a given point. The instant
  it passes overhead, gravity at that point inverts.
- T+0:00 to T+6:00 THE FLOATING WINDOW: full inversion within the zone.
  Rock, dust, water, and unanchored life drift slowly upward into a
  suspended layer 200–400m above the original ground — the SKY-REEF —
  where native Void Rays feed on the debris every cycle brings them.
- T+6:00 THE SLAM: the field collapses instantly. Everything not
  permanently anchored falls back down, experiencing a brief REBOUND
  gravity spike before settling to baseline.
- T+6:05 SETTLE: baseline gravity restored, dust and debris raining down
  and resettling, silence returning within seconds.

GROUND LAYER: dry, rust-bronze rock plains, cut by natural obsidian-black
outcrops called ANCHOR STONES — dense enough that their own mass resists
inversion entirely. Anything drilled, bolted, or physically attached to an
Anchor Stone remains grounded throughout a Floating Window. These are the
only safe places to be caught inside the Front.

SKY-REEF (temporary, 200–400m up, exists only during the Floating Window):
suspended boulders slowly rotating in place, drifting curtains of dust,
and — most strikingly — spheres of floating water, torn up from puddles
and shallow pools, wobbling and slowly merging into larger spheres exactly
as liquid behaves in true microgravity. Void Rays glide unhurried between
them, feeding.

ATMOSPHERE & GRAVITY: 1.05g baseline, thin dry atmosphere, a permanent
dusty bronze haze at the horizon from the ring debris overhead, visible
as a faint pale band across the sky at all times.

SOUND: Baseline — dry wind, distant creak of settling rock. Approach —
a rising, resonant subsonic hum, felt more than heard, plus the dry
skitter of trembling pebbles. Contact — a sharp, ringing tonal sweep as
the Front passes, like a struck bell decaying. Floating Window — near
total silence but for the slow groan of tether cable and a faint,
airy hum from the Void Rays' bodies. The Slam — a single massive
impact-crack of returning debris, then abrupt full silence.
```

## 👤 CHARACTER BIBLE — DR. KAYA UÇAN

```
IDENTITY: 29-year-old Turkish gravitational field researcher. First person
to survive a full tethered crossing of a live Inversion Front — riding out
an entire Floating Window anchored in open ground rather than sheltering.

FACE (photographed, not rendered): Sun-weathered light-olive skin with
real, visible pore texture, windburned cheeks, a faded pale burn scar
along the left jaw from an early anchor-rig malfunction, no airbrushing,
no plastic smoothness. Sharp, angular features, dark brows, deep-set
hazel eyes with visible catchlight and a habitual narrowed, calculating
look. Chapped lips pressed in concentration. A real, capable, weathered
human face.

HAIR: Dark brown, cropped short and bound tight in a low practical knot —
short enough to read clearly whether it is hanging with gravity or
drifting against it, by design (see GRAVITY VECTOR RULE — EXTENDED).

BUILD: Lean, compact, climber's build, 1.68m, strong forearms, low center
of gravity.

SUIT: Matte bronze-grey "Anchor Suit" with cyan-amber accent piping that
shifts brightness with anchor status, heavily mission-worn:
- a fixed mechanical tether reel unit mounted flush against her lower
  back/hip — NEVER a handheld device, always integrated into the suit
- weighted ballast panels sewn into the thighs and boots, visibly thicker
  and more rigid than the rest of the suit fabric
- a fully transparent, seamless visor ALWAYS revealing her complete face,
  dust-filmed on the ground, faintly fogged during the Floating Window
- a single scratched wrist display on her left forearm showing ONLY a
  pulsing amber ring that tightens and quickens as the Front approaches,
  shifts to a slow blue pulse during the Floating Window, and flashes a
  single sharp white pulse at the Slam — NEVER readable digits or text
- HANDS ALWAYS EMPTY — no handheld tools, scanners, or weapons

VISOR PERSISTENCE RULE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE in every frame
regardless of head angle. Never disappears or merges with the face.
Reflections must match actual environment lighting, including the
violet-white Front shimmer and the Void Rays' bioluminescence.

SUIT CONTINUITY RULE [LESSON 8]: Every detail above remains IDENTICAL
across all scenes — no design changes, no color shifts, no added/removed
equipment between shots.

CONTACT INTEGRITY RULE [LESSON 11]: When gripping ANY surface (anchor
line, rock, Anchor Stone, tether clip), her hands/fingers must maintain
COMPLETE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY — clear visible boundary at all contact
points, never merging or melting into what she touches. Head remains
physically connected to neck/torso at all times.

GRAVITY VECTOR RULE — EXTENDED [LESSON 15, inverted case]: During
baseline and Slam phases, her hair, loose suit elements, and any dust on
her suit hang and move with NORMAL downward gravity. During the Floating
Window, the local gravity vector points UPWARD/OUTWARD — her hair,
loose fabric, and dust on her suit must drift and hang AWAY from the
ground, matching that new vector exactly, consistent with every other
loose object in the same frame. Her body orientation must read as
physically pulled taut against her tether — never neutral, weightless
floating with no visible tension.

MOVEMENT SIGNATURE: Quick, precise plant-and-lock footfalls on approach;
one hand routinely checks the tether clip at her hip; a sharp, controlled
exhale immediately before locking an anchor clip; a habit of tilting her
head to gauge the Front's shimmer against the horizon.
```

## 🚀 SHIP BIBLE — ANCHOR VESSEL _DEMİR_

```
HULL LENGTH: 70 meters. Always anchored to this figure or a visible
reference object.

DESIGN LOGIC: A heavy-duty ground-drilling anchor vessel, designed to
physically root itself into bedrock before a Front arrives and remain
completely grounded and unaffected throughout an Inversion Cycle. It
never operates during an active Floating Window — by design, since
nothing not drilled into an Anchor Stone can resist inversion.

STRUCTURE (in countable parts, never one smooth mass):
- a low, dense central hull, 40m long, matte iron-bronze with dark
  reinforced plating, a single row of 1m viewports along its length as
  a scale ruler
- four massive drill-anchor legs, each terminating in a spiral drill
  spike driven 30m into bedrock, visibly locked in place
- two stabilizing thruster pods, 5m diameter, mounted at the stern, used
  only for repositioning between cycles, never during an active Front
- one forward cockpit window, 2.2m high by 3.8m wide

SCALE ANCHORS REQUIRED IN EVERY SHIP SHOT: at least one of — the 1m
viewports as a ruler along the hull, the 2.2m cockpit window with Kaya
visible inside at correct proportion, Kaya's 1.68m figure beside a
drill-anchor leg, or the hull silhouetted against the approaching Front
wall for direct scale contrast.

MANDATORY QUALIFIER IN EVERY SHIP PROMPT: "physically accurate
perspective, correct engineering proportions, realistic scale
relationships — the ship must never appear miniature, toy-like, or like
a compact spacecraft."

SHOT-SPLIT RULE: Never show the full 70m hull AND a legible human face in
the same frame.

GIANT-CREATURE SCALE RULE: The same qualifier applies to the Void Rays
(35m wingspan). Every prompt featuring one states its exact scale and
includes a human or ship reference where physically possible.
```

## 🎥 CAMERA BIBLE

```
LENS LOCK — exactly one lens per shot, never mixed:
- WIDE / ESTABLISHING / SHIP / SKY-REEF SCALE / FRONT WALL → 32mm Cooke
  anamorphic
- MEDIUM / CHARACTER / ACTION / TETHER TENSION → 75mm
- EXTREME MACRO (floating water sphere surface, dust texture, tether
  cable strands) → 100mm macro

DISTANCE LOCK: every prompt states camera distance in meters explicitly.

MOVEMENT VOCABULARY (single continuous move per shot): push-in,
pull-back, tilt-up, tilt-down, slow orbit, fixed lockoff, slow vertical
track (reserved for the Floating Window ascent/descent shots).

FIXED CLIP LENGTH: every video prompt is exactly 10 seconds, single
continuous shot.

CONTINUITY LOCK [LESSON 1 — CRITICAL]: SINGLE UNBROKEN CAMERA TAKE. No
teleport, no jump-cut, no reset, no angle change, no transitions. Every
frame flows directly from the previous. Each scene = SEPARATE 10-second
video. NEVER combined.

MOTION SPEED RULE [LESSON 12]: All character and object movement occurs
at physically consistent speed for its phase — real-time human speed on
the ground, and a slow, steady drift rate during the Floating Window
(never rocket-fast, never dreamlike slow-motion unless explicitly the
Slam's brief rebound-heavy beat).
```

## 💡 LIGHTING BIBLE (per phase — named explicitly, never just "cinematic lighting")

```
BASELINE / PRE-APPROACH: warm bronze late-afternoon key light, long
hard-edged shadows on rust-colored rock, pale dust haze band on the
horizon from the ring overhead.

APPROACH (T-15:00 to T-0:00): baseline light with a vertical band of
faint violet-blue refraction where the Front distorts the air, growing
brighter and sharper as it nears.

CONTACT (T-0:00): a sharp, thin violet-white line of light at the Front's
leading edge as it sweeps overhead, a brief band of shadow directly
beneath it.

THE FLOATING WINDOW (T+0:00 to T+6:00): overhead light scattered and
refracted through slowly drifting dust and floating water spheres,
producing shifting caustic-like light patterns on suspended rock
surfaces — the light moves because the translucent debris moves, never
because the environment itself warps. The Void Rays' pale
bioluminescent undersides add a sharp, directional blue-green glow to
nearby floating rock, with clear falloff, never soft bloom.

THE SLAM (T+6:00): a single sharp white flash as the field collapses,
then an immediate hard-edged return to baseline light with heavy,
short-lived dust-kick shadow.

AFTERMATH: calm, dust-hazed gold late light, soft settling particulate
catching the low sun, shadows returning to their normal hard edges.

ANTI-BLOOM DIRECTIVE [LESSON 3]: All bioluminescent and Front-shimmer
light sources emit SHARP, DIRECTIONAL light with clear falloff — never a
soft, diffused, dreamy glow. High contrast between lit and unlit areas
at all times. Skin retains visible pore texture and realistic shadow
under every light condition, including inside the Sky-Reef.
```

## ⚙️ PHYSICS BIBLE

```
- Gravity baseline: 1.05g.
- Approach phase: gravity remains at baseline; only trembling, incremental
  loosening of small unanchored particles signals the coming change.
- Floating Window: net gravity vector inverts to approximately -0.4g
  (a gentle upward pull) within the 4km-wide moving zone. Unanchored mass
  drifts upward at a slow, steady, measurable rate — comparable to a
  released balloon, never an explosive launch.
- The Slam: the field collapses instantly; falling objects and Kaya
  herself experience a brief REBOUND gravity spike of approximately 1.6g
  for roughly 2 seconds before settling back to 1.05g baseline — a hard,
  fast, heavy landing, not a gentle float back down.
- Anchor Stones and anything drilled or bolted into them are entirely
  unaffected by inversion and remain grounded throughout.
- Human-to-environment scale: Kaya 1.68m; ship Demir 70m; Anchor Beetles
  0.4m; Dust Hares 0.3m; Void Rays 35m wingspan; Front wall approx. 600m
  tall, 4km wide, advancing at a fixed, visible ground speed.

GRAVITY VECTOR RULE — EXTENDED [LESSON 15]: Every loose or unanchored
object in a given shot obeys the SAME local gravity vector at the SAME
moment — no object may hang or drift in a different direction from any
other object sharing the frame and the same inversion state. Vector
direction changes only at T-0:00 (down→up) and T+6:00 (up→down), never
gradually and never inconsistently between objects.

DYNAMIC FLOATING WATER RULE [extends LESSON 14 to inverted gravity]:
Water torn from puddles during the Floating Window behaves as real
microgravity fluid — forming wobbling, imperfect spheres that slowly
drift, deform slightly on contact with each other, and merge into larger
spheres exactly as true zero-g water does. Water must NEVER appear
frozen, gel-like, or perfectly geometric, and must never simply hang
static in the air.

VOLUMETRIC PARTICLES [LESSON 10]: All dust, debris, and drill-exhaust
must behave as VOLUMETRIC, physically accurate fluid/particle
simulations — dispersing, reacting to the current gravity vector, never
freezing, never flat, never painted-on.

COLLISION INTEGRITY [LESSON 17]: ALL objects, creatures, and the
character obey RIGID BODY COLLISION. Drifting boulders never pass
through each other or through Kaya. Ship drill-spikes rest IN bedrock,
never phasing through it. Tether cable never clips through her body or
the rock it's anchored to.

CREATURE LOCOMOTION [LESSON 18]: Void Rays move with slow, deliberate,
articulated wing-strokes, their full 35m wingspan flexing with visible
joint motion — never gliding as a rigid, motionless silhouette. Anchor
Beetles dig with visible articulated leg motion, never sliding into the
ground. Dust Hares' limbs remain visibly kicking/flailing while airborne
— never a limp, sliding blob.

WEIGHT & CONTACT [LESSON 4]: Visible weight and surface reaction at
every ground contact during baseline and post-Slam phases. No floating,
no sliding, ever, outside the explicitly inverted zone.

CONTACT INTEGRITY [LESSON 11]: Hands/fingers NEVER merge with any
surface, including floating rock and water spheres.

ENVIRONMENT STABILITY [LESSON 6]: All rock and terrain geometry remains
STATIC and rigid. Only POSITION changes (via the gravity vector);
individual rock and boulder SHAPES never warp, melt, or morph.

REFLECTION ACCURACY [LESSON 19]: Any reflection in the visor, in a
floating water sphere, or on wet rock must accurately match the object
being reflected in shape, scale, color, and angle, updating consistently
with camera movement.
```

## 🚫 NEGATIVE BIBLE (appended to every prompt)

```
No cuts, no transitions, no dissolves, no fades, no jump cuts, no
montage, no editing, no multiple camera angles, no scene changes, no
camera teleportation. No shaky camera, no video game chase-cam. Not
miniature, not toy-like. No CGI look, no Unreal Engine aesthetic, no
video game aesthetic. No airbrushed or plastic skin, no smooth skin
under colored light. No unnatural perfect facial symmetry. No
oversaturated colors. No bloom, no glow filter, no soft focus glow, no
dreamy haze, no lens flare on Front shimmer or Void Ray bioluminescence.

No explosive or rocket-like launch during the Floating Window — all
upward motion is a slow, steady drift. No arbitrary floating — every
unanchored object in frame must share the exact same gravity vector at
the exact same moment. No inconsistent drift direction between objects
sharing a shot. No frozen, gel-like, or static floating water — it must
wobble, deform, and merge like real microgravity fluid.

No object phasing or clipping through any other solid object. No hand-
surface merging. No terrain melting, warping, or shape morphing. No
frozen particles or static dust/exhaust. No rubber-banding or jello
effect on limbs. No face morphing behind the visor. No floating heads,
no disconnected neck joints. No unmotivated slow-motion drift outside
the Slam's brief rebound beat. No warped or mismatched reflections. No
handheld tools in her hands. No creature sliding — Void Rays and Anchor
Beetles use articulated limb/wing motion only.

No readable or unreadable text, digits, letters, or symbols on the
wrist display or any surface — abstract pulsing light only. No text
overlay, no title card, no credits, no subtitles, no watermarks, no
logos, no on-screen text of any kind.

VISOR PERSISTENCE [LESSON 5]: Visor ALWAYS VISIBLE regardless of head
angle.
FACE LOCK [LESSON 16]: Face maintains IDENTICAL bone structure and
proportions throughout.
SUIT CONTINUITY [LESSON 8]: Equipment matches CHARACTER BIBLE EXACTLY
across all scenes.
NO AI TEXT [LESSON 13]: Wrist display shows abstract pulsing light only,
NEVER text — this rule is applied strictly from Scene 0 onward, with no
exceptions.
```

### "Her 9 saatte bir, gökyüzü yeri çağırıyor. Ve 6 dakika boyunca, hiçbir şey aşağı düşmeyi hatırlamıyor."

Tek karakter (Dr. Kaya Uçan), sıfır kesme, sürekli akış — yerçekiminin
periyodik olarak tersine döndüğü bir gezegende, tek bir tether hattına
bağlı olarak 6 dakikalık bir "Yüzen Pencere"yi hayatta atlatma yarışı.

Bu belge tam **AI Production Bible** formatında, **13 sahne** olacak
şekilde yazılmıştır: **Sahne 0-2** yaklaşan Cephe'ye karşı yarış,
**Sahne 3** yerçekimi tersine döndüğü an, **Sahne 4-6** Gökada Resifi'nde
asılı kalma ve dev Gökvatozları'yla karşılaşma, **Sahne 7-8** geri sayım

(**3.5:** yükselen şelale/yüzen su küreleri, **6.5:** çaresizce
tekleyerek yükselen Toz Tavşanları) eklendi — ikisi de gerçek mikro-yerçekimi
fizik referanslarına (ISS su küresi görüntüleri) bilinçli olarak yaslanıyor.
