## STEP 3 — CAMERA LANGUAGE PATTERN

- What the camera DOES: The camera begins as a passive, neutral observer during baseline routine, maintaining distance and spatial stability. Upon system breach, it becomes intrusive and reactive, plunging into tight, hazardous spaces alongside the crew.
- Point of view: Coverage initially favors an objective, third-person perspective (shots 1–5), then shifts entirely to favor the female engineer's subjectively claustrophobic point of view during the conduit repair (shots 9–11).
- Recurring setups: Circular frame-within-a-frame motifs are returned to twice (the circular viewing porthole in shot 1 and the circular entrance to the 40cm crawlway in shot 9).
- Scale strategy: Progressively tightens from MS down to ECU (01:48 → 02:55), directly tracking the narrowing physical space and heightened bodily stakes.

## STEP 4 — THE MOST AUTHORED DECISION

Shot 10 (02:22–02:40): Placing a wide-angle lens deep inside the 40cm conduit rather than cutting back to wide reaction shots outside at the control console. By refusing to cut away to safety, the camera forces the viewer into suffocating spatial proximity with the character, anchoring the entire crisis to physical human endurance rather than technical jargon.
