## STEP 4 — THE MOST AUTHORED DECISION

Shot 2 (00:09–00:20). During a tense argument about defensive strategy, standard coverage would dictate cutting back and forth between the male and female soldiers speaking. Instead, the director drops the camera low and places the resting alien child entirely out of focus in the extreme foreground, while the speaking adults are kept sharp in the background. This deliberate framing visually anchors the soldiers' tactical debate over futility to the literal, helpless reason they are fighting, forcing the audience to look past the innocent child to see the exhausted defenders.
