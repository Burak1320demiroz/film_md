## STEP 4 — THE MOST AUTHORED DECISION

Shot 7 (00:26–00:41): Reverse dolly reveal framed by cots. Instead of cutting between the searching astronaut and the dead occupants of the room, the camera slowly dollies backward down the corridor. The shot begins on the distant astronaut crouching in a hatchway with a flashlight, and as the camera pulls back, two cots with motionless bodies enter the extreme left and right foreground. This decision uses foreground occlusion and depth of field to force a thematic connection: the living searcher is visually framed and trapped between the dead, conveying mounting dread and tragedy in a single, uninterrupted camera movement without relying on reverse-cut edits.
