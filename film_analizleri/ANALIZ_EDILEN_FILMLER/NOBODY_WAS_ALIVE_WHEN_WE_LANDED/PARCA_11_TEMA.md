## STEP 5 — THEMATIC VERDICT OF THIS SEGMENT

Underneath its plot of cryo-awakening and descent preparation, this segment explores the conflict between biological fragility and technological encapsulation. Awakening from stasis is depicted not as a glorious rebirth, but as a painful thawing process where shivering humans collapse onto wet metallic floors (01:36–01:49). When orbital scans reveal that the target planet lacks life signals ("BIOSIG 0", 02:27) and navigation is questionable ("WRONG", 02:08), the crew must immediately abandon their organic vulnerability and lock themselves into rigid, motorized armor (02:57–03:12). The segment argues that human survival in deep space requires surrendering natural physical vulnerability to cold, artificial machinery.
