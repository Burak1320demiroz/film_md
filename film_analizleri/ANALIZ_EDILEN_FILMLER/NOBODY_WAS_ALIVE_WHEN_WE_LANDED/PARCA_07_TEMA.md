## STEP 5 — THEMATIC VERDICT OF THIS SEGMENT

This segment contrasts grand technological ambition with fragile human reality. The characters exist in an engineered space governed by arbitrary systems (mirrored in the broken card deck). When the system fails, technology does not save them; desperate, traumatizing physical labor does. The ship acts as both protector and immediate threat, forcing human bodies into agonizingly tight, freezing spaces to maintain a fragile illusion of control over the void.
