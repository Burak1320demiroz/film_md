Kütüphanedeki hiçbir filmde yok ve tek bakışta "bu ne?" sorusunu doğuruyor.
Ama S42 geniş bir çekim, içinde insan yok — kapak kılavuzu ise yüzün
çerçevenin **%60'ını** kaplamasını istiyor (§2: yüzlü kapaklar %25-40 daha

**Çözüm: S43'ün yüzü + S42'nin arka planı.** S43 Kaptan'ın novum'u anladığı
kare; S42 anladığı şeyin kendisi. Kapak ikisini tek karede birleştiriyor —
filmde art arda gelen iki sahne, kapakta üst üste. Vaat edilen görüntü
filmde gerçekten var, sadece iki karede duruyor.

### 📸 IMAGE PROMPT

```
Photorealistic cinematic film cover image, EXACTLY 1280x720 pixels, 16:9
landscape format. NO TEXT ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE. YouTube thumbnail
optimized — maximum impact at 120 pixels wide.

SUBJECT — FILLS THE FRAME:
A 41-year-old woman pilot in a pressure suit, extreme close-up. Her head
and shoulders fill at least 60% of the total frame area, positioned in the
RIGHT THIRD of the frame, her body angled slightly left. HER EYES SIT ON
THE UPPER THIRD LINE. She is NOT looking at the camera — she looks left
and slightly down, at something in the lower left of the frame, and the
viewer wants to know what she is looking at.

Her face, seen through a FULLY TRANSPARENT SEAMLESS HELMET VISOR with no
tint and no obstruction: light olive skin with visible pore texture and
real subsurface scattering, NO airbrushing, NO smoothing. Sharp jawline,
narrow lips, dark grey-green eyes. An OLD PALE WHITE SCAR crosses her LEFT
EYEBROW and splits it in two. Two horizontal lines on her forehead. No
makeup. Dark brown hair in a tight low bun at the back, distinct grey
strands at the temples.

Her expression: QUIET RECOGNITION — the face of a competent, exhausted
person who has just understood something that cannot be undone. Not
screaming, not panicking. Lips closed and slightly tightened, eyes still
and wide open with sharp catchlights, one eyebrow fractionally raised. The
emotion must read instantly at thumbnail scale.

She wears a dark grey-blue pressure suit with WORN ORANGE reinforcement
panels at the shoulder, and three small abstract indicator marks on the
chest — a slow blue pulse, an amber bar, a green dot. No numbers, no
letters, no symbols of any kind on the suit. Fine grey dust sits on the
shoulder fabric and on the outside of the visor, with two wiped arc marks
cleared across the glass.

BACKGROUND — THE HOOK:
Behind her and to the LEFT, occupying the left third and the lower left of
the frame: a spacecraft HALF GROWN OUT OF THE GROUND. Its upper half is a
complete, perfectly formed cargo ship hull with every panel line in place;
its lower half is unfinished and merges continuously into the dark basalt
plain, hull becoming rock with no seam and no crater, no impact debris. The
ship is MATTE BONE-GREY STONE throughout — a single dead colour, no paint,
no stripes, no markings, no metal sheen, no glow, no lights. It is clearly
NOT crashed and NOT landed: it is growing upward out of the ground like a
tooth. It is thrown slightly soft by shallow depth of field and desaturated
to roughly 70% so it never competes with her face.
Far behind it, small and low on the horizon: two more identical grey ship
silhouettes in a row, suggesting a field that continues out of frame.

SETTING:
A dark basalt plain under a pale grey-green sky, thin atmosphere. A single
large pale moon disc is faintly visible high in the sky, low contrast. A
small orange dwarf star sits low and out of frame to the right.

COLOR — TWO COLORS ONLY:
- WARM ORANGE-AMBER: dominant on her face, her visor highlights and the
  orange reinforcement panels of her suit — this is the LIVING colour
- COOL DESATURATED GREY-GREEN: the sky, the plain and the entire grown
  ship — this is the DEAD colour
NO other colors. NO blue sky, NO teal, NO purple, NO magenta, NO red.
The two-value contrast is the point: a warm living face against a cold
colourless copy. Increase final saturation by 20% so both survive at
thumbnail scale.

LIGHTING:
Two motivated sources. (1) The low orange dwarf star off frame right,
throwing WARM directional light across the right side of her face and a
warm rim along her helmet edge and shoulder. (2) The wide grey-green sky
acting as a cool ambient fill on the left side of her face and on the grown
ship. Shadows are SOFT-EDGED — this planet has atmosphere, hard vacuum
shadows are wrong. Clear directional falloff. 0% bloom, 0% glow filter,
0% soft focus, no lens flare, no sun star, no god rays, no volumetric beams.

COMPOSITION:
- Face occupies 60%+ of the frame, positioned in the right third
- Eyes on the upper third line
- 30-40% of the frame is clean, uncluttered sky — open negative space in
  the UPPER LEFT, reserved for the title added later in post
- LOWER-RIGHT CORNER completely empty — no face, no ship, no detail.
  YouTube's duration badge covers this zone.
- The grown ship must remain readable as a distinct silhouette against the
  sky even when the image is shrunk to 120 pixels wide

TECHNICAL:
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no subtitles
- Photorealistic, 35mm anamorphic film grain
- Sharp focus on the eyes — they are the sharpest element in the image
- The visor stays a visible distinct glass surface and never disappears
- Copy material is matte bone-grey stone — it never glows, never emits
  light, never shimmers, never carries the colour of an original
- All dust obeys 0.9g — it rests on surfaces, it never hovers or swirls
- Must survive the postage-stamp test: at 120 pixels wide both the emotion
  on the face and the half-buried ship must still read instantly
- No CGI aesthetic, no Unreal Engine look, no video game render
```

# 🎞️ MOTION POSTER (opsiyonel · 3 saniye · sessiz döngü)

**Kullanım:** YouTube "video önizleme", Instagram feed, X kartı.
**Kaynak:** KAPAK A'nın onaylanmış imajı, image-to-video.

### 🎥 VIDEO PROMPT

```
Animate this still image with EXTREMELY MINIMAL motion, 3 seconds, seamless
loop, silent. Single unbroken take, no cuts.

The ONLY motion permitted:
(1) A very slow push-in of about 3% toward the woman's face across the full
    3 seconds, constant speed, no easing at the ends.
(2) A single slow blink from the woman, beginning at second 2.0 and
    completing by second 2.4.
(3) Fine grey dust drifting downward through the lower left of the frame,
    slowly, obeying 0.9 gravity — the dust falls and settles, it never
    hovers, never swirls, never rises.

EVERYTHING ELSE IS STATIC. The grown ship does not move, does not grow, does
not shift by a single pixel — growth is never visible in real time. The sky
does not move. The woman's head does not turn. Her suit does not shift. The
visor stays a visible distinct glass surface for every frame and never
disappears. Her facial geometry, the scar on her left eyebrow and her hair
do not change between the first and the last frame.

No camera shake, no handheld motion, no parallax pop, no lens flare, no
bloom, no glow filter, no light pulses, no colour shift. No text, no
letters, no numbers, no watermark, no subtitles, no end card.
Photorealistic, 35mm anamorphic film grain. The last frame must match the
first frame closely enough to loop without a visible cut.
```
