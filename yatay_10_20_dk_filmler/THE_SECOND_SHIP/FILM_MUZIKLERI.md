# 1. 🚀 PARÇA 1 — TRANSIT · 0:00–3:00 · 48 BPM

```
İŞLEV: yolculuk, yalnızlık, ölçek. Filmin açılışı — iki kişilik eski
bir kargo gemisi, boş uzay, aşağıda karanlık bir gezegen. Parçanın
sonu VURULMAYA denk gelir: müzik olaya tepki vermez (özgün tasarımın
mirası), sadece 2:40'tan sonra incelerek yerini boşluğa bırakır —
atış müziksiz düşer, parça sessiz biter.
```

```
CUE 1 PROMPT — "TRANSIT"

Instrumental cinematic space-film cue, exactly 3 minutes, one
continuous piece, no fade in.

TEMPO AND KEY: 48 BPM, 4/4, G minor (Aeolian). Slow, vast, patient.
HARMONIC RULE: the perfect fifth G–D is never sounded; harmony always
slips to the fourth (C) or the sixth (E flat) and never resolves.

SOUND WORLD: a huge, lonely church pipe organ recorded in a vast dry
space, sustained low chords breathing very slowly; a sustained sub-bass
drone on G doubled at 49, 98 and 196 Hz under everything; a faint,
slow ticking pulse — like a quiet clock — appearing and disappearing;
sparse high organ tones like distant starlight.

STRUCTURE:
- 0:00 to 0:40 — sub drone and one low organ chord alone, almost
  static. Emptiness and scale.
- 0:40 to 1:40 — the organ begins a very slow chord cycle, one change
  every four bars. The quiet clock ticking enters underneath at 1:00,
  soft and regular. A single high organ line floats far above.
- 1:40 to 2:40 — the fullest passage: organ chords widen, a low cello
  doubles the bass line, the ticking continues. It grows in size, not
  in speed — majestic but cold, never heroic.
- 2:40 to 3:00 — everything thins out layer by layer until only the
  sub drone and the ticking remain, and the ticking stops at 2:55.
  The piece ends on the bare unresolved drone, quiet, no final chord.

MIX: wide and deep but dry — long organ room, no cathedral wash over
the low end. Nothing above 8 kHz is prominent. Mono-compatible lows.

STRICTLY EXCLUDE: lyrics, vocals, choir, drums, drum kit, taiko,
trailer hits, braams, risers, whooshes, electric guitar, synth leads,
EDM elements, major-key resolution, heroic fanfare, tempo changes,
fade-out endings.
```

Instrumental cinematic survival cue, exactly 3 minutes, one continuous

STRUCTURE:
- 0:00 to 0:30 — the sub drone with a lone soft synth pad; a slow

STRICTLY EXCLUDE: lyrics, vocals, choir, acoustic drum kit, snare,

CUE 5 PROMPT — "SAAT"

Instrumental cinematic time-pressure cue, exactly 3 minutes, one

STRUCTURE:
- 0:00 to 0:30 — the ticking arpeggio alone over the drone, quiet and

MIX: precise and clean; the ticking always audible on a phone
