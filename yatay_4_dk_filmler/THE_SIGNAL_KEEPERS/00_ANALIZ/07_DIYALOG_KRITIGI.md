## FINDINGS BY TEST

**T1 · FUNCTION.** Delete slot 1: the film loses the only evidence that the pulse
in the valley is answering a HUMAN thing, and K03's hook becomes a light that
blinks for no reason. Delete slot 2: K20 has nothing to destroy — the core comes
out of a machine that was merely humming, and the film's price evaporates. Both
`FUNCTIONAL`.
⚠️ Slot 2 was one step from `REDUNDANT`: an identical line, heard again, is only
information if something around it changed. Three things changed and all three are
written and audible — the line is now COMPLETE where it was torn, its source is in
the frame (the grille, 207 px), and the room it is addressed to is empty. Without
those three the milder label would not have been earned.
⭐ And the second hearing does something the first cannot: it turns the line from a
MESSAGE into an OBJECT. At 12.0 s it is a voice; at 173.0 s it is a thing standing
in a cradle that she is about to unplug.

**T2 · ON-THE-NOSE.** "If anyone hears this" is a condition. "we are here" is a
fact. No emotion is named anywhere: not afraid, not alone, not hopeful. `SUBTEXT`
both slots. No `THINKING-OUT-LOUD` — the line is addressed to someone who was not
there when it was recorded and is not there now. No `CLIMAX-DIRECT` is spent: the
climax (K21) and the closing image (K24) have no speech at all.

**T3 · EXPOSITION.** One clause per idea, nothing explained. No mission name, no
year, no distance, no instruction. The information arrives inside resistance in
the strongest form available: the line is a plea for contact, played TO the only
person who can make contact happen, by a machine she is about to silence in order
to make it happen. No `LECTURE`, no `AS-YOU-KNOW` (there is no second speaker), no
`OVERLOADED`.

**T4 · VOICE.** Swap test against the film's only other possible register
(MACHINE): a machine in this repo speaks agentless, terse and numeric — it does
not say "if anyone", and it does not say "we". Swap against the BEING: the being
has no speech at all, its only utterance is one tapped tone, and it does not write
or read anything. The line cannot move to either. `DISTINCT`.

**T5 · REGISTER FIT.** RECORDED HUMAN: pronoun-led, conditional, no numbers and no
units. `FIT`. No `MISPLACED-NUMBER` — the film's one number (41 years) is never
spoken. It lives in the release package and in the repaired panels on screen, and
the audience is left to not-count it.

**T6 · NATURALNESS.** Seven words, one sentence, one comma. "If anyone hears this"
is the spoken English of a person who does not expect to be heard; a written
English would have said "to whoever receives this transmission". No calque, no
hyper-formality, no relative clause. `PASS`.
⚠️ Origin note, recorded honestly: the owner's treatment wrote this line in
Turkish and wrote a SECOND line for the being ("We heard you."). The second line
was removed with the owner's approval — a speaking mouth on the being opens two
risks at once (lip sync and the synthetic stamp) and a third question the film
cannot answer (which language). The answer is SENT, not spoken. Deviation recorded
in 02_KONSEPT_VE_RISK.md §1 and in 00_HIKAYE_ONAYI ADAYLAR [D74 pattern].

**T7 · ECONOMY & SPEAKABILITY.** Read aloud with its pause: ~2.8 s. Slot 1 sits at
2.0 s inside a 10 s clip and runs out by ~6.0 s; slot 2 starts at 3.0 s and is
still running when the clip ends — that overrun is deliberate and it is what K19
and K20 stand on (the recording is playing UNDER the refusal and UNDER the
removal). No line fills a clip; one speaker; no overlap. `SPEAKABLE` both.

**T8 · PLACEMENT & IRONY.**
`FIRST-LINE` — the first words of the film arrive at 12.0 s, on the catalyst clip,
inside the 15 s ceiling the hook card sets. They land on a structural beat: the
panel starts pulsing and the voice breaks in on top of it. Not wasted.
`NO-IRONY-WINDOW` — and that is DECLARED, not missed: this film has no information
asymmetry (03 §1 · QA_SPEC A). The audience learns everything with her. The rubric's
irony test therefore has nothing to fail: the line is not hiding a fact from
anyone, it is a fact nobody in the frame can act on.
`EARLY-EMBODIMENT` — no speaking mouth anywhere in the film, 0 clips. Her mouth is
visible behind a clear visor in 7 face clips and is closed in all of them; the only
voice belongs to people who are not in the frame and never will be. The authority
of the voice is never spent, and it CANNOT be spent — the mouth that made it is
41 years and one planet away. ⭐ This is the film's strongest dialogue decision.

   lets her speak once, the recording loses its authority and the whole sound design
   (one sentence, twice, from nobody, in air that kills the treble) collapses. The
   tool-setting box in THE_SIGNAL_KEEPERS_URETIM_DEFTERI.md §2 is what enforces it
   ("konuşan ağız: kadraj dışı"), not a prompt sentence [D215].
