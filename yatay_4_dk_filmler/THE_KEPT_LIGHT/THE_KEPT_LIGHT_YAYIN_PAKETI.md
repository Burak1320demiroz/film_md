Everything he has ever thrown away is still there, hanging under the floor.

A disposal station hangs from the underside of a rock above a black hole.
One man works its bay. His job today is to cut a disused module free of six
clamps and let it go — and below the hatch, letting go is the one thing that
never finishes.

• Gravitational time dilation is measurable at human scale: two optical
  clocks raised 33 cm apart ticked at different rates (NIST, Science, 2010).

  horizon — it slows, reddens and dims without arriving.
• Tides at the horizon of a supermassive black hole are mild: tidal force
  drops as mass over distance cubed, while the horizon grows with mass.

  The bright whirlpool is a feeding one; this one is not.
