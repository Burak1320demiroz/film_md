### CUE A — "BASINÇ" (S1-S12 · ~2:20 üret, 2:00 kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 72 BPM, 4/4, minor, slow,
bone-dry mix with no reverb at all — every sound dies instantly, as if
played in thin dead air. Patient, calm, indifferent sci-fi score for
air slowly leaving a sealed place. The piece opens on ONE thin
breath-like sustained tone, alone, for the first bars — nothing else.
Then a soft dry muted metallic tick enters, one tick every two beats,
like a slow clock in an aluminium frame: this tick NEVER changes —
same tempo, same level, same timbre from the first bar to the last; it
reacts to nothing. Beneath it a deep 45 Hz sub-bass root doubled by a
quiet companion tone one or two octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so
the weight survives on small phone speakers; reinforce the low end
with a dense harmonic stack at roughly 100 Hz, 150 Hz and 200 Hz so
the missing fundamental is implied rather than played; the perceived
weight must survive full loss of everything below 150 Hz. Over long
gaps, single low felt-piano notes state a three-note FALLING motif
whose third note is never played — the line stops mid-phrase every
time and does not continue. THE ONLY THING THAT MOVES IS THE TOP END:
across the whole piece the high frequencies slowly close down, as if a
filter were shutting and the air itself were getting too thin to carry
them — by the last third only the low tone, the tick and the sub
remain, everything above them gone. No crescendos, no swells, no
risers, no drums, no strings, no choir, no melody beyond the
unfinished motif; even where the story turns urgent, nothing reacts.
Over the final 20 seconds the tone thins to the edge of hearing with
the tick still going underneath, then everything stops dead on a bar
line — no fade out, no final note. [Instrumental]
```

### CUE B — "MÜHÜR" (S13-S18 + kelepçe darbesi + S22-S24 koda · ~1:45 üret)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 120 BPM, 4/4, minor, dry
tight mix with no reverb — close, precise, sealed. A controlled-
adrenaline action cue for one man fighting to seal a leaking
greenhouse: a fight, not a chase — nothing panics, everything
tightens. It opens with ONE soft sustained tone, alone, the first
note of a three-note falling motif, over a faint 45 Hz sub-bass root
doubled by a quiet companion tone one or two octaves higher (roughly
90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on small phone speakers; reinforce
the low end with a dense harmonic stack at roughly 100 Hz, 150 Hz and
200 Hz so the missing fundamental is implied rather than played; the
perceived weight must survive full loss of everything below 150 Hz.
Then it BUILDS one layer every 10 seconds, each on a bar line: a soft
heartbeat kick on half notes; a ticking sixteenth-note shaker-and-rim
grid like a pulse held under control; staccato low strings pumping a
narrow two-chord ostinato locked hard to the grid; then, about forty
seconds in, on ONE downbeat, full force: pounding low toms and taiko
in tight patterns, hard low string stabs, short dark low brass punches
— rhythmic, no heroic fanfare — a relentless, exact forward drive for
about twenty seconds, tightening rather than getting louder, the
falling motif stabbing through, still unfinished, its third note never
played. Then everything STOPS DEAD on a bar line — total silence, no
tail, no reverb, no fade. After a full silence of several seconds, a
low two-bar rising figure climbs — sub, toms and low strings together,
four seconds, tightening — and lands on ONE single hard low hit,
damped instantly, dead, like a lever bar locking shut: no ring, no
tail, nothing after it. Then a long full silence. Then a single quiet
sustained tone returns, alone, and holds for a long time; about twenty
seconds into it the three-note falling motif finally COMPLETES, once,
softly, on felt piano, like something set down; that last tone holds
steady and level and is still sounding when the piece is cut. No EDM
drop, no white-noise risers, no choir, no epic swell, no final
cadence. [Instrumental]
```
