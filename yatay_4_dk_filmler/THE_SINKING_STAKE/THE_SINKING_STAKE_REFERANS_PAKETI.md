```
Photorealistic reference sheet of one physical effect, four panels in one
image, plain flat lighting, no people, no equipment.

never widens, never leans, never branches, never drifts sideways and never
reads as smoke, steam clouds, fog or fire.

PANEL 1  a ring of vapour around a buried white disc foot, seen from the

         box, seen from above — the reference for S7 and S8
PANEL 3  one wrist-wide column rising out of a small round opening in
         untouched snow, seen from low and looking up, running out of the
         top of the panel — the reference for S20, S21, S24 near column
```
