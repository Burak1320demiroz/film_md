### CUE A — "SAYIM" (S1-S11 · ~2:05 üret, 1:50 kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 72 BPM, 4/4, minor, slow,
bone-dry mix with no reverb at all — every sound dies instantly, as if
played in thin dead air. A patient, cold, indifferent machine score for
a place where the light is going out. The piece is built on ONE dry
muted mechanical tick, one tick on every beat, like a meter counting:
the tick NEVER changes — same tempo, same level, same timbre from the
first bar to the last, and it reacts to nothing that happens. ⭐ Every
twelfth tick is MISSING: the count runs one, two, three up to eleven
and then leaves a hole exactly where the twelfth should fall, again and
again, all the way through. Under the tick sits a still, unmoving low
tone and a deep 45 Hz sub-bass root doubled by a quiet companion tone
one or two octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on
small phone speakers; reinforce the low end with a dense harmonic stack
at roughly 100 Hz, 150 Hz and 200 Hz so the missing fundamental is
implied rather than played; the perceived weight must survive full loss
of everything below 150 Hz. Every twenty or thirty seconds a single low
felt-piano note states a two-note RISING motif whose second note always
arrives late and never goes any higher — the phrase never completes.
THE ONLY THING THAT MOVES IS THE TOP END: across the whole piece the
high frequencies close down one step at a time, as if a filter were
shutting while daylight drains away; by the last third only the low
tone, the tick and the sub remain. No crescendos, no swells, no risers,
no drums, no strings, no choir, no melody beyond the unfinished motif;
even when the story turns urgent, nothing reacts. The piece ends by
stopping dead on a bar line — no fade out, no final note, no ritardando.
[Instrumental]
```
