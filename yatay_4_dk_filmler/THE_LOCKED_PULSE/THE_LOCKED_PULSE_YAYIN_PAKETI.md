### DESCRIPTION (§2) — film-specific lines only

```
In a spinning ring, weight is spin. When the machine spins the ring faster, everything on it gets heavier.

One engineer, one coolant leak, six sleepers in cryo. The station's automation keeps them cold the only way it can: by
spinning the ring up. He has to close the leak before he can no longer stand.

THE SCIENCE
• Rotating habitats make "gravity" by spinning: a = ω²R. A 56-metre ring gives one Earth g at 4 rpm — and only a quarter g at 2 rpm.
• Drop something inside a spinning ring and it does not fall straight: it lands to one side, trailing the spin (the Coriolis effect).
  The sideways offset depends on drop height and radius, not on how fast the ring turns.
• A hanging strap is a pendulum: T = 2π√(L/g). At a quarter g a one-metre strap swings once every four seconds; at one g, every two.
• A coolant loop's pressure head scales with g — so a controller really can hold loop pressure by spinning the ring faster.
  Nobody asked what that does to the person standing in it.
```
