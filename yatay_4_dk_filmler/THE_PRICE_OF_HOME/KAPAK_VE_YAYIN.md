### Açıklama
An alien offers two stranded astronauts a passage back to Earth. His price is every memory one of them has of the person they love. THE PRICE OF HOME is a four-minute science-fiction short about an impossible bargain—and a relationship that may have to begin again. Created with AI-assisted image and video generation, original story, dialogue, sound design and editing. Would you have accepted Vey's price? #SciFi #ShortFilm #Alien
