## 🎤 ANA MÜZİK PROMPTU — SUNO / LYRIA / STABLE AUDIO

> **Üretim ayarı:** 3:00 uzunluk · enstrümantal · vokalsiz (nefes-tonu hariç,
> kelime YOK) · 60 BPM sabit · tek parça, tek seferde üretilir.
> Kısa süre limiti olan araçta 6 bölüm ayrı üretilmez — **süre limiti 3 dakikaya
> yetmiyorsa araç değiştirilir**, parçalanmaz (§14.1).

```
A single continuous 3-minute cinematic instrumental score for a hard
science-fiction film set on the black nucleus of a comet approaching the
sun. Strictly 60 BPM, 4/4, D minor (Aeolian). Absolutely no drums, no
percussion kit, no beat, no rhythm section, no melody hooks, no build to
an epic climax, no trailer risers, no orchestral swell.

CORE SOUND — five layers only:

1. A continuous sub-bass drone on D1 (36.7 Hz) that NEVER changes in
pitch, volume or texture for the entire three minutes. It is doubled by a
quiet companion tone one octave higher at D2 (73.4 Hz) and a second, very
quiet companion at D3 (146.8 Hz), so the weight survives on small phone
speakers; the low fundamental adds depth on headphones but is never the
only carrier of the emotion. This drone is the indifferent universe: it
does not react to anything that happens.

2. A dry granular ice texture — bowed metal, bowed glass, tiny brittle
grains of noise, close-mic'd and completely unreverberated. Mineral, cold,
non-musical. It thickens slowly between 0:24 and 1:28 and thins away after
2:00, disappearing entirely by 2:28. It must sound like material, not like
atmosphere.

3. A solo cello, played sparsely and with almost no vibrato, entering only
four times in the whole piece — at 0:08, 0:32, 1:44 and 2:28, and never
anywhere else. Its phrase at 0:32 RISES AND THEN STOPS MID-PHRASE — it does
not resolve, it does not continue, and the silence after it is longer than
the phrase itself. That same unfinished phrase returns and finally completes
at 1:44. The cello is one human being; it is never doubled, never
harmonised, never sectioned into strings.

4. A wordless female breath-tone — no lyrics, no syllables, no language,
just a low sustained human breath at the edge of pitch. It appears only
after 1:28 and again in the final section. It must sound like something
old that is still switched on.

5. A single very quiet pure sine ping that sounds once every 41 seconds,
deliberately NOT aligned to the beat or the bar, drifting further off-grid
each time. It is a machine's own clock, indifferent to the music.

STRUCTURE (the piece is one continuous arc, no gaps, no fades between
sections):
[0:00-0:24] Sub drone and one thin granular grain. Almost nothing. Cold.
            One short cello note at 0:08, then nothing.
[0:24-0:56] Granular texture accumulates. At 0:32 the cello rises and stops
            mid-phrase. The silence after it is longer than the phrase.
[0:56-1:28] NOTHING CHANGES for these 32 seconds. No entry, no swell, no
            reaction of any kind. Held, indifferent, exactly level.
[1:28-2:00] Breath-tone enters underneath. At 1:44 the unfinished cello
            phrase returns and completes — quiet human warmth, not big.
[2:00-2:28] Everything slowly runs out of air. Over the last 8 seconds the
            texture thins to the sub drone alone and stops. Not a cut and
            not a fade: exhaustion.
[2:28-3:00] Breath-tone and cello open out, and a faint short reverb appears
            for the first time. At 2:44 a perfect fifth D-A sounds for the
            FIRST AND ONLY TIME in the entire piece — the single resolution,
            gentle, not triumphant.
[3:00]      The piece is CUT at exactly 3:00. No fade out, no final chord,
            no ritardando, no cymbal, no resolution after the fifth.

HARMONIC RULE — the most important instruction: the perfect fifth D-A must
NOT be voiced anywhere before 2:44. Use minor seconds, minor thirds,
sevenths, open octaves and unisons instead. The tonic fifth is withheld for
the entire piece and released once.

SPACE: Completely DRY until 2:28 — no reverb, no delay, no room, no width.
This is vacuum; there is no air to carry an echo. Only in the final 32
seconds does a faint, short, thin reverb appear for the first time, as if a
very thin atmosphere had formed.

EMOTION: patient, mineral, awed, unhurried. Not sad, not frightening, not
heroic, not mysterious-thriller. The feeling is of something enormous that
has been asleep for seventy-one years and is now simply waking up, without
noticing that anyone is standing on it.

[Instrumental] [No Vocals] [No Lyrics] [No Drums] [No Percussion]
[Sustained] [Dry Mix] [Cut ending, no fade]
```

   transient içermediği için kurguda kesintisiz uzatılabilir/döngülenebilir.

   Sub drone: 0:00 → 5:24 kesintisiz, 5:36 → 8:00 kesintisiz.

   olduğu için ek nokta duyulmaz). Faz geçişlerinde HİÇBİR değişiklik yok.

3. SESSİZLİK PENCERESİ — 5:24 → 5:36

   Noor'un nefesi ve suit pompası kalır. S42'nin görüntüsü filmin en büyük

   S32'deki çello tamamlanması ve S56'daki beşli, kareye MÜZİK KAYDIRILARAK
   değil GÖRÜNTÜ kaydırılarak hizalanır — klip başlarında 2-6 karelik pay

   13 diyalog sahnesinde müzik -6 dB. Ducking DEĞİL, manuel stem otomasyonu.
   Granüler doku 2-5 kHz'de dar bir notch alır (Noor'un ses bandı).

   ses önce biter, görüntü sonra kararır.
