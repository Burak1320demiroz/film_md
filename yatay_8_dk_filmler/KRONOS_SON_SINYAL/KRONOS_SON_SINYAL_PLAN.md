## 📖 BIBLE'LAR

### 👤 Character Bible

**Kestrel:** 32-year-old female commander, short dark hair, small permanent scar near left eyebrow, wearing a cream-gray pressure suit with petrol-blue shoulder panels. Helmet visor clear and raised in interior scenes.
**Aras:** 35-year-old male system engineer, short dark hair, very short stubble beard, wearing a cream-gray pressure suit with orange shoulder panels. Helmet visor clear and raised in interior scenes.

### 🌍 World Bible
**Kronos Station:** Earth orbit space station consisting of Module A (Command/Habitat, 8m long, neutral light-gray) and Module B (Experiment/Energy, 7m long, warm interior light), connected by a red pressure hatch (H-R).

### 🎥 Camera Bible
Lenses: 35mm (Wide exterior/interior), 50mm (Medium two-person/single), 65mm (Close-up face/action), 85mm (Macro display/details). Stable camera motion, no artificial jitter.

### 💡 Lighting Bible
Module A: Cool neutral-white diffuse illumination.
Module B: Warm amber interior light transitioning to red emergency warning.
Exterior: High contrast orbital solar illumination and Earth atmosphere background.

### ⚙️ Physics Bible
Microgravity zero-g environment: All loose objects float along linear momentum vectors. No atmospheric drag in vacuum. Sound is suppressed in exterior vacuum shots.

### 🚫 Negative Bible
no atmospheric haze in space vacuum, no artificial gravity gait, no floating teardrops, no explosion fireball in vacuum, no lens flare artifact, no duplicate character features, no loose suit floating without tether.
