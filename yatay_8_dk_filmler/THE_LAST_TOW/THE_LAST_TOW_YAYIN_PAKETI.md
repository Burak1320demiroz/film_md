```
In vacuum, clean metal that touches clean metal does not come apart again.

A salvager works alone in a yard where every wreck has slowly welded itself into one
body. There is a sealed hatch she has walked past for years, and the only thing that
can open it is a temperature difference that crosses this yard once every 44 days.

THE SCIENCE
• Cold welding is real and it is a spacecraft design hazard: without oxide layers,
  clean metal surfaces bond on contact in vacuum. Suspected case: Galileo's high-gain
  antenna, stuck on deployment, 1991 (NASA/JPL).
• An airless surface runs from about 100 K in shadow to about 390 K in sunlight
  (LRO Diviner, Moon).
• With the star 2.3° above the horizon, a horizontal surface gets about 55 W/m² and a
  surface facing the star gets 1361 W/m². Upright faces blaze; the ground stays dark.

CHAPTERS
```
