## LOGLINE (25 words)

> A former military pilot docks with a prison ship to free her captive sister; when
> she refuses to leave the others, the last gate is already closing.

## DESCRIPTION — the copy the hook narration becomes

```
I came for my sister.
She was aboard a prison ship with hundreds of captive astronauts.
She would not leave them. So we took the ship.
Now a fleet is behind us, and the last gate is closing.
We have to get everyone through.
```

⭐ These five lines are the film's own opening narration (H1–H4) and double as the
description. Nothing in them is a promise the film does not keep: the docking, the
sister, the hijack, the pursuit and the closing gate are all on screen inside the
first forty seconds.

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY IN THE FILM — honest list

```
✅ a real docking sequence, shown as four separate states: alignment, soft capture,
   four hard clamps, pressure equalisation (not two ships cutting to "docked")
✅ a 2.4-kilometre hull leaving its cradle in one wide shot, with the port and the
   gas giant in the same frame
✅ a closing gate whose opening only ever gets smaller, verified against the ship's
   own fixed cross-section twice before it fails
✅ twenty-four independent transfer pods, introduced in the first minute and used in
   the last three
✅ adult characters only — no child character, no child voice, no childhood scene
⛔ NOT in the film: a fleet battle, a boarding melee, an on-screen death, a gore shot,
   an explosion with a shockwave, or a crowd scene
```

## THE SCIENCE — what is real and what is invented

```
REAL (used as the design basis · sources below)
  · Orbital free fall: everything aboard is weightless because the ship and its
    contents fall together, not because "there is no gravity out there".
  · Docking is staged, not instant: alignment → soft capture → damping → hard latch.
    The film shows those stages as separate shots because they are separate events.
  · Magnetic boots and handrails attach a person to a surface. They do NOT give
    weight to the person's hair, to a loose strap, or to anything they let go of.
INVENTED (declared, not smuggled)
  · The star gate itself, both gas giants, the ships and their power. The gate is an
    open science-fiction assumption of this world.
⛔ No source below endorses the gate, the planets or the flight times.
```
