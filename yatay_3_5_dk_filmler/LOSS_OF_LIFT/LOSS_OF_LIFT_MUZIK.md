## 🎼 CUE PROMPTLARI (Google Flow / Lyria — [Instrumental])

### CUE A — "ASİT" (S0-S12 · ~2:20 üret, 2:09 kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 48 BPM, 4/4, minor key, very
slow, dry mix with almost no reverb, dark thick muffled timbres as if
heard inside dense air. Patient, quietly grieving sci-fi score for a
slow irreversible sinking. THE STRUCTURE IS SUBTRACTION: the piece
begins FULL — a soft airy organ-like drone, a low cello pad, a thin
glass-harmonic tone around 600-900 Hz, a slow dry woody tick, and a
deep 45 Hz sub-bass root doubled by a quiet companion tone one or two
octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on small
phone speakers. Then, about every 25 seconds, ONE layer is quietly and
permanently REMOVED — it never returns; the music leaks. A two-note
FALLING motif appears early and repeats sparsely, never resolving.
Just past the midpoint, one single warm low woodwind phrase — brief,
tender, alone, the only warm color — then the stripping continues. No
crescendos, no risers, no drops, no percussion build, no choir, no
epic swell; the energy only ever decreases. By the end only the thin
glass tone and the doubled low root remain. The piece ends suspended
and unresolved, cut clean on a bar line — no fade out. [Instrumental]
```

### CUE B — "GECE" (S13-S19 · ~1:20 üret, 70 s kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 48 BPM, 4/4, minor, bone-dry
mix, dark thick muffled timbres, extremely sparse — a score at the
edge of silence for a long night of acceptance. Foundation: one thin
sustained tone (bowed glass or bowed metal, held steady around
600-900 Hz) over a barely-present 45 Hz sub root doubled one or two
octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) for small speakers. Over long
gaps, single soft felt-piano notes — one note at a time, no phrase,
no flourish, seconds of air between them. THE TONE NEVER REACTS: it
never rises, never swells, never brightens — even where the story
lifts with false hope, the tone holds its exact pitch and level,
indifferent as physics. The two-note falling motif returns once,
thinner than before, and again does not resolve. Across the final 15
seconds the tone thins to the very edge of hearing and then stops
dead on a bar line — total silence, no fade, no final note.
[Instrumental]
```

### CUE C — "YUKARI" (S23-S24 · ~0:30 üret, 20 s kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 48 BPM, 4/4, opening minor
and resolving ONCE to an open major — the only resolution in the
film. Dry, warm, close, still muffled and intimate. After long
silence the music re-enters softly, and FOR THE FIRST TIME LAYERS ARE
ADDED instead of removed: one warm sustained major chord swelling
gently — relief, not triumph — over a soft low woodwind root, a quiet
string pad, and the 45 Hz sub root doubled one or two octaves higher
(roughly 90-160 Hz) for small speakers. The falling two-note motif
returns INVERTED — two rising notes — and completes, once, quietly,
like something handed over. No percussion, no click, no pulse; nothing
in the music follows any blinking light. The chord holds and decays to
a single sustained tone that is still sounding when the piece is cut —
no fade out, no final cadence, no epic swell, no choir. [Instrumental]
```
