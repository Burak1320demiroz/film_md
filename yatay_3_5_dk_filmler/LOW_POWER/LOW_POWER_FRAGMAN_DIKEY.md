## 🔒 Master Cinema Lock

Photorealistic live-action cinematography, natural 35mm film grain, no

daytime sky is butterscotch brown and never blue; at sunset the sky
immediately around the pale white-blue sun turns blue while the rest stays
brown, and that relationship never inverts. All dust falls downward and

optically clear with zero tint and zero mirror coating; the suit's single
compact back unit is canonical and carries no extra tank, hose or cable.

needle ever moves. No text of any kind on screen, on any panel, dial or
