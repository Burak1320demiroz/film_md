## 🎼 CUE PROMPTLARI (Google Flow / Lyria — [Instrumental])

### CUE A — "GECE" (S0-S12 · ~2:20 üret, 2:09 kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 96 BPM, 4/4, minor key, dry mix
with almost no reverb. Dark, patient, pressure-building sci-fi score for
a night escape on a lightless world. Foundation: a deep 45 Hz sub-bass
drone doubled by a quiet companion tone one or two octaves higher
(roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on small phone speakers; the
low fundamental adds depth on headphones but is never the only carrier
of the emotion. Textures of darkness: dry struck-metal ticks like
cooling rock, bowed low strings, and a two-note RISING motif that never
resolves — the sound of a line of light walking closer. Structure: first
20 seconds near-still (drone + air), then one new layer every 20
seconds; a dry, even eighth-note pulse enters at the midpoint, pressing
but never panicked; the launch section (final 40 seconds) adds low
taiko-like drum hits locked tight to the grid — discipline under
pressure, no chaos, no swirl. After the launch peak the track thins
abruptly to a cold, weightless high drone with the two-note motif
hanging unresolved. No drops, no white-noise risers, no epic brass, no
choir. The piece ends clean on a suspended chord — cut, no fade out.
[Instrumental]
```

### CUE B — "YAKLAŞMA" (S13-S19b-S20 · ~1:35 üret, 85.5 s kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 72 BPM, 4/4, minor, bone-dry
mix — zero reverb, vacuum-dry, intimate. Quiet procedural trust score
for a blind stern-first docking: a soft metronome-like click pulse
(muted wood/rim clicks) under long cold string pads — steady hands, held
breath. The 45 Hz sub-bass root is doubled by a quiet companion tone one
or two octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on
phone speakers. Single dry close-miked piano notes land only on decision
moments — one note each, no flourish, long gaps between them. The
metronome pulse NEVER reacts, never speeds, never falters — even where
the film aborts and turns away, the click keeps its exact rate,
indifferent as procedure. Through the approach the texture THINS instead
of building: layer by layer removed as concentration deepens — pads
fall away, then the piano falls silent, until only the click and the
faint doubled root remain. The two-note rising motif from the first cue
returns once, thinner, and again does not resolve. The piece ends by
simply STOPPING on a bar line — total silence, no fade, no final hit.
[Instrumental]
```

### CUE C — "RIHTIM" (S23-S24 · ~0:30 üret, 20 s kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 72 BPM, 4/4, opening minor and
resolving ONCE to an open major — the only resolution in the film. Dry,
warm, close: after long silence, the music re-enters softly with one
warm sustained major chord swelling gently — relief, not triumph — over
a soft low woodwind root and a quiet string pad. The 45 Hz sub root is
doubled one or two octaves higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) for small
speakers. The two-note rising motif finally COMPLETES, once, quietly, as
if exhaled. No percussion at all, no click — the metronome's work is
done. The chord holds through a slow departure and decays to a single
sustained tone that is still sounding when the piece is cut — no fade
out, no final cadence, no epic swell, no choir. [Instrumental]
```
