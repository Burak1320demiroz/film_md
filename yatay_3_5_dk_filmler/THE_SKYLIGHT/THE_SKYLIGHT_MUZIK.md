### CUE A — "SAAT" (S3-S19 · ~3:00 üret, 2:45 kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 48 BPM, 4/4, minor, very
slow, bone-dry mix with no reverb at all — every sound dies instantly,
as if played in thin dead air. Patient, calm, indifferent sci-fi score
for a single day passing inside a vast dark chamber lit by one column
of daylight. The piece opens on ONE thin sustained glass-harmonic
tone, alone, for the first bars — nothing else. Then a soft dry woody
pulse enters, one soft tap every two beats, like a slow clock: this
pulse NEVER changes — same tempo, same level, same timbre from the
first bar to the last; it reacts to nothing. Beneath it a deep 45 Hz
sub-bass root doubled by a quiet companion tone one or two octaves
higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on small phone
speakers; reinforce the low end with a dense harmonic stack at
roughly 100 Hz, 150 Hz and 200 Hz so the missing fundamental is
implied rather than played; the perceived weight must survive full
loss of everything below 150 Hz. Over long gaps, single felt-piano
notes state a three-note FALLING motif whose third note is never
played — the line stops mid-phrase every time and does not continue.
THE ONLY THING THAT MOVES IS THE COLOR: across the whole piece the
sustained tone slowly darkens and lowers, its brightness closing like
a filter, while the clock pulse stays exactly the same. No crescendos,
no swells, no risers, no drums, no choir, no melody beyond the
unfinished motif; even where the story turns hopeful or lonely,
nothing reacts. Over the final 30 seconds the tone thins to the edge
of hearing with the pulse still ticking underneath, then everything
stops dead on a bar line — no fade out, no final note. [Instrumental]
```

### CUE B — "AYRILIŞ" (S25-S30 + S32 koda · ~1:30 üret, 60 s + koda kullan)

```
Instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics. 120 BPM, 4/4, minor, dry
tight mix with no reverb — close, precise, sealed. A controlled-
adrenaline launch cue: a departure, not an escape — nothing panics,
everything tightens. It opens with ONE soft sustained tone, alone,
the first note of a three-note falling motif, over a faint 45 Hz
sub-bass root doubled by a quiet companion tone one or two octaves
higher (roughly 90-160 Hz) so the weight survives on small phone
speakers; reinforce the low end with a dense harmonic stack at
roughly 100 Hz, 150 Hz and 200 Hz so the missing fundamental is
implied rather than played; the perceived weight must survive full
loss of everything below 150 Hz. Then it BUILDS one layer at a time,
every 8-10 seconds: a soft heartbeat kick on half notes; a ticking
sixteenth-note shaker-and-rim grid like a pulse held under control;
staccato low strings pumping a narrow two-chord ostinato locked hard
to the grid. About thirty seconds in, on ONE downbeat, full force:
pounding low toms and taiko in tight patterns, hard low string stabs,
short dark low brass punches — rhythmic, no heroic fanfare — a
relentless, exact forward drive for about twenty seconds, tightening
rather than getting louder, the motif stabbing through, still
unfinished. Then, on a bar line, ALL drums drop out and the harmony
opens into one wide sustained suspended chord — wide, not high: it
does not climb, it does not resolve, it simply holds, vast and level,
for about ten seconds over the soft sub root. Then everything STOPS
DEAD on a bar line — total silence, no tail, no reverb, no fade.
After a full silence, a single quiet tone returns and the three-note
falling motif finally COMPLETES, once, softly, like something set
down; that last tone holds steady and is still sounding when the
piece is cut. No EDM drop, no white-noise risers, no choir, no epic
swell, no final cadence. [Instrumental]
```
