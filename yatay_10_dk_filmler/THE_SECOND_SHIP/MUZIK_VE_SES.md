## 🎧 GLOBAL MÜZİK PROMPTU (tek parça · 330 saniye)

> Üretim: tek seferde 330 saniye alınamıyorsa 3 × 110 saniyelik bölüm
> aynı seed / aynı stil promptuyla üretilir ve kurguda birleştirilir.
> **Sahne sahne parça üretilmez** (§14.1).

```
GLOBAL SCORE PROMPT — THE SECOND SHIP

Instrumental cinematic score, 5 minutes 30 seconds, one continuous
unbroken piece with no sections that restart, no fade in, no fade out.

TEMPO AND KEY: 48 BPM, 4/4, G minor (Aeolian). Slow, patient, unhurried.
The perfect fifth G–D is never sounded fully anywhere in the piece except
in the final 20 seconds; before that the harmony always slips to the fourth
or the sixth instead of resolving.

CORE INSTRUCTION — ANEMPATHETIC: this score does not react to events. It
does not swell, it does not accelerate, it has no impacts, no risers, no
braams, no percussion hits, no build-and-release. Nothing in the music
knows that anything dramatic is happening. It behaves like weather.

LAYERS, in order of prominence:
1. A sustained sub-bass drone on G, present in every single bar, never
   changing pitch and never changing volume. It is doubled one octave up
   and two octaves up (49 Hz, 98 Hz, 196 Hz) so it survives on a phone
   speaker.
2. A dry granular stone texture: fine, grainy, dusty, like small mineral
   particles settling on a hard surface. Non-pitched, quiet, always
   slightly irregular. No reverb tail.
3. A solo viola, warm and human but restrained, playing very sparsely — it
   enters only four times in the whole piece and is silent for long
   stretches. Its phrase is a slow rising figure of five notes that stops
   before its final note every time it appears, and only completes in the
   last 20 seconds of the piece.
4. A single wordless human vocal tone, one held note at a time, processed
   as if replayed through a very old degraded tape machine: band-limited,
   slight wow and flutter, soft tape hiss. No words, no syllables, no
   melody, no choir, no vocal harmony. One tired human tone.
5. A low sustained string cluster of exactly three notes that never resolve
   to the tonic and never move to a cadence. They hang unfinished.

STRUCTURE across the 5:30, without any hard section boundaries:
- Opens with drone and cluster alone, viola entering quietly after 20
  seconds and leaving before the first minute ends.
- The middle third is the emotional centre: the degraded human vocal tone
  becomes the most prominent element, alone against the drone, with the
  granular texture very quiet underneath.
- The final 80 seconds bring the viola back for the last time. In the last
  20 seconds, and only there, the harmony finally opens onto a clean
  perfect fifth on G. The piece stops on that fifth abruptly, with no fade,
  no repeat, no final chord flourish and no tail.

MIX AND SPACE: extremely dry. Almost no reverb anywhere. No large hall, no
cathedral, no ambience wash. Wide low end, narrow high end. Nothing above
8 kHz is prominent. Target a quiet, patient mix that sits under dialogue.

STRICTLY EXCLUDE: drums, percussion of any kind, taiko, timpani, snare
builds, trailer hits, braams, risers, downers, whooshes, orchestral swells,
crescendos into silence, choir, lyrics, synth arpeggios, pulsing eighth
notes, ostinato patterns, sound design impacts, piano, guitar, brass, sci-fi
laser sounds, heartbeat sounds, ticking clocks, breathing sounds, glitch
edits, key changes, tempo changes, and any moment that feels triumphant,
heroic, menacing or scary.
```

A single sustained sub-bass drone on the note G, 6 minutes long,
