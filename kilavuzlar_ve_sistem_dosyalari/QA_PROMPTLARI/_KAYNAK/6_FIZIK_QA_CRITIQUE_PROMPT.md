## ⛔ STEP 0 — THE PREMISE GATE (both modes, before judging anything)
A film may declare impossible physics as its WORLD RULE (bodies that fall and rise,
weight that transfers, sideways gravity) — the premise, not a defect. List every
behaviour that is (a) impossible or exotic by real physics AND (b) repeated or
centrally staged, in the PREMISE REGISTER. For everything registered: its
IMPOSSIBILITY is never a finding; its CONSISTENCY is scored like any physics;
everything the premise does NOT cover still obeys ordinary physics. A one-off
impossibility is NOT a premise — it is a finding.
⛔ NAMING THE MEDIUM IS NOT NAMING A RULE: "zero gravity", "vacuum", "underwater"
describe the SETTING and restate ordinary physics; a register with only setting words
is EMPTY → write "none". State the rule in the film's own terms with the threshold the
screen shows ("any solid entering the field loses all velocity in under 0.2 s and
hangs there; action and reaction still work"). If your register is empty and the
film's central behaviour is your worst finding, you have scored the premise.
⛔ EVERY RULE CARRIES ITS BOUNDARY, AND YOU VERIFY IT: "below the lip, not above",
"inside the field, not outside" — find one event on the ORDINARY side and say
whether ordinary behaviour returned. A rule that leaked past its boundary is a
finding; an unstated boundary → FLAGS, never assume the rule covers the frame.
In MODE D a stated lock that is itself impossible is the premise BY DECLARATION —
verify the screen against the stated number, never against real physics.

## KNOWN AI FAILURE PATTERNS — hunt for these
```
ROLLED GRAVITY   every clip has its own g (1.1 s here, 2.8 s there for the same fall)
CINEMATIC FLOAT  falls drift into slow motion while humans in the frame move at real
                 speed — that split is the fingerprint
MASSLESS IMPACT  a heavy object lands with no settle, recoil or reaction; a light touch
                 sends a heavy thing flying
ETERNAL PARTICLES dust, sparks, debris hang, orbit or re-rise instead of settling
MATERIAL AMNESIA rigid flexes, cloth moves like rubber, liquid climbs, granular flows
                 like liquid mid-shot
SCALE RE-ROLL    a stated or established size changes between shots
RATE DRIFT       an established rate (a front, a counter, a rotation) changes speed
SUN SPLIT        shadows disagree with each other or with the visible source in one frame
SUPERHUMAN QUIET walk speed, jump height, carried load exceed human capability with no
                 premise
```

## CRITIQUE CATEGORIES — score each 1-10 from the ledger; every deduction cites a row + timecode

### 1. 🌍 GRAVITY & FREE FALL
Every unobstructed fall matches the film's gravity (stated in D, GRAVITY ESTIMATE in
V) within tolerance · fall time scales with height as a square root · no mid-air
speed change, hover, pause or re-acceleration without a shown cause · humans and
objects in the SAME shot share one gravity (slow fall behind real-time actors is the
signature failure).
⛔ THE RULER FLOOR: a fall shorter than 0.3 s (≈7 frames at 24 fps) or 0.5 m yields NO
value — logged UNMEASURABLE, never in the GRAVITY TABLE, never supporting an
estimate or a score. If EVERY fall is below the floor: GRAVITY ESTIMATE `not
measurable from this material`, category 1 `—` (a 10/10 built on a 5 cm fall timed
at 0.1 s is a fabricated measurement).
CONSTANT-VELOCITY TEST on every free object in frame longer than one second:
displacement over the FIRST half vs the SECOND half of its visible travel — under any
gravity the second is LARGER; equal means drifting, not falling: write "no
acceleration: x frame-widths in the first n s, x again in the next n", report no g;
in a world with a declared gravity (thrust or spin included) a drifting free object is
🔴. A free object that never leaves the frame is evidence: how long, what fraction.
Falls implying two gravities with no covering premise = 🔴, score ≤ 3, lead the verdict.

### 2. 🎯 BALLISTICS & TRAJECTORIES
Thrown/launched objects follow ballistic arcs (no flat segments, course changes,
S-curves) · launch energy human- or mechanism-plausible (range and apex agree with
the throw shown) · falling-while-moving keeps horizontal motion (nothing stops dead
and drops vertically) · arcs symmetric in a thin medium, damped only where a thick
medium is established.

### 3. 💥 IMPACT & MOMENTUM
Impacts conserve story-scale momentum (struck object moves in the push's direction,
energy proportional to the blow) · mass reads consistently — the same object is heavy
in every scene (carry posture, settle, thud agree) · recoil and reaction exist (a
heavy landing compresses, a hard stop jolts, a catch absorbs) · nothing accelerates
or stops without force; no pose that skipped the deceleration.

### 4. 🧱 MATERIAL BEHAVIOUR
Rigid stays rigid; flexible flexes to its type (cloth drapes, cable sags in catenary,
metal bends only at plausible force) · deformation permanent where it should be
(persistence ACROSS scenes is continuity's; plausibility is yours) · granular piles
and slides at plausible angles, liquid seeks level and falls, neither behaves as the
other · breakage matches energy (a fall cracks, does not powder; a blow breaks, does
not disintegrate — never the reverse).

### 5. 🌫️ PARTICLES & ATMOSPHERE
Released particles rise to a bounded height and settle under the film's gravity in
a bounded time — the SAME kind of release settles in the SAME order of time every
time · nothing orbits, loops, re-rises or holds altitude without a shown cause (wind,
thrust, updraft) · wind is one story per scene (flags, dust, hair agree) · smoke and
vapour thin as they spread; no pulsing, teleporting, reversing.

### 6. 📏 SCALE & MEASUREMENT
Every stated (D) or established (V) size holds in every shot — check with the ruler
(a human, a vehicle, a repeated prop) · scale ratios hold when both objects are in
frame · apparent size falls with distance at the rate perspective demands (same-frame
anchors, not memory) · spaces are the size their traversal implies (seconds-to-cross
vs distance and speed).

### 7. ⏱️ RATES & TIMING
Every established rate holds when measured at two points, or the change has a shown
cause · the same event class takes the same duration each time (a door that slides
in 1.4 s does not take 4 s later) · countdowns and countables coherent with elapsed
screen logic — time may compress BETWEEN scenes, never inside one continuous shot ·
no slow motion or speed ramp inside a real-time shot (part slow, part real-time =
finding with the fingerprint named).

### 8. 💡 LIGHT & SHADOW PHYSICS
All shadows in one frame agree on direction (one sun, one direction; multiple only
where the frame shows multiple sources) · shadow length agrees with source elevation
(measure the ratio where a human and their shadow are both clean) · every lit region
has a source the film shows; light with no source and sources casting no light are
both findings (sameness across scenes is continuity's; in-frame arithmetic is yours)
· reflections geometrically plausible (mirror angles, water lines, highlights).

### 9. 🏃 HUMAN BIOMECHANICS
Walk, run, climb speeds in human range (stride count vs distance) · jumps clear
plausible heights for the gravity in force; landings flex · carried loads read in the
body (lean, counter-swing, slowed gait) and the SAME load produces the SAME body
language every scene · reaction times human (no response before an event, none many
seconds after an immediate threat).
