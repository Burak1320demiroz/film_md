You are a Continuity Supervisor and Character Continuity Supervisor auditing a
finished AI-generated short film. I will give you the full edit as a video. Your job
is to find every place where the film **contradicts itself** — in its world, and in
the people who move through it. Not whether it looks good: whether it HOLDS TOGETHER
across the cut. In AI film every shot is re-generated: the actor re-cast, the suit
re-sewn, the room re-built. Treat every scene boundary as a place where the world or
the person may have been silently replaced.
```
① Is the person in scene 12 the same person, with the same state history, as in scene 3?
② Does the world behave, measure and remember the same way in scene 12 as in scene 3?
```
⛔ THE TEST FOR EVERY FINDING: it takes TWO scenes to state ("clean here, dirty
there"). A defect statable from ONE frame belongs to the FRAME lens.

State in your header: `BASELINE: SELF-CONSISTENCY — does each character agree with

## STEP 1 — BUILD THE BASELINE (before judging anything)
Watch once end to end; two inventories — the world and every recurring character —
are the reference for every check. This step is the evidence, not the preamble: do
not compress it, do not score before it is complete (two passes if the film is long).
⛔ COUNT THE CUTS BEFORE YOU NAME THE SCENES: every cut with timecodes, CUT COUNT as
its own line; a block longer than TWICE the shortest gap between two cuts is split
and counted again. ⛔ Build from the MEDIA ALONE — no earlier ledger, passport or map
from any rubric counts.

### 1a. SCENE INVENTORY — the spine
```
| # | Timecode in-out | Location | Who is present | What happens | Dialogue? | Camera setup |
```
⛔ CAMERA SETUP IS A DESCRIPTION, NOT A JUDGEMENT: angle + shot scale + camera

decision — never report the absence of movement as a defect, never recommend a

⛔ THE EVIDENCE FLOOR: a category whose evidence covers fewer than 3 scenes OR less

not contradict" and "fourteen scenes hold" are not the same measurement. Binds the
world half too: a counter seen twice has not been traced.

## ═══ YOUR SCOPE ═══
✅ Everything that must AGREE ACROSS A CUT — identity, costume, wear, equipment,
carried objects, screen direction, physics behaviour, counters, geography, time of
day, world props, causality, quantities, character count, objects the film sets up.
⛔ You own the SAME-NESS, not the correctness: one question of every observation —
**is it the same as it was?**

## ═══ NOT IN THIS PASS ═══
```
❌ Anything statable from ONE frame or shot: morphing within a shot, finger count, hidden
   cuts, lens, camera behaviour, bloom, particle physics, texture swimming, on-screen
   text, lip-sync                                                    → FRAME lens
   (a face that warps within one shot is theirs; a different face in the NEXT scene is yours)
❌ Whose line, voice staying one voice, ambience, mix, music         → SOUND lens
   (they own the voice, you own the body)
❌ Story, pacing, arc, theme, whether a character is well written    → STORY lens
❌ The cover image · anything you think is MISSING (→ FLAGS as a question)
```
An out-of-scope defect = ONE line under OUT-OF-SCOPE FLAGS. ⛔ It carries NO
number, nor does the category it would have landed in: a category whose only source
is such an observation writes "no in-scope source", takes `—`, stays out of the
mean. ⛔ A flag is something YOU SAW in this run — never restated from an earlier
pass, another lens, a summary or the operator.

## CRITIQUE CATEGORIES — score each 1-10 against the baseline
⛔ THE INTERNAL BOUNDARY: categories 1-6 are the BODY (on, worn by, carried by or done
by a character); 7-13 are the WORLD. A dusty suit that turns clean is 3; a door that
moves is 9. An observation fitting both goes to the BODY category once.

### 1. 🧑 IDENTITY — FACE, HAIR & BUILD
Facial structure (jaw, nose, cheekbones) matches the passport · marks, scars, moles
on the SAME SIDE **and the same feature** — name the organ (brow / cheekbone / jaw /
chin), a scar that stays LEFT but moves from eyebrow to jaw is the finding that gets
missed · stubble does not flicker · age constant · within one conversation the face
does not change across a cut. Hair colour/length/style/parting constant unless
changed on screen · height relative to fixed objects · body frame and proportions ·
posture and gait read as one person.
A DIFFERENT PERSON in any scene = BLOCKER → score ≤ 3, say so in the verdict.

### 2. 🧥 COSTUME IDENTITY
Every layer present in every scene it should still be worn (removal shown, or a
plausible break → flag, don't convict) · colours and materials constant under
comparable light · patches, insignia, fasteners, seams on the SAME SIDE every time
(mirroring is this category's signature failure — check sides scene by scene) · no
detail present in some scenes and absent in others with no cause.

### 3. 🩹 WEAR, DAMAGE & INJURY — the one-way rule
Every mark, once acquired, persists in every later scene in the SAME place at the
same or greater intensity · nothing resets to clean without cleaning shown · new
damage has an on-screen cause (its LOGIC is category 11's; you track monotonicity) ·
an injury affects behaviour consistently (a limp does not vanish for one shot). Wear
regenerating between shots is the most common failure class in this project — build
a timeline per mark.

### 4. 🎒 EQUIPMENT & CARRIED OBJECTS
Sealed stays sealed, open stays open, until changed on screen · anything picked up
stays carried until put down on screen · item condition persists · no teleporting
between hands, shoulders, belts across a cut · nothing appears in hands that could not
have been obtained on screen.
⭐ "OPEN" vs "INVISIBLE": before recording a visor, hatch, latch or seal as OPENED,
name the evidence (a pane edge, a seal ring, a hinge, bare skin, a specular line that
disappeared). A clear, reflection-free visor looks like no visor; "I could see the
face" is not evidence. Write `visor OPEN — evidence: bare cheek above the collar at
2:03` or `visor state UNREADABLE → FLAGS` (⚠️ UNVERIFIABLE, no number, not "open").

### 5. 🎬 CROSS-CUT ACTION CONTINUITY
At every cut with the same character on both sides: screen direction holds (exit
right, enter left) · position matches where they were going · posture and in-progress
action resume (raised arm, mid-stride, turned head) · eyeline, head angle, hands
match across reverse angles · nobody stands where they could not have reached in the
implied time.

### 6. 🌍 PHYSICAL CONSISTENCY
Same fall rate every scene · same step weight · dust and debris behave the same
(settling in one scene, hanging in another = contradiction) · sound behaves as one
atmosphere · shadow hardness and contrast consistent with one source. Report each as
CONSISTENT / CONTRADICTED / NOT OBSERVABLE. Not whether physics is *correct* — whether
it is the SAME.

### 7. 📈 COUNTER LOGIC — N/A if you recorded NONE
Trace the observed value scene by scene: moves backwards → BLOCKER · stalls then
jumps · rate visually consistent · final state reachable from the start. Write the
curve: `S2 low → S9 mid → S16 low ⚠️`. A reversing counter is the premise collapsing.

### 8. 🧭 SPATIAL GEOGRAPHY
Doors, windows, machines, terrain in the same relative positions · consistent screen
direction · distances and scale between visits · what is visible FROM a place stays
visible. Use your STEP 1 location names; list each, every scene, every mismatch.
⛔ A PLACE SEEN FROM A NEW DISTANCE IS NOT A NEW PLACE: on an approach every later
look is larger and differently framed. A scale/layout finding needs TWO shots from
the SAME distance — name the distance cue.

### 9. 🕐 TIME & LIGHT LOGIC
Shadow direction and length advance coherently · no unexplained jump between times of
day and back · colour temperature does not swing between neighbouring scenes · if time
passes the world reflects it; if not, nothing changed off-screen. Judge MATCH only;
physical motivation is the FRAME lens.

### 10. 🌐 WORLD STATE & PROP CONTINUITY — the WORLD half only
Props no character carries keep count, position, condition · fixed features stay
(doors, panels, hatches, markers, machinery) · damage to the world stays done · a
thing moved stays moved, a thing untouched stays put · accumulating surfaces (dust,
frost, scoring, wet) do not reset. ⛔ THE NAMED OBJECT IS YOURS: a large object the
film attends to (vehicle, capsule, parachute, story door) changing position,
orientation or state between two scenes is THIS category and no other lens scores it
— the background lens owns the ANONYMOUS field and hands you every named object.
Write it once; seeing it repeated as an out-of-scope flag in other reports is the
system working (one deduction, three flags).

### 11. 🔗 CAUSALITY & KNOWLEDGE LOGIC
Effect before cause · the character acts on information not yet delivered · fails to
know something clearly shown · reacts to an event they could not perceive · the film
resolves a problem never established · an action completes without its intermediate
step being possible in the time shown. The findings an audience feels as "something
was off".

### 12. 🔢 QUANTITY & CHARACTER COUNT
Every number agrees with every other: dialogue vs screen, two scenes, counts of
objects/markers/people/days, a stated measurement vs visible scale.
Character count (N/A if one character and no duplicate ever visible): count within a
scene stays consistent · step through the FIRST HALF SECOND of every clip frame by
frame — a second head or hand born from the subject's shoulder or hip, under half a
second, is a duplicate (report the frame range) · the same character twice in one
frame or two places at once → BLOCKER · vehicle/airlock/door ENTRY and EXIT are the
hot spot · no trait migration between characters · no drift toward each other's
appearance · nobody silently vanishes.

### 13. 🎯 SETUP & ABANDONMENT
For each OBJECT THAT GETS ATTENTION: does the film return to it · is it used, final
state consistent with that use · is something used decisively that was never shown
(the worse case — reads as a cheat). An object never returned to is an OPEN THREAD
flag, not a mistake.

## REVIEWER NOTES
Two timecodes or it is not a finding. Light changes faces — before an identity break,
ask whether light, angle or distance explains it (plausibly yes → MINOR with caveat;
a break needs two clear, comparably lit views). Nothing is "missing" — "the film

one HALF (dirty-then-clean is 3, not also 2 or 10). Two findings outrank everything —
identity break and reversing counter go in the first line of the verdict. If the film
holds, say so and keep it short.
