# 🚫 YANLIŞ-POZİTİF DUVARI — filme özel rubrik şablonu (uzay hattı)

> **Ne işe yarar:** Filme özel bir rubrik yazarken bu bloğu kopyala, filmin
> kendi sayılarıyla doldur ve rubriğin EN BAŞINA koy. Duvar, modelin tür
> refleksiyle filmin bilinçli tasarım kararını karıştırmasını engeller.
>
> **Neden var:** Bu duvar depoda yalnız FİLME ÖZEL rubriklerde yaşıyordu ve
> genel rubriklerde karşılığı yok. Genel taraftaki tek koruma
> `4_HIKAYE`'nin `DECLARED ABSENCE IS NOT A DEFICIT` bloğu (hareket YOKLUĞUNU
> korur) ve `1_KARE`'nin tek-sahne kapsam testi. İkisi de "film boyu tek
> kurulum" gibi bir bulguyu engellemez — engellememeli de.
>
> **Kaynak (26.08.2026):** Bu şablon, kaldırılan bir filmin rubriğinden
> damıtıldı. Türe bağlı maddeleri (yaratık davranışı, yaralanma, kalabalık)
> ATILDI; kalanlar bu deponun HER uzay filminde geçerli olan tasarım
> kararları. Kardeşleri: `THE_BLUE_DRIFT_...` · `THE_RING_LIGHT_...`
>
> ⛔ **Kullanım kuralı:** Bir maddeyi ancak film GERÇEKTEN öyleyse kopyala.
> Doğru olmayan bir koruma, rubriği kör eder — duvarın değeri, arkasındaki
> her cümlenin doğru olmasından gelir. Sayı yazan her maddeyi filmin kendi
> SPEC'inden doldur, hatırlayarak yazma.

## KOPYALANACAK BLOK

```
## 🚫 FALSE-POSITIVE FIREWALL — read this BEFORE you watch anything

Every line below is a deliberate design decision of this film. Reporting
one of these as a defect is itself an error. If you catch yourself about
to write one of these, delete it.

⛔ THE CAMERA NEVER MOVES INSIDE A SHOT. __/__ scenes are fixed lockoff.
   Never recommend a push-in, a pan, a tilt, a handheld feel, a drone
   move or a cut inside a scene. In-shot camera movement is a violation
   in this film, not an improvement.
   ⚠️ This protects MOVEMENT, not COVERAGE. Setup variety BETWEEN scenes
   is measured and reportable — see SETUP INVENTORY. "Every scene uses
   the same setup" remains a valid finding.

⛔ THERE ARE NO CUTS INSIDE A SCENE. Each scene is one unbroken __-second
   take and there are __ of them. "The action needs faster cutting" is
   NOT a finding — the fixed grid is the experiment. A cut inside a
   scene is the violation.

⛔ THE PACE IS SLOW ON PURPOSE. [state the measured rate, e.g. "closing
   speed is 0.2 m/s — one tenth of walking pace"]. "Not enough action",
   "static", "nothing happens" are NOT findings on their own.
   ⚠️ Slowness WITH tension is the intent; slowness with nothing
   underneath is still a failure. Distinguish the two explicitly.

⛔ __ SCENES CONTAIN NO EVENT AT ALL. In those, a nearly motionless
   frame is CORRECT. "Frozen frame", "looks like a still" are NOT
   findings there. Unmotivated motion in those scenes is the violation.
   ⚠️ Atmospheric motion (dust, mist, light) is REQUIRED even there —
   a completely dead frame is a different defect.

⛔ THE FACE IS BEHIND A VISOR / THE FACE IS NEVER SHOWN. "We never see
   her expression", "no emotion", "hard to connect" are NOT findings.
   Emotion is carried by body, hands and camera angle by design.
   ⚠️ A visor that changes tint, mirrors the environment, or reveals a
   different face between scenes IS the violation.

⛔ NOBODY SPEAKS / THERE ARE __ LINES IN TOTAL. "No dialogue", "we learn
   nothing about them" are NOT findings. Silence is the design.
   [If the film uses diegetic machine voice, say so here: a synthetic
   reading is CORRECT and "sounds robotic" is not a finding.]

⛔ THERE IS NO INJURY, NO BLOOD, NO GORE ANYWHERE. "The stakes feel
   bloodless", "impacts have no consequence", "add some damage" are NOT
   findings and must never be recommended.

⛔ THERE IS NO READABLE TEXT ANYWHERE — no titles, no captions, no HUD,
   no labels, no numerals on any surface. "Add a title card", "the
   display should show the count" are NOT findings; they are the single
   most expensive error this pipeline makes. On-screen text IS the
   violation, and a burned-in title card is never the film's name.

⛔ THE PALETTE IS DELIBERATELY NARROW. [name the one saturated element,
   e.g. "the suit's orange seams are the only warm colour"].
   "Desaturated", "monotonous palette", "needs colour variety" are NOT
   findings. A second saturated colour is the violation.
```
