>>> EXCEPTION 1 - THE LICENSED DUPLICATE (S84 and S90) <<<

    In S84 and S90 a second figure appears that is the woman's exact
    duplicate: the same face, the same bone structure, the same
    scar through the same eyebrow, the same low bun, the same suit
    with the same panels, the same height. THIS DUPLICATION IS
    INTENDED AND IS THE INFORMATION OF THE PHASE. Never report it
    as a duplicate-figure error, a cloning artefact, a face-swap or
    a continuity break.
    The duplicate is separated from her by ONE thing, absolutely:
    MATERIAL. It is rendered ENTIRELY in matte bone-grey stone -
    every form present, every colour absent. No skin tone, no
    orange panel, no brown hair, no colour of any kind anywhere on
    it, and it never glows, shimmers or sheds dust.
    TWO details of the copy are load-bearing:
    - its VISOR is copied as CLEAR GLASS, not as stone. The glass is
      the one part of it that carries no colour because glass has
      none. You can see its grey face through it.
    - its RIGHT HAND IS BARE, exactly like hers: the glove was on
      the floor at the moment the plate read her, so the glove was
      not copied. A gloved right hand on the copy is a finding.
    WHAT FAILS HERE: a THIRD figure or any further copy; ANY
    difference between the two faces (bone structure, features,
    scar position, age, expression asymmetry beyond posture); any
    colour anywhere on the grey figure; a stone visor; a gloved
    right hand on the copy; the grey figure glowing, shimmering,
    crumbling or shedding dust; the two figures morphing into or
    toward each other.

>>> EXCEPTION 2 - THE VISOR OPENS AND SEALS (S85, then S86) <<<

    The sealed figure's visor is an opaque dark grey-green plate
    everywhere in this film. It becomes transparent exactly ONCE,
    in S85, and it seals again in the first two seconds of S86.
    - S85: at 0s the visor is fully opaque. From ~2s the tint
      DRAINS: one even, continuous change across the WHOLE glass at
      once, slow as a tide going out. By ~5s the glass is clear and
      the face behind it reads plainly. It never re-tints inside
      S85.
    - S86: at 0s the tint RETURNS - one even, continuous change
      across the whole visor, the reverse of how it left. By ~2s the
      glass is fully opaque and it STAYS opaque for every frame
      after. The face is gone and does not come back.
    - In every other scene of this phase where the sealed figure
      appears (S90), the visor is fully opaque throughout.
    WHAT FAILS HERE: the change happening in patches, as a wipe, as
    a moving edge, or with any flicker or stutter; the change
    reversing inside a scene; the visor going clear in any scene
    other than S85; the visor still clear after ~2s in S86; a face,
    eye, silhouette or glow appearing behind the glass once it is
    sealed; the glass fogging or throwing a glare that hides the
    face while it is meant to be clear.

>>> EXCEPTION 3 - THE ONE CONTROLLED GROWTH (S83) <<<
