## THE THREE THAT MATTER

1. **The Wrist Device Shift (0:01 vs 1:36)**
   *Why:* The emotional climax relies on her reading the final message from her wrist. If the viewer remembers it was a cloth patch in the first shot, the emotional payoff feels like a cheat.
   *Fix:* Regenerate or crop the 0:00-0:09 shot to hide the left wrist, or accept it as a continuity error.

2. **The Magic Moons (0:10 vs 1:27)**
   *Why:* The sudden appearance of massive celestial bodies makes it feel like she teleported to a different solar system mid-exploration.
   *Fix:* Cut scene 10 (1:27-1:33) as it does not advance the narrative and introduces the contradiction.

3. **Takeoff Zone Shift (0:14 vs 2:04)**
   *Why:* The geography changes entirely. She landed in a linear parking lot and took off from a radial crop circle.
   *Fix:* Acceptable in a montage edit, but could be fixed by assuming the ship relocated off-screen.
