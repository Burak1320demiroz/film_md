## REVIEWER NOTES

The core concept—a covert rescue mission that evolves into an opportunistic prison revolt—is strong and delivers a satisfying character turn at 3:27. However, the film suffers from severe structural padding in its first half: spending nearly a full minute on docking arms and hydraulic clamps completely drains tension before the protagonist even steps onto the station. Compress the mechanical docking, introduce Elena's situation earlier on the cockpit screens, and give the final breakout frame room to breathe so the ending feels earned rather than truncated.
