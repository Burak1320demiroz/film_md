## THE THREE THAT MATTER

1. **Jupiter Orientation & Topology Drift Across Cockpit Views (00:02 vs 00:12 vs 00:32)**
   * *What*: The background planet Jupiter cloud bands rotate violently from diagonal (25°) to horizontal (0°) to near-vertical (60°) between adjacent cuts looking out cockpit windows.
   * *Why*: AI generation treated each window background as an independent prompt without a unified 3D skybox anchor.
   * *Cheapest fix*: Re-render background plates for S2 and S4 or replace exterior window backgrounds with a fixed 3D Jupiter matte plate in composite.

2. **2D Single-Plate Parallax Collapse on Capital Ship Orbit (00:20–00:29)**
   * *What*: The foreground starship and background planet Jupiter move as a single frozen 2D plate with zero parallax separation during camera movement.
   * *Why*: Diffusion model generated camera motion by panning/warping a flat 2D image.
   * *Cheapest fix*: Separate the starship and Jupiter into distinct 2.5D layers, keyframing relative motion in post/VFX.

3. **Unannounced Cabin Crew Count & Character Disparity (01:00 vs 02:00 vs 02:30)**
   * *What*: Cabin crew headcount drops from 5 (S1/S7) to 3 (S13), and an alien crew member suddenly replaces a human occupant in S16.
   * *Why*: Inconsistent seed/prompt conditioning across interior cabin generation passes.
   * *Cheapest fix*: Re-cut or tightly crop S13 and S16 to obscure background seats, or re-generate cabin interiors with fixed character references.
