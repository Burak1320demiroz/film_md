2. **Decompression Momentum Deficit (02:20):** A 70 kPa hull rupture vents into vacuum, but the detached outer fragments drift away at a leisurely ~0.5 m/s rather than accelerating rapidly under explosive pressure discharge. *Fix:* Trim the initial 1.5 seconds of panel failure or speed-ramp the fragment dispersal.
3. **Corridor Walking Gait in Zero-G (01:30):** The astronaut demonstrates distinct footfall contacts on floor mats during corridor traversal without holding handrails or wearing magnetic boot locks. *Fix:* Cut around lower-body framing or regenerate shot with continuous floating posture.
