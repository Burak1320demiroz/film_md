## REVIEWER NOTES

This film possesses striking world-building aesthetics and strong tactical textures—specifically the tactile weight of manual levers, airlocks, and retro-industrial machinery contrasted against laser grids and gravitational black holes.

However, as a short film, it suffers from severe structural dispersion. Rather than following one character with a clear objective and a single core hazard, the material functions as a montage of disconnected sci-fi action beats. The protagonist's identity shifts constantly between figures in white and blue suits, and physical mechanics (pulling levers and pressing buttons) are repeated across seven separate shots without escalating the narrative stakes.

To transform this into a compelling narrative short, streamline the story into a single continuous survival thread: a lone astronaut disabling a facility drone, reaching the launch bay, and taking off into the laser grid before an interceptor forces a decision near the black hole. Cut the extraneous console manipulations and context-breaking crashes to keep the viewer anchored in the character's core predicament.
