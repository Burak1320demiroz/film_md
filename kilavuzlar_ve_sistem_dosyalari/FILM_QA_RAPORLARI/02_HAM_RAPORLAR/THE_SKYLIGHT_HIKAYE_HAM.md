THE THREE THINGS THAT MOST HURT THIS FILM
0:40 (S4) through 2:50 (S17) — Rhythm and Redundancy: The film loops through endless variations of "astronaut stands/kneels in a light shaft." It pads the runtime without advancing the story.

3:40 (S22) — Absence of Character Interiority: The protagonist never faces a choice, an obstacle, or a moment of reflection that requires human emotion. A drone could have completed this mission.

5:00 (S30) — Flat Ending: Escaping the planet carries no weight because the planet was never dangerous, and staying was never a temptation.

THE THREE THINGS WORTH PROTECTING
1:10 (S7) — The Cairn Building: The rock stack gives the character a tangible, physical goal. It is the only action that anchors the wandering.

0:00 (S0) — The Scale: The opening crater shot establishes a massive, imposing world beautifully.

3:20 (S20) — The Artifact: Examining the square device introduces a flicker of mystery, even if abandoned.
