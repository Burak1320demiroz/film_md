## REVIEWER NOTES

The short succeeds in delivering a clean, classic sci-fi survival narrative with high visual clarity and strong technical pacing. However, its dramatic tension is flattened by uniform scene structure: nearly every scene is cut to an exact ~10-second duration, turning the middle act into an itemized checklist of station breakdowns rather than an escalating snowball of failure. Trimming redundant maintenance procedures (S6, S7) and eliminating line narration will significantly tighten viewer retention while elevating the emotional payoff of the final orbital reach.
