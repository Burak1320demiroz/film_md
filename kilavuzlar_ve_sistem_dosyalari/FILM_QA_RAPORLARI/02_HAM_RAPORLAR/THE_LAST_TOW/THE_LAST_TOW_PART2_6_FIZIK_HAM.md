## THE THREE THAT MATTER

1. **Horizontal Levitation / Cantilevered Carry (02:10–02:50, Shots 14–17)**: The astronaut holds a full-sized suited human extended straight out horizontally with arms alone, showing zero torso tilt, spine torque, or gravitational deflection.
   * *Why*: Violates mass rotational equilibrium ($T = r \times F$) and human muscle biomechanics; a 90 kg load at a 1 m moment arm generates ~900 N·m of uncompensated torque.
   * *Cheapest Fix*: Trim shots 16 and 17 completely (02:30–02:50), re-cutting from hatch emergence directly to tool assembly at 02:50.

2. **Billowing Dust Cloud in Vacuum (01:00–01:10, Shot 7)**: Fine particulates billow upward in a soft atmospheric plume when opening the exterior hatch.
   * *Why*: In a hard vacuum without atmospheric gas drag, ejected dust grains follow strict parabolic ballistic trajectories rather than floating clouds.
   * *Cheapest Fix*: Trim 1.5 seconds off Shot 7 during initial dust release or frame around internal pressure venting.

3. **Massless Impact during Sledgehammering (01:30–02:05, Shots 10–13)**: Heavy hammer impacts against metal floor plates yield zero handle recoil or kinetic reaction in the gloves.
   * *Why*: Newton's third law requires conservation of momentum and mechanical shock reflection back into the operator's arms upon impact.
   * *Cheapest Fix*: Trim 3–5 frames at the exact point of impact to hide the rigid post-strike pose.
