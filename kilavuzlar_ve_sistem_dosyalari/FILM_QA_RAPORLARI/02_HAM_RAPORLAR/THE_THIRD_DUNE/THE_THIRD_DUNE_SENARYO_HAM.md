## THE THREE THINGS THAT MOST HURT THIS FILM

1. 0:00-0:40 — Delayed Start — We watch 40 seconds of slow walking and wind
   measurements before the actual threat (the storm and the shifting ground) is
   established.
2. 0:50 — Narrating the visible — The line "Kum taşınıyor" (The sand is moving)
   tells the viewer exactly what the image of sand pouring through his fingers
   already shows.
3. 2:40 — Character Interiority — The characters act like completely unaffected
   robots while abandoning their entire mission. Without seeing the psychological
   weight of this loss, the emotional stakes are capped.

## THE THREE THINGS WORTH PROTECTING

1. 3:10 — The Cockpit Choice — "Kütle mi, süre mi? İkisi birden yok." This is a
   brilliant, punchy line that perfectly articulates the film's central dilemma.
2. 1:20 — The Anomaly — "Haritada iki sırt vardı. Üç tane sayıyorum." This line
   introduces a psychological, almost supernatural dread to an otherwise standard
   environmental threat.
3. 3:30 — The Final Image — The visual of the perfectly lined-up cargo boxes left
   behind in the sand is a beautiful payoff to the theme of sacrifice.

## THE ONE THING NOTHING ELSE DOES

Using a rapidly, almost impossibly shifting geological landscape (a dune
multiplying overnight) as a ticking clock, rather than just relying on a standard
approaching weather event.
