## THE THREE THINGS THAT MOST HURT THIS FILM

1. **Lack of Narrative Drive:** The film is a series of maintenance chores without a clear goal, obstacle, or consequence.
2. **Absence of Character Interiority:** The astronauts are blank slates; we don't know what they want, so we can't care if they get it.
3. **No Escalation or Climax:** The story flatlines; the tasks don't get harder, the stakes don't rise, and it ends without a resolution.

## THE THREE THINGS WORTH PROTECTING

1. The stark, desolate atmosphere is well-established early on (S4, S5).
2. The initial sense of procedural realism in the tasks (S1, S2).
3. The quiet, contemplative tone, though it needs to be paired with underlying tension.
