## REVIEWER NOTES

However, the structure breaks down in Act 3: the rigid 10-second shot grid undermines natural pacing, an unearned third character suddenly spawns at 3:10, and the story stops abruptly with a minute of aimless walking rather than a earned payoff. Cutting the redundant walking beats and converting the final canyon obstacle into an active tether-based decision will elevate this short into a focused, highly compelling survival piece.
