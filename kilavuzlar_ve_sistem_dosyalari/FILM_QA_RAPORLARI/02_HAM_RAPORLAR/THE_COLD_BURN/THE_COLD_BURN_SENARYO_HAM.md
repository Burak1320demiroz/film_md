THE THREE THINGS THAT MOST HURT THIS FILM
1:40 (S7) — The station explosion — It resets the narrative entirely. The film spends its first half building an ice-storm escape, then abandons that premise for a completely unrelated space-debris disaster.
0:10-0:20 (S2) — The sample collection — The characters retrieve a blue sample, but the film never assigns it value. Without knowing what it is, we don't care if they drop it.
3:30 (S11) — The escape jump — The characters make no hard choices to survive; they just fly away. The lack of a Central Choice strips the protagonist of agency.

THE THREE THINGS WORTH PROTECTING

2:20 (S8) — The docking sequence — Visually establishes the vulnerability of the ship against the massive, ruined structure.

THE ONE THING NOTHING ELSE DOES
